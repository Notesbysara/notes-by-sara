Interest Coverage Ratio

Beginner

Also known as: times interest earned, TIE, credit rating ratio, debt service cover

Ability to pay interest from operating profit. Use it to judge whether a company can pay the interest on its debt. Credit rating agencies watch it closely. A ratio below about 1.5 to 2 is usually a warning sign.

The formula

Interest coverage=EBITInterest expense\text{Interest coverage}=\frac{EBIT}{\text{Interest expense}}

Interest coverage = EBIT / Interest expense

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
EBITEarnings before interest and taxmoney
Interest expenseInterest the company owes for the periodmoney

Worked example

A company has EBIT of 20,000 and interest expense of 8,000. What is its interest coverage ratio?

  1. Coverage = EBIT / interest20000 / 8000= 2.50

The interest coverage ratio is 2.50. Earnings cover interest 2.50 times.
