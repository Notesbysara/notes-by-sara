Debt-to-Income Ratios and Affordable Housing Payment

Beginner

Also known as: DTI, front end ratio, back end ratio, mortgage affordability

How lenders will view your debt load and the most they would allow. Use it to see how lenders judge affordability. The 28% and 36% limits are a traditional guideline; lenders vary and some allow higher. The result is the most you could be approved for, not what is comfortable, so budget below it. Convert the maximum payment to a loan size using the loan payment formula.

The formula

Front-end=HY,Back-end=H+DY,Hmax=min⁡(0.28 Y,  0.36 Y−D)\text{Front-end}=\frac{H}{Y},\quad \text{Back-end}=\frac{H+D}{Y},\quad H_{max}=\min(0.28\,Y,\;0.36\,Y-D)

Front-end ratio = housing payment / gross monthly income; Back-end ratio = (housing payment + other debt payments) / gross monthly income; Maximum housing payment = the smaller of 28% of income and 36% of income - other debt payments

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
HMonthly housing payment (loan, property tax, insurance)money
DOther monthly debt payments: cars, student loans, card minimumsmoney
YGross monthly income (before tax)money

Worked example

Gross income is 7,000 a month, the planned housing payment is 1,800 and other debt payments are 500.

  1. Front-end ratio = housing / income1800 / 7000= 25.7%
  2. Back-end ratio = (housing + debts) / income(1800+500) / 7000= 32.9%
  3. Maximum housing payment = min(28% of income, 36% of income - debts)min(0.28 x 7000, 0.36 x 7000-500)= 1,960.00
  4. Room left under the maximum (negative means over)1960-1800= 160.00
  5. Within the guideline? (1 = yes, 0 = no)1 if 160>=0 else 0= 1

The front-end ratio is 25.7% and the back-end ratio is 32.9%. The highest housing payment under the 28/36 guideline is 1,960.00, so the room left under it is 160.00 (a negative number means over the guideline).
