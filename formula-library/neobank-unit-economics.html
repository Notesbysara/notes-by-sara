Neobank Unit Economics: Margin per Customer and Payback

IntermediateFintech & Digital FinanceAlso known as: neobanks, digital bank profitability, ARPU, cost to serve, customer lifetime
← Narrative Contagion (SIR Model)Net Advantage to Leasing (Lease vs. Buy) →

Finds: whether a digital bank earns enough per customer.

When to use it: Use it to judge whether an app-based bank can make money. Many customers hold small balances, so income per customer is low and many neobanks need years of scale to cover costs. Compare the margin per customer with the cost of acquiring one and the time a customer stays. Fraud losses and a lack of a main-bank relationship lower income.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Margin\displaystyle \text{Margin}=ARPUfee+ARPUNII−C\displaystyle =ARPU_{fee}+ARPU_{NII}-CPayback\displaystyle \text{Payback}=CACMargin/12\displaystyle =\frac{CAC}{\text{Margin}/12}LTV\displaystyle LTV=Marginchurn\displaystyle =\frac{\text{Margin}}{churn}

In words: Margin per customer per year = fee and interchange income + net interest income − cost to serve; Payback in months = acquisition cost / (monthly margin); Lifetime value = yearly margin / yearly churn; Breakeven customers = fixed cost / margin per customer

What each symbol means

ARPUYearly revenue per customer from fees and interchange, and from net interest on deposits and loansmoney
CYearly cost to serve a customer (support, fraud losses, cloud, cards)money
CACCost to win a new customermoney
churnShare of customers leaving each yearpercent

Worked example

A neobank earns 45 a year per customer from fees and interchange and 38 from net interest, with 52 cost to serve, 35 to acquire a customer, and 18% yearly churn. Fixed costs are 20m.

Margin per customer per year45 + 38 − 5231
Payback in months35 ÷ (31 ÷ 12)13.5
Lifetime value31 ÷ 0.18172.22
LTV / CAC172.222 ÷ 354.92
Customers needed to cover fixed costs20,000,000 ÷ 31645,161
The answer

Margin per customer is 31 a year. Payback is 13.5 months and lifetime value is 172.22, which is 4.92 times the acquisition cost. To cover 20m of fixed costs the bank needs 645,161 customers.
