Optimal Risky Portfolio (Maximum Sharpe, Two Assets)

IntermediateQuantitative FinanceAlso known as: optimization methods, tangency portfolio, maximum Sharpe ratio, mean variance optimization, Lagrange
← Operational Risk: Basic Indicator Capital and Expected LossOption Greeks: Delta, Gamma, Vega, Theta, Rho →

Finds: the risky mix with the best return per unit of risk.

When to use it: Use it to find the mix of two risky assets with the highest Sharpe ratio. It comes from an optimization: maximize the Sharpe ratio subject to weights adding to 1, and set the derivative to zero. For more than two assets the same solution is found with matrix algebra or numerical optimizers.

Worked example

The answer

The best mix is 53.23% in asset 1 and 46.77% in asset 2. It has an expected return of 8.13% and a volatility of 13.44%, giving the highest possible Sharpe ratio of 0.382.
