Present Value of an Ordinary Annuity

Intermediate

Also known as: present value of annuity, PV annuity, value of regular payments

Value today of regular payments. Use it to value a stream of equal payments received at the end of each period, such as a pension payout or the payments on a loan.

The formula

PV=PMT×1−(1+r)−nrPV = PMT \times \frac{1-(1+r)^{-n}}{r}

PV = PMT x (1 - (1 + r)^-n) / r

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
PVPresent value of all the paymentsmoney
PMTPayment received at the end of every periodmoney
rDiscount rate per period, as a decimalper period
nNumber of paymentsperiods

Worked example

A plan pays 1,000 at the end of each year for 10 years. With a discount rate of 5%, what is the plan worth today?

  1. Annuity factor = (1 - (1 + r)^-n) / r(1-(1+0.05)^(-10)) / 0.05= 7.7217
  2. PV = PMT x annuity factor1000 x 7.72173= 7,721.73

The 10 payments are worth 7,721.73 today.
