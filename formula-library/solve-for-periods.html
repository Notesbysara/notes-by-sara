Number of Periods to Reach a Target

Intermediate

Also known as: how long to reach a goal, solve for n, time to grow, years to reach target

Time needed to reach a target. Use it when you know the starting amount, the target and the rate, and want to know how long it will take.

The formula

n=ln⁡(FV/PV)ln⁡(1+r)n = \frac{\ln(FV/PV)}{\ln(1+r)}

n = ln(FV / PV) / ln(1 + r)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
nNumber of periods neededperiods
FVTarget amountmoney
PVAmount you start withmoney
rInterest rate per period, as a decimalper period
lnNatural logarithmfunction

Worked example

You have 1,000 and want 2,000. At 8% a year, how many years will it take?

  1. Growth needed = FV / PV2000 / 1000= 2.0000
  2. n = ln(growth needed) / ln(1 + r)ln(2) / ln(1+0.08)= 9.01

It takes 9.01 years.
