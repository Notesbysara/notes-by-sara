Futures Daily Settlement and Margin Account

Intermediate

Also known as: marking to market, variation margin, initial margin, maintenance margin, margin call futures

A futures margin account's balance and any margin call. Use it to follow a futures position through daily settlement. Gains and losses are paid each day, unlike a forward. When the balance falls below the maintenance level, the holder must deposit enough to return to the initial margin.

The formula

Daily gain=N×M×(Ft−Ft−1),Balancet=Balancet−1+Daily gain\text{Daily gain}=N\times M\times(F_{t}-F_{t-1}),\quad \text{Balance}_{t}=\text{Balance}_{t-1}+\text{Daily gain}

Daily gain on a long position = Contracts x Multiplier x (Today's futures price - Yesterday's futures price); Margin balance = Previous balance + Daily gain

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
NNumber of contracts held (long)number
MContract multiplier, the money value of one price pointmoney
F_tSettlement price of the futures on day tmoney
Initial marginDeposit required per contract to open the positionmoney
Maintenance marginLevel per contract below which a margin call is issuedmoney

Worked example

You buy 10 index futures contracts with a multiplier of 50. The initial margin is 6,000 per contract and the maintenance margin is 4,500. Settlement prices on four days are 4,000, 3,980, 3,950 and 3,920. What happens to the account?

  1. Daily gain = contracts x multiplier x price change= -10,000.00, -15,000.00, -15,000.00
  2. Margin balance after each day (no top-ups)= 50,000.00, 35,000.00, 20,000.00
  3. Maintenance level for the whole position4500 x 10= 45,000.00
  4. Lowest balancemin([50000, 35000, 20000])= 20,000.00
  5. Margin call to restore the initial margin (0 if none)max(6000 x 10-20000, 0) if 20000<45000 else 0= 40,000.00

The daily gains are -10,000.00, -15,000.00, -15,000.00. The balance after each day is 50,000.00, 35,000.00, 20,000.00. The maintenance level is 45,000.00 and the lowest balance is 20,000.00, so a margin call arrives and 40,000.00 must be deposited to return to the initial margin.
