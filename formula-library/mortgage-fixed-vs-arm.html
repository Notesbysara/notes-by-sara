Fixed vs Adjustable-Rate Mortgage

Intermediate

Also known as: mortgages, ARM reset, teaser rate, mortgage payment shock

What each mortgage costs, including a rate reset. Use it to compare a fixed rate with an adjustable rate that starts lower and resets. The adjustable loan wins if rates stay low or if you sell or refinance before the reset; it loses if the rate rises and you stay. Enter a stress rate for the reset, not the hoped-for one. This ignores rate caps and fees, which you should add from the loan terms.

The formula

PMT=iL1−(1+i)−n,Bk=L (1+i)n−(1+i)k(1+i)n−1PMT=\frac{iL}{1-(1+i)^{-n}},\quad B_{k}=L\,\frac{(1+i)^{n}-(1+i)^{k}}{(1+i)^{n}-1}

Payment = i x Loan / (1 - (1 + i)^(-n)); Balance after k payments = Loan x ((1 + i)^n - (1 + i)^k) / ((1 + i)^n - 1); after the rate resets, the new payment is the payment formula on the remaining balance over the remaining months

LLoan amountmoney

Worked example

A 300,000 loan over 30 years. A fixed loan costs 6.5%. An adjustable loan starts at 5.5% for 5 years and then resets to 8.0% for the remaining 25 years.

  1. Fixed payment300000 x (0.065 / 12) / (1-(1+0.065 / 12)^(-30 x 12))= 1,896.20
  2. Adjustable payment before reset300000 x (0.055 / 12) / (1-(1+0.055 / 12)^(-30 x 12))= 1,703.37
  3. Monthly saving before the reset1896.2-1703.37= 192.84
  4. Balance at the reset300000 x ((1+0.055 / 12)^(30 x 12)-(1+0.055 / 12)^(5 x 12)) / ((1+0.055 / 12)^(30 x 12)-1)= 277,381.81
  5. Payment after the reset (remaining term)277382 x (0.08 / 12) / (1-(1+0.08 / 12)^(-(30-5) x 12))= 2,140.88
  6. Reset payment minus the fixed payment2140.88-1896.2= 244.67
  7. Total paid, fixed1896.2 x 30 x 12= 682,633.47
  8. Total paid, adjustable1703.37 x 5 x 12+2140.88 x (30-5) x 12= 744,465.36
  9. Adjustable minus fixed over the full term744465-682633= 61,831.90

The fixed payment is 1,896.20. The adjustable loan starts at 1,703.37, saving 192.84 a month for 5 years. After the reset the payment jumps to 2,140.88, which is 244.67 more than the fixed payment. Over the full term the fixed loan costs 682,633.47 and the adjustable loan 744,465.36, so the adjustable loan costs 61,831.90 more in this scenario.
