Backtest Summary: Return, Risk, Drawdown and Information Ratio

Intermediate

Also known as: construct and backtest a diversified portfolio, backtest report, CAGR volatility Sharpe drawdown, tracking error

A full performance report for a tested strategy. Use it to report a backtest in full rather than just the return. A good result must beat a simple benchmark after costs, in data the rule was not tuned on. Two years of monthly data, as here, is too short to say much; use many years and different market conditions, and keep the test free of look-ahead.

The formula

CAGR=WT12/n−1,σ=s12,Sharpe=12(rˉ−rf)σ,IR=12 (r−b)‾TECAGR=W_{T}^{12/n}-1,\quad \sigma=s\sqrt{12},\quad Sharpe=\frac{12(\bar{r}-r_{f})}{\sigma},\quad IR=\frac{12\,\overline{(r-b)}}{TE}

Growth of 1 = product of (1 + monthly return); Annualized return (CAGR) = growth^(12 / months) - 1; Volatility = monthly standard deviation x square root of 12; Sharpe = 12 x (average monthly return - monthly risk-free rate) / volatility; Maximum drawdown = largest fall from a previous peak; Tracking error = standard deviation of monthly excess returns x square root of 12; Information ratio = annualized average excess return / tracking error

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
r, bMonthly return of the portfolio and of the benchmarkpercent
r_fMonthly risk-free ratepercent

Worked example

Twenty-four monthly returns of a portfolio and its benchmark are in the calculator; the risk-free rate is 0.3% a month.

  1. Growth path of 1 invested= 1.0000, 1.0210, 1.0077, 1.0259, 1.0330, 1.0083, 1.0395, 1.0520, 1.0436, 1.0634, 1.0910, 1.0747, 1.0844, 1.0995, 1.0764, 1.0947, 1.1188, 1.1121, 1.1243, 1.1333, 1.1118, 1.1418, 1.1567, 1.1520, 1.1705
  2. Final growth of 1= 1.170
  3. Annualized return= 8.19%
  4. Benchmark annualized return= 4.33%
  5. Annualized volatility= 5.67%
  6. Sharpe ratio= 0.79
  7. Maximum drawdown= 2.4%
  8. Tracking error= 0.38%
  9. Information ratio= 9.73

The portfolio grew 1.170 times, an annual return of 8.19% against 4.33% for the benchmark. Volatility is 5.67%, the Sharpe ratio 0.79, and the worst fall from a peak 2.4%. Tracking error is 0.38% and the information ratio 9.73.
