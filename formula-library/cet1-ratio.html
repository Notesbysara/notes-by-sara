CET1, Tier 1 and Leverage Ratios

Intermediate

Also known as: capital ratios, risk weighted assets, Basel III, capital buffers

Whether a bank's capital covers its risks. Use it to judge whether a bank has enough capital for the risk it takes. Regulators set minimums (under Basel III, 4.5% CET1 plus buffers, 7% with the conservation buffer) and a separate leverage ratio, which does not depend on risk weights. The headroom shows how much a bank can lose or lend before it hits a limit. Local requirements differ, so enter your own.

The formula

RWA=∑wiEi,CET1 ratio=CET1RWA,Leverage ratio=Tier 1Total exposureRWA=\sum w_{i}E_{i},\quad CET1\ ratio=\frac{CET1}{RWA},\quad \text{Leverage ratio}=\frac{\text{Tier 1}}{\text{Total exposure}}

Risk-weighted assets = sum of (risk weight x exposure); CET1 ratio = common equity tier 1 capital / RWA; Tier 1 ratio = (CET1 + additional tier 1) / RWA; Total capital ratio = (Tier 1 + Tier 2) / RWA; Leverage ratio = Tier 1 / total (unweighted) exposure

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
w_iRisk weight of exposure i: lower for safe assets such as sovereign bonds, higher for riskier loanspercent
CET1Highest-quality capital: common shares and retained earnings, after deductionsmoney
AT1, T2Additional Tier 1 and Tier 2 capital: lower-quality loss-absorbing instrumentsmoney

Worked example

Exposures are 3,000, 1,500, 2,500 and 1,000 with risk weights of 20%, 50%, 100% and 150%. CET1 capital is 520, additional Tier 1 is 60, Tier 2 is 80 and total assets are 8,000. The requirement is 7% CET1.

  1. Risk-weighted assets = sum of exposure x weight= 5,350.00
  2. RWA density = RWA / total exposure5350 / 8000= 66.9%
  3. CET1 ratio520 / 5350= 9.72%
  4. Tier 1 ratio(520+60) / 5350= 10.84%
  5. Total capital ratio(520+60+80) / 5350= 12.34%
  6. Spare CET1 above the requirement520-0.07 x 5350= 145.50
  7. Extra RWA the capital could support = CET1 / req - RWA520 / 0.07-5350= 2,078.57
  8. Leverage ratio = Tier 1 / exposure(520+60) / 8000= 7.25%

Risk-weighted assets are 5,350.00, only 66.9% of total assets. The CET1 ratio is 9.72%, the Tier 1 ratio 10.84% and the total capital ratio 12.34%. Against a 7% requirement there is 145.50 of spare CET1, which would support 2,078.57 more risk-weighted assets. The leverage ratio is 7.25%.
