CET1, Tier 1 and Leverage Ratios

IntermediateBanking & Financial InstitutionsAlso known as: capital ratios, risk weighted assets, Basel III, capital buffers
← Central Counterparty Initial Margin (Value-at-Risk Method)Classification Metrics: Precision, Recall and F1 →

Finds: whether a bank's capital covers its risks.

When to use it: Use it to judge whether a bank has enough capital for the risk it takes. Regulators set minimums (under Basel III, 4.5% CET1 plus buffers, 7% with the conservation buffer) and a separate leverage ratio, which does not depend on risk weights. The headroom shows how much a bank can lose or lend before it hits a limit. Local requirements differ, so enter your own.

The formula

RWA\displaystyle RWA=∑wiEi\displaystyle =\sum w_{i}E_{i}CET1 ratio\displaystyle CET1\ ratio=CET1RWA\displaystyle =\frac{CET1}{RWA}Leverage ratio\displaystyle \text{Leverage ratio}=Tier 1Total exposure\displaystyle =\frac{\text{Tier 1}}{\text{Total exposure}}

In words: Risk-weighted assets = sum of (risk weight x exposure); CET1 ratio = common equity tier 1 capital / RWA; Tier 1 ratio = (CET1 + additional tier 1) / RWA; Total capital ratio = (Tier 1 + Tier 2) / RWA; Leverage ratio = Tier 1 / total (unweighted) exposure

What each symbol means

w_iRisk weight of exposure i: lower for safe assets such as sovereign bonds, higher for riskier loanspercent
CET1Highest-quality capital: common shares and retained earnings, after deductionsmoney
AT1, T2Additional Tier 1 and Tier 2 capital: lower-quality loss-absorbing instrumentsmoney

Worked example

Exposures are 3,000, 1,500, 2,500 and 1,000 with risk weights of 20%, 50%, 100% and 150%. CET1 capital is 520, additional Tier 1 is 60, Tier 2 is 80 and total assets are 8,000. The requirement is 7% CET1.

Risk-weighted assets5,350
RWA density5,350 ÷ 8,00066.9%
CET1 ratio520 ÷ 5,3509.72%
Tier 1 ratio(520 + 60) ÷ 5,35010.84%
Total capital ratio(520 + 60 + 80) ÷ 5,35012.34%
Spare CET1 above the requirement520 − 0.07 × 5,350145.50
Extra RWA the capital could support520 ÷ 0.07 − 5,3502,078.57
Leverage ratio(520 + 60) ÷ 8,0007.25%
The answer

Risk-weighted assets are 5,350, only 66.9% of total assets. The CET1 ratio is 9.72%, the Tier 1 ratio 10.84% and the total capital ratio 12.34%. Against a 7% requirement there is 145.50 of spare CET1, which would support 2,078.57 more risk-weighted assets. The leverage ratio is 7.25%.
