Number of Payments to Repay a Loan

Intermediate

Also known as: how long to pay off a loan, time to repay, solve for n loan, nper

How long a loan takes to repay. Use it to find how long a loan takes to repay when you choose the payment, for example when you pay more than the minimum on a credit card.

The formula

n=−ln⁡ ⁣(1−P rPMT)ln⁡(1+r)n = \frac{-\ln\!\left(1-\dfrac{P\,r}{PMT}\right)}{\ln(1+r)}

n = -ln(1 - P x r / PMT) / ln(1 + r)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
nNumber of payments neededperiods
PLoan amountmoney
rInterest rate per payment period, as a decimalper period
PMTPayment you make each periodmoney
lnNatural logarithmfunction

Worked example

You owe 10,000 at 12% a year, charged monthly. You pay 200 a month. How many months until the debt is gone?

  1. Rate per period = annual rate / payments per year0.12 / 12= 1.0000%
  2. Interest in the first period = P x r10000 x 0.01= 100.00
  3. Ratio = 1 - interest / PMT1-100 / 200= 0.5000
  4. n = -ln(ratio) / ln(1 + r)-ln(0.5) / ln(1+0.01)= 69.66
  5. Years = n / payments per year69.6607 / 12= 5.81

It takes about 69.66 payments, which is 5.81 years.
