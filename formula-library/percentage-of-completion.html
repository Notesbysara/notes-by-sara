Revenue Recognized by Percentage of Completion

Intermediate

Also known as: revenue recognition formula, contract revenue, cost-to-cost method

Revenue to record on a long contract. Use it for long contracts such as construction, where revenue is recognized as the work progresses rather than all on completion.

The formula

Revenue to date=Contract price×Costs to dateEstimated total costs\text{Revenue to date}=\text{Contract price}\times\frac{\text{Costs to date}}{\text{Estimated total costs}}

Revenue to date = Contract price x (Costs to date / Estimated total costs)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
Contract priceTotal price agreed for the projectmoney
Costs to dateCosts incurred on the project so farmoney
Estimated total costsTotal costs expected for the whole projectmoney
Revenue to dateRevenue recognized from the start up to nowmoney

Worked example

A builder has a contract for 1,000,000 with an estimated total cost of 800,000. Costs to date are 300,000 and 150,000 of revenue was recognized in earlier periods. How much revenue is recognized this period?

  1. Percentage complete = costs to date / estimated total costs300000 / 800000= 37.50%
  2. Revenue to date = contract price x percentage complete1,000,000 x 0.375= 375,000.00
  3. Revenue this period = revenue to date - earlier revenue375000-150000= 225,000.00

The project is 37.50% complete, so revenue to date is 375,000.00 and the revenue recognized this period is 225,000.00.
