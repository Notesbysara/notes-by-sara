Green Bonds: Greenium Saving and Sustainability-Linked Step-Up Cost

IntermediateRegulation, Ethics & ESGAlso known as: green, social and sustainability-linked bonds, greenium, coupon step-up, use of proceeds bond, KPI-linked bond
← Grant Element of a Concessional LoanGreenwashing Check: Claimed vs Aligned Share →

Finds: what a green label saves and what a missed target costs.

When to use it: Use it to see what labeled bonds change. A green bond ties the use of the money to eligible projects and may price slightly tighter (the greenium, often a few basis points). A sustainability-linked bond has no use-of-proceeds rule but its coupon steps up if the issuer misses a target, which is the cost of failure. A small step-up may be too weak to change behavior.

The formula

Greenium\displaystyle \text{Greenium}=yconv−ygreen\displaystyle =y_{conv}-y_{green}Saving\displaystyle \text{Saving}=∑tN (yconv−ygreen)(1+yconv)t\displaystyle =\sum_{t}\frac{N\,(y_{conv}-y_{green})}{(1+y_{conv})^{t}}Step-up\displaystyle \text{Step-up}=N Δc m\displaystyle =N\,\Delta c\,m

In words: Greenium = yield on a similar ordinary bond − yield on the green bond; Present value of the saving to the issuer = sum over years of (notional x greenium) discounted at the ordinary yield; For a sustainability-linked bond, extra interest if the target is missed = notional x coupon step-up x remaining years after the test date

Worked example

A 500m 7-year green bond yields 4.15% while the issuer's ordinary bond yields 4.30%. A sustainability-linked bond of the same size steps up the coupon by 0.25 percentage points for the last 4 years if a target is missed.

Greenium in basis points0.0015 × 10,00015
Present value of the yearly saving500,000,000 × 0.0015 × (1 − (1 + 0.043)^(−7)) ÷ 0.0434,452,054.95
Extra interest if the target is missed500,000,000 × 0.0025 × 45,000,000

The answer

The greenium is 0.150% or 15.0 basis points. Over 7 years the issuer saves 4,452,054.95 in present value. If the sustainability target is missed, the step-up costs 5,000,000 in extra interest, or 1% of the notional.
