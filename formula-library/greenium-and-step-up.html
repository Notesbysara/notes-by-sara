Green Bonds: Greenium Saving and Sustainability-Linked Step-Up Cost

Intermediate

Also known as: green, social and sustainability-linked bonds, greenium, coupon step-up, use of proceeds bond, KPI-linked bond

What a green label saves and what a missed target costs. Use it to see what labeled bonds change. A green bond ties the use of the money to eligible projects and may price slightly tighter (the greenium, often a few basis points). A sustainability-linked bond has no use-of-proceeds rule but its coupon steps up if the issuer misses a target, which is the cost of failure. A small step-up may be too weak to change behavior.

The formula

Greenium=yconv−ygreen,Saving=∑tN (yconv−ygreen)(1+yconv)t,Step-up=N Δc m\text{Greenium}=y_{conv}-y_{green},\quad \text{Saving}=\sum_{t}\frac{N\,(y_{conv}-y_{green})}{(1+y_{conv})^{t}},\quad \text{Step-up}=N\,\Delta c\,m

Greenium = yield on a similar ordinary bond - yield on the green bond; Present value of the saving to the issuer = sum over years of (notional x greenium) discounted at the ordinary yield; For a sustainability-linked bond, extra interest if the target is missed = notional x coupon step-up x remaining years after the test date

Worked example

A 500m 7-year green bond yields 4.15% while the issuer's ordinary bond yields 4.30%. A sustainability-linked bond of the same size steps up the coupon by 0.25 percentage points for the last 4 years if a target is missed.

  1. Greenium0.043-0.0415= 0.150%
  2. Greenium in basis points0.0015 x 10000= 15.0
  3. Present value of the yearly saving500,000,000 x 0.0015 x (1-(1+0.043)^(-7)) / 0.043= 4,452,054.95
  4. Extra interest if the target is missed500,000,000 x 0.0025 x 4= 5,000,000.00
  5. Extra interest as a share of notional5,000,000 / 500,000,000= 1.00%

The greenium is 0.150% or 15.0 basis points. Over 7 years the issuer saves 4,452,054.95 in present value. If the sustainability target is missed, the step-up costs 5,000,000.00 in extra interest, or 1.00% of the notional.
