Confidence Interval for a Mean

Intermediate

Also known as: confidence interval, margin of error, 95% confidence interval

A likely range for the true average. Use it to give a range that is likely to contain the true average, based on a sample. It works best when the sample is reasonably large.

The formula

xˉ±z sn\bar{x} \pm z\,\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}

mean +/- z x s / sqrt(n)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
x-barSample meanpercent
zCritical value for the confidence level (1.96 for 95%)number
sSample standard deviationpercent
nSample sizenumber

Worked example

Over 36 months a fund had a mean return of 8% and a standard deviation of 12%. What is the 95% confidence interval for its average return?

  1. Standard error = s / square root of n0.12 / sqrt(36)= 2.00%
  2. Margin of error = z x standard error1.96 x 0.02= 3.92%
  3. Lower bound = mean - marginmean-0.0392= 4.08%
  4. Upper bound = mean + marginmean+0.0392= 11.92%

The 95% interval runs from 4.08% to 11.92%.
