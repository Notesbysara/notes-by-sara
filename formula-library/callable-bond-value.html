Callable and Putable Bond Value

IntermediateFixed IncomeAlso known as: embedded option, callable bond price, putable bond price, call option on a bond
← Call and Put Payoffs and ProfitCapital Allocation Line and Optimal Risky Weight →

Finds: what a bond with an embedded option is worth.

When to use it: Use it to price a bond with an embedded option. The issuer owns a call option, so the holder gets a lower price. The holder owns a put option, so the bond is worth more. The option value comes from an interest rate model such as a binomial tree.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Pcallable\displaystyle P_{callable}=Pstraight−Vcall\displaystyle =P_{straight}-V_{call}Pputable\displaystyle P_{putable}=Pstraight+Vput\displaystyle =P_{straight}+V_{put}

In words: Callable bond price = Straight bond price − Value of the call option; Putable bond price = Straight bond price + Value of the put option

What each symbol means

P_straightPrice of an identical bond with no optionmoney
V_callValue of the issuer's right to redeem early, which the bondholder has effectively soldmoney
V_putValue of the holder's right to sell the bond back earlymoney

Worked example

A straight bond is worth 102.50 per 100. The issuer's call option is worth 4.10. A similar putable bond has a put option worth 2.30. What are the callable and putable prices?

Callable price102.5 − 4.198.40
Putable price102.5 + 2.3104.80
Call value as a share of the straight price4.1 ÷ 102.54%
The answer

The callable bond is worth 98.40 and the putable bond is worth 104.80. The call option takes 4% off the price that the same bond would otherwise have.
