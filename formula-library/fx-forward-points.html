Forward Exchange Rate and Forward Points

IntermediateInternational FinanceAlso known as: spot and forward exchange rates, forward premium, forward discount, swap points
← Forward Contract: Fair Price, Value and PayoffForward Rate from Spot Rates →

Finds: the forward rate and its premium or discount.

When to use it: Use it to price a forward exchange contract. The forward is set so that no one can profit from borrowing in one currency and lending in the other: the currency with the higher interest rate trades at a forward discount. Forward points are the quoted difference, added to the spot. The forward is not a prediction of the future spot rate. Day-count conventions vary by currency.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

F\displaystyle F=S 1+rdT/3601+rfT/360\displaystyle =S\,\frac{1+r_{d}T/360}{1+r_{f}T/360}Points\displaystyle \text{Points}=(F−S)×10,000\displaystyle =(F-S)\times10{,}000

In words: Forward rate = spot x (1 + domestic rate x days / 360) / (1 + foreign rate x days / 360), where rates are quoted as domestic currency per 1 unit of foreign currency; Forward points = (forward − spot) x 10,000; Annualized forward premium = (F / S − 1) x 360 / days

What each symbol means

SSpot rate: units of domestic currency per 1 foreign currencyrate
r_d, r_fDomestic and foreign interest rates for the periodper year
TDays to the forward datedays
FForward rate, set by the interest differential and not by a forecastrate

Worked example

EUR/USD spot is 1.0850 (USD per EUR). The 6-month USD rate is 5% and the EUR rate is 3%, with 180 days to the forward date.

Forward rate1.085 × (1 + 0.05 × 180 ÷ 360) ÷ (1 + 0.03 × 180 ÷ 360)1.09569
Forward points(1.09569 − 1.085) × 10,000106.9
Annualized forward premium(1.09569 ÷ 1.085 − 1) × 360 ÷ 1801.97%
The answer

The 180-day forward rate is 1.09569, which is 106.9 points above spot. The euro trades at a forward premium of 1.97% a year because USD interest rates are higher. Locking in the forward means a euro buyer pays 1.09569 for each euro at the forward date.
