Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)

IntermediateCorporate FinanceAlso known as: WACC, firm cost of capital, blended cost of capital, discount rate for the firm
← Warrant Value with DilutionWeighted Average Cost per Unit →

Finds: blended cost of all capital.

When to use it: Use it as the discount rate for a firm's overall cash flows (FCFF) and for projects that have the same risk as the firm. Use market values for the weights, not book values.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

WACC\displaystyle WACC=EVre+DVrd(1−T)+PVrp\displaystyle =\frac{E}{V}r_{e}+\frac{D}{V}r_{d}(1-T)+\frac{P}{V}r_{p}

In words: WACC = E/V x re + D/V x rd x (1 − T) + P/V x rp

What each symbol means

E, D, PMarket values of equity, debt and preferred stockmoney
VTotal capital, E + D + Pmoney
r_eCost of equityper year
r_dPre-tax cost of debtper year
r_pCost of preferred stockper year
TTax rate, as a decimaldecimal

Worked example

A company has equity of 600 (cost 10%), debt of 400 (cost 6%) and no preferred stock. The tax rate is 25%. What is its WACC?

Total capital V600 + 400 + 01,000
Weight of equity600 ÷ 1,00060%
Weight of debt400 ÷ 1,00040%
Weight of preferred0 ÷ 1,0000%
After-tax cost of debt0.06 × (1 − 0.25)4.50%
WACC0.6 × 0.1 + 0.4 × 0.045 + 0 × 07.80%
The answer

The WACC is 7.80%. Equity makes up 60% of the capital, and the after-tax cost of debt is 4.50%.
