Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)

Intermediate

Also known as: WACC, firm cost of capital, blended cost of capital, discount rate for the firm

Blended cost of all capital. Use it as the discount rate for a firm's overall cash flows (FCFF) and for projects that have the same risk as the firm. Use market values for the weights, not book values.

The formula

WACC=EVre+DVrd(1−T)+PVrpWACC=\frac{E}{V}r_{e}+\frac{D}{V}r_{d}(1-T)+\frac{P}{V}r_{p}

WACC = E/V x re + D/V x rd x (1 - T) + P/V x rp

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
E, D, PMarket values of equity, debt and preferred stockmoney
VTotal capital, E + D + Pmoney
r_eCost of equityper year
r_dPre-tax cost of debtper year
r_pCost of preferred stockper year
TTax rate, as a decimaldecimal

Worked example

A company has equity of 600 (cost 10%), debt of 400 (cost 6%) and no preferred stock. The tax rate is 25%. What is its WACC?

  1. Total capital V = E + D + P600+400+0= 1,000.00
  2. Weight of equity = E / V600 / 1000= 60.00%
  3. Weight of debt = D / V400 / 1000= 40.00%
  4. Weight of preferred = P / V0 / 1000= 0.00%
  5. After-tax cost of debt = rd x (1 - T)0.06 x (1-0.25)= 4.50%
  6. WACC = we x re + wd x after-tax rd + wp x rp0.6 x 0.1+0.4 x 0.045+0 x 0= 7.80%

The WACC is 7.80%. Equity makes up 60.00% of the capital, and the after-tax cost of debt is 4.50%.
