Ethereum Gas Fee Cost

Beginner

Also known as: Ethereum and smart contracts, gwei, base fee, priority tip, transaction cost on chain

The cost of a blockchain transaction. Use it to see what a transaction or smart-contract call costs. Fees rise when the network is busy and depend on how much computation the contract uses, not on the amount of money sent. A swap in a decentralized exchange may use five to ten times the gas of a plain transfer.

The formula

Fee=G×(b+t)×10−9 ETH,FeeUSD=Fee×P\text{Fee}=G\times(b+t)\times 10^{-9}\ \text{ETH},\quad \text{Fee}_{USD}=\text{Fee}\times P

Fee in ETH = gas used x (base fee + priority tip) in gwei x 0.000000001; Fee in money = fee in ETH x price of ETH; The base fee is burned, so burned part = gas used x base fee; Simple transfers use 21,000 gas and smart-contract calls use much more

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
GGas units the transaction uses (a measure of computation)units
bBase fee per unit of gas, set by network demandgwei
tPriority tip to the block producergwei
PPrice of ETHmoney

Worked example

A plain transfer uses 21,000 gas. The base fee is 18 gwei, the tip is 2 gwei and ETH is 3,000. A token swap uses 150,000 gas.

  1. Fee in ETH21000 x (18+2) / 1000000000= 0.000420
  2. Fee in money0.00042 x 3000= 1.26
  3. Burned part in ETH21000 x 18 / 1000000000= 0.000378
  4. Cost of the swap in money150000 x (18+2) / 1000000000 x 3000= 9.00

The transfer costs 0.000420 ETH, which is 1.26 in money. Of this, 0.000378 ETH is burned. A 150,000 gas swap at the same prices costs 9.00.
