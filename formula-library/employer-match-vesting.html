Employer Match and Vesting

Beginner

Also known as: pension matching, 401k match, vested balance, free money

Employer money earned, vested and left on the table. Use it to see how much employer money you earn and how much is yours if you leave. Your own contributions are always yours, while the employer's part vests over time. A match is an immediate return on your contribution, so contributing at least up to the cap is usually the first priority. Schedules differ: enter the start and full years for your plan (equal values give a cliff schedule).

The formula

Match=m×min⁡(c, cap)×S,Vested match=Match×v\text{Match}=m\times\min(c,\,cap)\times S,\quad \text{Vested match}=\text{Match}\times v

Employer match = match rate x min(your contribution %, match cap %) x salary; Vested share v rises with years of service (0 before the start year, 1 after the full year, in between linear for a graded schedule); Vested match = match x v

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
SSalarymoney
cYour contribution as a share of salarypercent
capHighest contribution share the employer will matchpercent
mEmployer match rate on matched contributionspercent
vVested share of the employer's money that is yours if you leavepercent

Worked example

Salary is 60,000. You contribute 4%. The employer matches 50% of contributions up to 6% of salary. Vesting is graded from year 2 to year 6, and you have 3 years of service.

  1. Your contribution = salary x share60000 x 0.04= 2,400.00
  2. Employer match = rate x min(contribution %, cap %) x salary0.5 x min(0.04, 0.06) x 60000= 1,200.00
  3. Immediate return on your contribution = match / contribution1200 / 2400= 50.0%
  4. Vested share0 if 3<2 else (1 if 3>=6 else (3-2) / (6-2))= 25.0%
  5. Vested match1200 x 0.25= 300.00
  6. Match forfeited if you leave1200-300= 900.00
  7. Match if you contribute the full cap0.5 x 0.06 x 60000= 1,800.00
  8. Match left unclaimed1800-1200= 600.00

You contribute 2,400.00 and the employer adds 1,200.00, an immediate return of 50.0% on your contribution. At 3 years of service 25.0% is vested, so 300.00 of the match is yours and 900.00 would be forfeited if you left. Contributing the full 6% would earn a match of 1,800.00 instead, so 600.00 a year is being left unclaimed.
