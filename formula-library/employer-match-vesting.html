Employer Match and Vesting

BeginnerPersonal FinanceAlso known as: pension matching, 401k match, vested balance, free money
← Emerging Market Return: Local, Unhedged and HedgedEndowment Spending Rule (Yale) →

Finds: employer money earned, vested and left on the table.

When to use it: Use it to see how much employer money you earn and how much is yours if you leave. Your own contributions are always yours, while the employer's part vests over time. A match is an immediate return on your contribution, so contributing at least up to the cap is usually the first priority. Schedules differ: enter the start and full years for your plan (equal values give a cliff schedule).

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Match\displaystyle \text{Match}=m×min⁡(c, cap)×S\displaystyle =m\times\min(c,\,cap)\times SVested match\displaystyle \text{Vested match}=Match×v\displaystyle =\text{Match}\times v

In words: Employer match = match rate x min(your contribution %, match cap %) x salary; Vested share v rises with years of service (0 before the start year, 1 after the full year, in between linear for a graded schedule); Vested match = match x v

What each symbol means

SSalarymoney
cYour contribution as a share of salarypercent
capHighest contribution share the employer will matchpercent
mEmployer match rate on matched contributionspercent
vVested share of the employer's money that is yours if you leavepercent

Worked example

Salary is 60,000. You contribute 4%. The employer matches 50% of contributions up to 6% of salary. Vesting is graded from year 2 to year 6, and you have 3 years of service.

Your contribution60,000 × 0.042,400
Employer match0.5 × min(0.04, 0.06) × 60,0001,200
Immediate return on your contribution1,200 ÷ 2,40050%
Vested share0 if 3<2 else (1 if 3>=6 else (3 − 2) ÷ (6 − 2))25%
Vested match1,200 × 0.25300
Match forfeited if you leave1,200 − 300900
Match if you contribute the full cap0.5 × 0.06 × 60,0001,800
Match left unclaimed1,800 − 1,200600
The answer

You contribute 2,400 and the employer adds 1,200, an immediate return of 50.0% on your contribution. At 3 years of service 25.0% is vested, so 300 of the match is yours and 900 would be forfeited if you left. Contributing the full 6% would earn a match of 1,800 instead, so 600 a year is being left unclaimed.
