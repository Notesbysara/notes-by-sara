Market Manipulation Screens: Order-to-Trade and Cancel Ratios

IntermediateRegulation, Ethics & ESGAlso known as: spoofing, layering, wash trading, order-to-trade ratio, cancellation rate
← Margin Call PriceMarket Ratios: P/B, P/S and EV/EBITDA →

Finds: whether trading patterns look like manipulation.

When to use it: Use it to see how exchanges and regulators screen for manipulation. Placing large orders and canceling them before execution (spoofing) gives a false view of demand; trading with oneself (wash trades) gives a false view of volume. A high ratio is a flag to investigate, since market makers can also have high ratios for legitimate reasons.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

OTR\displaystyle OTR=OrdersTrades\displaystyle =\frac{\text{Orders}}{\text{Trades}}Cancel rate\displaystyle \text{Cancel rate}=CanceledOrders\displaystyle =\frac{\text{Canceled}}{\text{Orders}}Self-match\displaystyle \text{Self-match}=VselfVtotal\displaystyle =\frac{V_{self}}{V_{total}}

In words: Order-to-trade ratio = orders entered / trades executed; Cancellation rate = orders canceled / orders entered; Self-trade share = volume where the same beneficial owner is on both sides / total volume; Very high ratios and quick cancellations are warning signs of spoofing or layering, and a high self-trade share of wash trading

What each symbol means

OrdersOrders entered in the periodcount
TradesOrders that were executedcount
V_selfVolume traded with oneself (no change in true ownership)shares

Worked example

In one day an account entered 50,000 orders, had 400 trades and canceled 48,000 orders. 5% of its volume was against itself. The exchange flags accounts with a ratio above 100 and a cancellation rate above 90%.

Order-to-trade ratio50,000 ÷ 400125
Cancellation rate48,000 ÷ 50,00096%
Self-trade share0.055%
Orders not canceled1 − 0.964%
Flag (11 if (125>100 and 0.96>0.9) else 01
The answer

The order-to-trade ratio is 125.0 and the cancellation rate is 96.0%. Both exceed the limits of 100 and 90%, so the account is flagged (flag = 1). Self-trading is 5.0% of volume. The share of orders that stayed live or filled is 4.0%.
