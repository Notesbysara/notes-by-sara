Market Manipulation Screens: Order-to-Trade and Cancel Ratios

Intermediate

Also known as: spoofing, layering, wash trading, order-to-trade ratio, cancellation rate

Whether trading patterns look like manipulation. Use it to see how exchanges and regulators screen for manipulation. Placing large orders and canceling them before execution (spoofing) gives a false view of demand; trading with oneself (wash trades) gives a false view of volume. A high ratio is a flag to investigate, since market makers can also have high ratios for legitimate reasons.

The formula

OTR=OrdersTrades,Cancel rate=CanceledOrders,Self-match=VselfVtotalOTR=\frac{\text{Orders}}{\text{Trades}},\quad \text{Cancel rate}=\frac{\text{Canceled}}{\text{Orders}},\quad \text{Self-match}=\frac{V_{self}}{V_{total}}

Order-to-trade ratio = orders entered / trades executed; Cancellation rate = orders canceled / orders entered; Self-trade share = volume where the same beneficial owner is on both sides / total volume; Very high ratios and quick cancellations are warning signs of spoofing or layering, and a high self-trade share of wash trading

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
OrdersOrders entered in the periodcount
TradesOrders that were executedcount
V_selfVolume traded with oneself (no change in true ownership)shares

Worked example

In one day an account entered 50,000 orders, had 400 trades and canceled 48,000 orders. 5% of its volume was against itself. The exchange flags accounts with a ratio above 100 and a cancellation rate above 90%.

  1. Order-to-trade ratio50000 / 400= 125.0
  2. Cancellation rate48000 / 50000= 96.0%
  3. Self-trade share0.05= 5.0%
  4. Orders not canceled1-0.96= 4.0%
  5. Flag (1 = both limits exceeded)1 if (125>100 and 0.96>0.9) else 0= 1

The order-to-trade ratio is 125.0 and the cancellation rate is 96.0%. Both exceed the limits of 100 and 90%, so the account is flagged (flag = 1). Self-trading is 5.0% of volume. The share of orders that stayed live or filled is 4.0%.
