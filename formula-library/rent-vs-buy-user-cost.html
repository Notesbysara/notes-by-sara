Rent vs Buy: Annual Cost of Owning

IntermediatePersonal FinanceAlso known as: renting vs buying a home, user cost of housing, price to rent ratio, break even rent
← Remaining Loan BalanceReplicating Portfolio (One-Step Binomial) →

Finds: whether owning costs more or less than renting, per year.

When to use it: Use it for a first comparison of renting and buying. Owning costs interest, tax, upkeep and the return lost on the down payment, offset by price growth. If the annual cost of owning is below annual rent, buying looks cheaper, but price growth is uncertain. It leaves out buying and selling costs, tax relief and how long you will stay, which matter a lot over short stays.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Cost of owning\displaystyle \text{Cost of owning}=P[i(1−d)+o d+τ+m−g]\displaystyle =P\left[i(1-d)+o\,d+\tau+m-g\right]Price-to-rent\displaystyle \text{Price-to-rent}=P12R\displaystyle =\frac{P}{12R}

In words: Annual cost of owning = Price x [mortgage rate x (1 − down payment share) + opportunity return x down payment share + property tax rate + maintenance rate − price growth rate]; Price-to-rent ratio = Price / (12 x monthly rent)

What each symbol means

PPurchase pricemoney
iMortgage interest rateper year
dDown payment as a share of pricepercent
oReturn you give up by tying money in the down paymentper year
tau, mProperty tax rate and maintenance rate, as shares of priceper year
gExpected annual rise in the home's priceper year
RMonthly rent for a comparable homemoney

Worked example

A home costs 400,000 with 20% down at a 6.5% mortgage rate. Property tax is 1.1%, maintenance 1.0%, expected price growth 3% and the down payment could otherwise earn 5%. Comparable rent is 2,200 a month.

Annual cost of owning400,000 × (0.065 × (1 − 0.2) + 0.05 × 0.2 + 0.011 + 0.01 − 0.03)21,200
Per month21,200 ÷ 121,766.67
Annual rent12 × 2,20026,400
Price-to-rent ratio400,000 ÷ 26,40015.2
Owning cheaper than renting? (11 if 21,200<26,400 else 01
Break-even monthly rent21,200 ÷ 121,766.67
Monthly cost of owning if growth is 1%400,000 × (0.065 × (1 − 0.2) + 0.05 × 0.2 + 0.011 + 0.01 − 0.01) ÷ 122,433.33
The answer

Owning costs 21,200 a year, or 1,766.67 a month, against rent of 26,400 a year. The price-to-rent ratio is 15.2. The break-even rent is 1,766.67 a month: if your rent is above that, buying looks cheaper. If prices grew only 1% the monthly cost of owning would be 2,433.33.
