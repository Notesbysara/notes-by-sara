Household Financial Health Ratios

Beginner

Also known as: household finance, savings rate, debt service ratio, liquidity ratio, solvency ratio

A quick scorecard of a household's finances. Use it as a quick household health check, much as analysts use ratios for a company. Common guides are a savings rate near 10 to 20% or more, debt service below about 35%, three to six months of liquidity and a rising solvency ratio. Guides depend on circumstances, so use them to spot weak areas and track progress, not as pass or fail marks.

The formula

Savings rate=SY,  Debt service=DY,  Liquidity=Liquid assetsE,  Solvency=A−LA\text{Savings rate}=\frac{S}{Y},\; \text{Debt service}=\frac{D}{Y},\; \text{Liquidity}=\frac{\text{Liquid assets}}{E},\; \text{Solvency}=\frac{A-L}{A}

Savings rate = monthly saving / monthly income; Debt service ratio = monthly debt payments / monthly income; Liquidity ratio = liquid assets / monthly essential spending (months of cover); Solvency ratio = (assets - liabilities) / assets

Worked example

A household has take-home income of 5,200 a month, saves 600, pays 900 in debt payments and spends 3,000 on essentials. It holds 18,000 in liquid assets, 330,000 in total assets and 175,000 in liabilities.

  1. Savings rate = saving / income600 / 5200= 11.5%
  2. Debt service ratio = debt payments / income900 / 5200= 17.3%
  3. Liquidity ratio = liquid assets / essentials (months)18000 / 3000= 6.0
  4. Solvency ratio = (assets - liabilities) / assets(330000-175000) / 330000= 47.0%
  5. Ratios inside common guides? (1 = all four, 0 = not all)1 if (0.115385>=0.1 and 0.173077<=0.35 and 6>=3 and 0.469697>0) else 0= 1

The savings rate is 11.5%, debt payments take 17.3% of income, liquid assets cover 6.0 months of essentials, and the solvency ratio is 47.0%. A result of 1 here means all four fall inside the common guides: 1.
