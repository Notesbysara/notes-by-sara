Embedded Finance: Platform Revenue from Financial Products

BeginnerFintech & Digital FinanceAlso known as: embedded finance, banking as a service, attach rate, take rate, revenue share
← Efficiency Ratios: Asset, Inventory, Receivable and Payable TurnoverEmergency Fund Target and Monthly Saving →

Finds: what a platform earns from embedded finance.

When to use it: Use it to size what a non-bank platform (a marketplace or software firm) can earn by offering payments, loans or insurance inside its own product. The bank partner holds the license and takes a share. Growth depends on how many customers use the product, which is the attach rate.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

R\displaystyle R=G×a×t\displaystyle =G\times a\times tΠ\displaystyle \Pi=G a (t−k)−F\displaystyle =G\,a\,(t-k)-F

In words: Revenue = platform sales volume x share of sales that use the financial product (attach rate) x take rate; Profit = volume x attach rate x (take rate − partner cost rate) − fixed cost; Break-even attach rate = fixed cost / (volume x (take rate − cost rate))

What each symbol means

GGross volume through the platformmoney
aAttach rate: share of volume that uses the embedded productpercent
tTake rate: platform's revenue as a share of that volumepercent
kCost rate paid to the bank or partner behind the productpercent
FFixed cost of integration and compliancemoney

Worked example

A platform processes 500m of sales. 15% of volume uses its embedded lending. It earns 2.5% of that volume and pays its bank partner 1.0%. Integration and compliance cost 1.2m a year.

Revenue500,000,000 × 0.15 × 0.0251,875,000
Partner cost500,000,000 × 0.15 × 0.01750,000
Profit1,875,000 − 750,000 − 1,200,000−75,000
Break-even attach rate1,200,000 ÷ (500,000,000 × (0.025 − 0.01))16%
The answer

Revenue is 1,875,000, partner cost 750,000 and profit −75,000 after fixed costs. The break-even attach rate is 16.0%.
