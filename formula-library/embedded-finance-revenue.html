Embedded Finance: Platform Revenue from Financial Products

Beginner

Also known as: embedded finance, banking as a service, attach rate, take rate, revenue share

What a platform earns from embedded finance. Use it to size what a non-bank platform (a marketplace or software firm) can earn by offering payments, loans or insurance inside its own product. The bank partner holds the license and takes a share. Growth depends on how many customers use the product, which is the attach rate.

The formula

R=G×a×t,Π=G a (t−k)−FR=G\times a\times t,\quad \Pi=G\,a\,(t-k)-F

Revenue = platform sales volume x share of sales that use the financial product (attach rate) x take rate; Profit = volume x attach rate x (take rate - partner cost rate) - fixed cost; Break-even attach rate = fixed cost / (volume x (take rate - cost rate))

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
GGross volume through the platformmoney
aAttach rate: share of volume that uses the embedded productpercent
tTake rate: platform's revenue as a share of that volumepercent
kCost rate paid to the bank or partner behind the productpercent
FFixed cost of integration and compliancemoney

Worked example

A platform processes 500m of sales. 15% of volume uses its embedded lending. It earns 2.5% of that volume and pays its bank partner 1.0%. Integration and compliance cost 1.2m a year.

  1. Revenue = G x a x t500,000,000 x 0.15 x 0.025= 1,875,000.00
  2. Partner cost = G x a x k500,000,000 x 0.15 x 0.01= 750,000.00
  3. Profit = revenue - cost - fixed1,875,000-750000-1,200,000= -75,000.00
  4. Break-even attach rate1,200,000 / (500,000,000 x (0.025-0.01))= 16.0%

Revenue is 1,875,000.00, partner cost 750,000.00 and profit -75,000.00 after fixed costs. The break-even attach rate is 16.0%.
