Purchasing Power Parity: Valuation and Expected Rate

Intermediate

Also known as: PPP, Big Mac index, real exchange rate, relative PPP

Whether a currency looks over or under-valued and where parity points. Use it as a long-run anchor for exchange rates. The same goods should cost the same everywhere after conversion; if a basket is cheaper abroad, that currency looks undervalued. Relative PPP says the currency of the higher-inflation country should weaken by the inflation gap. PPP holds poorly in the short run and for non-traded goods and services, and it can take years to correct.

The formula

SPPP=PfPd,Misvaluation=SSPPP−1,St=S0(1+πf1+πd)tS^{PPP}=\frac{P_{f}}{P_{d}},\quad \text{Misvaluation}=\frac{S}{S^{PPP}}-1,\quad S_{t}=S_{0}\left(\frac{1+\pi_{f}}{1+\pi_{d}}\right)^{t}

Absolute PPP rate (foreign currency per 1 domestic) = foreign price of a basket / domestic price of the same basket; Misvaluation = spot / PPP rate - 1; Relative PPP: the foreign currency per domestic unit changes by (1 + foreign inflation) / (1 + domestic inflation) each year

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
P_f, P_dPrice of the same basket (for example a burger) in foreign and domestic currencymoney
SSpot rate: foreign currency per 1 unit of domestic currencyrate
pi_f, pi_dInflation abroad and at homeper year

Worked example

A burger costs 5.69 in the US and 6.50 Swiss francs in Switzerland. The spot rate is 0.88 francs per dollar. Inflation is 3% in the US and 1% in Switzerland.

  1. PPP rate = foreign price / domestic price6.5 / 5.69= 1.1424
  2. Misvaluation = spot / PPP - 10.88 / 1.14236-1= -23.0%
  3. Relative PPP rate after n years = S x ((1 + foreign) / (1 + domestic))^n0.88 x ((1+0.01) / (1+0.03))^5= 0.7978
  4. Change in the rate over n years0.797817 / 0.88-1= -9.3%

The PPP rate is 1.1424 francs per dollar. The market rate of 0.88 is -23.0% from it (a negative number means the franc is overvalued against the dollar by this measure). With a 2-point inflation gap relative PPP implies the rate will fall to 0.7978 francs per dollar after 5 years.
