Purchasing Power Parity: Valuation and Expected Rate

IntermediateInternational FinanceAlso known as: PPP, Big Mac index, real exchange rate, relative PPP
← Purchasing Power of Money Over TimePut-Call Parity →

Finds: whether a currency looks over or under-valued and where parity points.

When to use it: Use it as a long-run anchor for exchange rates. The same goods should cost the same everywhere after conversion; if a basket is cheaper abroad, that currency looks undervalued. Relative PPP says the currency of the higher-inflation country should weaken by the inflation gap. PPP holds poorly in the short run and for non-traded goods and services, and it can take years to correct.

The formula

SPPP\displaystyle S^{PPP}=PfPd\displaystyle =\frac{P_{f}}{P_{d}}Misvaluation\displaystyle \text{Misvaluation}=SSPPP−1\displaystyle =\frac{S}{S^{PPP}}-1St\displaystyle S_{t}=S0(1+πf1+πd)t\displaystyle =S_{0}\left(\frac{1+\pi_{f}}{1+\pi_{d}}\right)^{t}

In words: Absolute PPP rate (foreign currency per 1 domestic) = foreign price of a basket / domestic price of the same basket; Misvaluation = spot / PPP rate − 1; Relative PPP: the foreign currency per domestic unit changes by (1 + foreign inflation) / (1 + domestic inflation) each year

What each symbol means

P_f, P_dPrice of the same basket (for example a burger) in foreign and domestic currencymoney
SSpot rate: foreign currency per 1 unit of domestic currencyrate
pi_f, pi_dInflation abroad and at homeper year

Worked example

A burger costs 5.69 in the US and 6.50 Swiss francs in Switzerland. The spot rate is 0.88 francs per dollar. Inflation is 3% in the US and 1% in Switzerland.

PPP rate6.5 ÷ 5.691.1424
Misvaluation0.88 ÷ 1.14236 − 1−23%
Relative PPP rate after n years0.88 × ((1 + 0.01) ÷ (1 + 0.03))^50.7978
Change in the rate over n years0.797817 ÷ 0.88 − 1−9.3%
The answer

The PPP rate is 1.1424 francs per dollar. The market rate of 0.88 is −23.0% from it (a negative number means the franc is overvalued against the dollar by this measure). With a 2-point inflation gap relative PPP implies the rate will fall to 0.7978 francs per dollar after 5 years.
