DV01 (Dollar Value of a Basis Point)

IntermediateFixed IncomeAlso known as: PV01, price value of a basis point, PVBP, key rate duration, rate risk in money terms
← Duration Gap and Equity SensitivityEarly-Payment Discount Return (Supply Chain Finance) →

Finds: rate risk in money for a 1 basis-point move.

When to use it: Use it to express interest rate risk in money. Traders set limits and hedge in DV01. To hedge, trade an instrument with an offsetting DV01. Key rate duration breaks the same risk down by maturity point on the curve, so you can see exposure to the 2-year rate separately from the 10-year rate.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

DV01\displaystyle DV01=Dmod×P×0.0001\displaystyle =D_{mod}\times P\times 0.0001

In words: DV01 = Modified duration x Price x 0.0001 (the money change in price for a 1 basis-point change in yield)

What each symbol means

D_modModified durationyears
PPrice (or market value) of the positionmoney
0.0001One basis point as a decimaldecimal

Worked example

A 10-year bond with a 5% coupon paid twice a year yields 6%. You hold 10 million of face value. What is the DV01 of the position?

Price per bond925.61
Modified duration7.665
DV01 per bond7.66505 × 925.613 × 0.00010.7095
DV01 of the position0.709486 × 10,000,000 ÷ 1,0007,094.86
Direct check: price fall for +1 bp, per bond x position7,091.54
The answer

A 1 basis-point rise in yield cuts the value of the position by 7,094.86. Repricing the bond directly gives 7,091.54, confirming it.
