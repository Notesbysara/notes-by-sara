Bid-Ask Spread and Trading Cost

Beginner

Also known as: spread, market maker spread, round trip cost, mid price

The cost of buying and selling straight away. Use it to measure the cost of trading. A market maker earns the spread by buying at the bid and selling at the ask. A buyer who sells straight away loses the whole spread.

The formula

Spread=Pask−Pbid,Relative spread=Pask−Pbid(Pask+Pbid)/2\text{Spread}=P_{ask}-P_{bid},\quad \text{Relative spread}=\frac{P_{ask}-P_{bid}}{(P_{ask}+P_{bid})/2}

Spread = Ask - Bid; Relative spread = Spread / Mid price

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
P_askLowest price a seller will accept, the price you pay when you buymoney
P_bidHighest price a buyer will pay, the price you get when you sellmoney
MidHalfway between bid and askmoney

Worked example

A stock is quoted at 49.95 bid and 50.05 ask. You buy 500 shares and sell them straight back. What does the round trip cost?

  1. Spread = ask - bid50.05-49.95= 0.1000
  2. Mid price = (ask + bid) / 2(50.05+49.95) / 2= 50.0000
  3. Relative spread = spread / mid0.1 / 50= 0.200%
  4. Round-trip cost = spread x shares0.1 x 500= 50.00

The spread is 0.1000, which is 0.200% of the mid price. Buying at the ask and selling at the bid costs 50.00 on 500 shares.
