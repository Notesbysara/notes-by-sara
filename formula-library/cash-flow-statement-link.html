Linking the Three Statements: Cash Flow and Balance Check

Intermediate

Also known as: indirect method, cash flow statement, balance sheet check, three statement model

Ending cash and a check that the balance sheet balances. Use it to see how the three statements connect. Net income flows into the cash flow statement and retained earnings. Capex and depreciation move fixed assets. Debt and dividends change liabilities and equity. Ending cash goes back to the balance sheet, and the balance sheet must still balance, which is the check at the bottom.

The formula

ΔCash=CFO+CFI+CFF,CFO=NI+D&A−ΔNWC\Delta Cash=CFO+CFI+CFF,\quad CFO=NI+D\&A-\Delta NWC

Cash flow from operations = Net income + Depreciation - Increase in working capital; Cash flow from investing = -Capex; Cash flow from financing = Debt issued - Debt repaid - Dividends + Shares issued; Ending cash = Opening cash + total cash flow

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
NINet income from the income statementmoney
D&ADepreciation and amortization, a non-cash expense added backmoney
Delta NWCIncrease in net working capital from the balance sheet, which uses cashmoney
CFO, CFI, CFFCash flows from operating, investing and financing activitiesmoney

Worked example

A company has net income of 90, depreciation of 90, an increase in working capital of 20 and capex of 110. It issues 50 of debt, repays 20 and pays dividends of 30. Opening cash is 100.

  1. CFO = NI + D&A - increase in NWC90+90-20= 160.00
  2. CFI = -capex-110= -110.00
  3. CFF = debt issued - repaid - dividends50-20-30= 0.00
  4. Net change in cash160+-110+0= 50.00
  5. Ending cash100+50= 150.00
  6. Change in assets = cash change + capex - depreciation + increase in NWC50+110-90+20= 90.00
  7. Change in liabilities and equity = debt change + NI - dividends50-20+90-30= 90.00
  8. Difference (must be zero)90-90= 0.00

Operations bring in 160.00, investing uses -110.00 and financing gives 0.00. Cash rises by 50.00 to 150.00. Balance check: assets rose by 90.00 and liabilities plus equity rose by 90.00, a difference of 0.00, so the model balances.
