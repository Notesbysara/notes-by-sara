Sum-of-the-Parts Valuation

IntermediateValuationAlso known as: SOTP, conglomerate valuation, segment valuation, conglomerate discount
← Student Loan Interest Accrual and CapitalizationSum-of-the-Years'-Digits Depreciation →

Finds: value of a multi-business group.

When to use it: Use it for a group with several businesses that deserve different multiples. It shows whether the whole is worth more or less than the parts.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

EV\displaystyle EV=∑iEBITDAi×Mi\displaystyle =\sum_{i}EBITDA_{i}\times M_{i}Equity\displaystyle \text{Equity}=EV(1−Discount)−Net debt\displaystyle =EV(1-\text{Discount})-\text{Net debt}

In words: EV = sum of (Segment EBITDA x Segment multiple); Equity = EV x (1 − Conglomerate discount) − Net debt

What each symbol means

EBITDA_iEBITDA of business segment imoney
M_iMultiple for companies similar to segment inumber
DiscountConglomerate discount, a markdown for the cost and complexity of a diversified grouppercent
Net debtDebt minus cash for the groupmoney

Worked example

A group has three segments with EBITDA of 120, 80 and 50, valued at multiples of 10, 8 and 12. A 10% conglomerate discount applies and net debt is 500. What is the equity value?

Value of each segment1,200, 640, 600
Enterprise value2,440
Value after discount2,440 × (1 − 0.1)2,196
Equity value2,196 − 5001,696
The answer

The segment values are 1,200, 640, 600, adding up to 2,440. After the discount and net debt, the equity is worth 1,696.
