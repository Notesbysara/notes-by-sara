Remaining Loan Balance

IntermediateFinance FoundationsAlso known as: outstanding balance, balance after k payments, amortization balance, how much do I still owe
← Relevering BetaRent vs Buy: Annual Cost of Owning →

Finds: how much of a loan is still owed.

When to use it: Use it to find how much of a loan is still owed part-way through, for example how much of a mortgage remains after 5 years.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Bk\displaystyle B_{k}=P(1+r)k−PMT (1+r)k−1r\displaystyle = P(1+r)^{k}-PMT\,\frac{(1+r)^{k}-1}{r}

In words: Balance after k payments = P x (1 + r)^k − PMT x ((1 + r)^k − 1) / r

What each symbol means

B_kBalance still owed after k paymentsmoney
POriginal loan amountmoney
rInterest rate per payment period, as a decimalper period
PMTRegular paymentmoney
kNumber of payments already madeperiods

Worked example

You took a 200,000 loan at 6% a year over 30 years with monthly payments. How much do you still owe after 60 payments (5 years)?

Rate per period0.06 ÷ 120.5000%
Total payments30 × 12360
Payment200,000 × 0.005 ÷ (1 − (1 + 0.005)^(−360))1,199.10
Balance200,000 × (1 + 0.005)^60 − 1,199.1 × ((1 + 0.005)^60 − 1) ÷ 0.005186,108.71
Principal repaid so far200,000 − 186,10913,891.29
The answer

After 60 payments you still owe 186,108.71. You have repaid only 13,891.29 of the loan so far.
