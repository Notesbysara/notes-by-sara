Remaining Loan Balance

Intermediate

Also known as: outstanding balance, balance after k payments, amortization balance, how much do I still owe

How much of a loan is still owed. Use it to find how much of a loan is still owed part-way through, for example how much of a mortgage remains after 5 years.

The formula

Bk=P(1+r)k−PMT (1+r)k−1rB_{k} = P(1+r)^{k}-PMT\,\frac{(1+r)^{k}-1}{r}

Balance after k payments = P x (1 + r)^k - PMT x ((1 + r)^k - 1) / r

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
B_kBalance still owed after k paymentsmoney
POriginal loan amountmoney
rInterest rate per payment period, as a decimalper period
PMTRegular paymentmoney
kNumber of payments already madeperiods

Worked example

You took a 200,000 loan at 6% a year over 30 years with monthly payments. How much do you still owe after 60 payments (5 years)?

  1. Rate per period = annual rate / payments per year0.06 / 12= 0.5000%
  2. Total payments = years x payments per year30 x 12= 360
  3. Payment = P x r / (1 - (1 + r)^-n)200000 x 0.005 / (1-(1+0.005)^(-360))= 1,199.10
  4. Balance = P x (1 + r)^k - PMT x ((1 + r)^k - 1) / r200000 x (1+0.005)^60-1199.1 x ((1+0.005)^60-1) / 0.005= 186,108.71
  5. Principal repaid so far = P - balance200000-186109= 13,891.29

After 60 payments you still owe 186,108.71. You have repaid only 13,891.29 of the loan so far.
