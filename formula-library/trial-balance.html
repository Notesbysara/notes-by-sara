Debits Equal Credits (Trial Balance)

BeginnerFinance FoundationsAlso known as: trial balance check, double entry check, debit credit rule
← DCF Enterprise Value (Explicit Period plus Terminal Value)Debt Ratio →

Finds: whether the books balance.

When to use it: Use it as the basic check on bookkeeping. After recording every transaction, total debits must equal total credits. If they do not, there is an error to find.

FormulaExample

The formula

∑Debits\displaystyle \sum \text{Debits}=∑Credits\displaystyle =\sum \text{Credits}

In words: Total debits = Total credits

What each symbol means

DebitsEntries on the left side of an account. They increase assets and expenses, and reduce liabilities, equity and revenuemoney
CreditsEntries on the right side of an account. They increase liabilities, equity and revenue, and reduce assets and expensesmoney

Worked example

A trial balance lists debit balances of 600 (cash), 400 (equipment) and 350 (expenses), and credit balances of 400 (loan), 500 (capital) and 450 (revenue). Do the totals agree?

Total debits1,350
Total credits1,350
Difference1,350 − 1,3500
The answer

Total debits are 1,350 and total credits are 1,350. The difference is 0, so the books balance.
