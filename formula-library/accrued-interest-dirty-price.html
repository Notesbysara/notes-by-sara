Accrued Interest and Dirty Price

IntermediateFixed IncomeAlso known as: clean price, full price, government bond settlement, treasury note
← Accrual Ratio (Earnings Quality)Additional Funds Needed (Percent-of-Sales Forecast) →

Finds: what a buyer pays between coupon dates.

When to use it: Use it when you buy a government bond between coupon dates. Quoted prices are clean, without accrued interest. The buyer pays the clean price plus the interest earned since the last coupon, which the seller is owed.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

AI\displaystyle AI=F cm×dsincedperiod\displaystyle =\frac{F\,c}{m}\times\frac{d_{since}}{d_{period}}Pdirty\displaystyle P_{dirty}=Pclean+AI\displaystyle =P_{clean}+AI

In words: Accrued interest = Coupon payment x Days since last coupon / Days in the coupon period; Dirty (full) price = Clean (quoted) price + Accrued interest

Worked example

A 1,000 face value government bond with a 4% coupon paid twice a year is quoted at a clean price of 99.50. It is 62 days since the last coupon in a 181-day period. What does the buyer pay?

Coupon payment1,000 × 0.04 ÷ 220
Accrued interest20 × 62 ÷ 1816.85
Clean price in money1,000 × 99.5 ÷ 100995
Dirty price995 + 6.850831,001.85
The answer

The coupon payment is 20, of which 6.85 has accrued. The clean price is 995 per bond, so the buyer pays 1,001.85 in total.
