Beta (Regression Slope)

Intermediate

Also known as: stock beta, market beta, regression slope, systematic risk

Stock sensitivity to the market. Use it to measure how much a stock tends to move when the market moves. A beta above 1 means the stock swings more than the market, and below 1 means it swings less.

The formula

β=Cov(Ri,Rm)Var(Rm)\beta = \frac{Cov(R_{i},R_{m})}{Var(R_{m})}

Beta = Cov(stock, market) / Var(market)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
betaHow strongly the stock's return moves with the marketnumber
Cov(R_i,R_m)Covariance of the stock's returns and the market's returnspercent squared
Var(R_m)Variance of the market's returnspercent squared

Worked example

A stock returned 12%, -6% and 18% while the market returned 8%, -2% and 12% in the same three periods. What is the stock's beta?

  1. Covariance of stock and market= 0.0090
  2. Variance of the market= 0.0052
  3. Beta = covariance / market variance0.009 / 0.0052= 1.73

The beta is 1.73, so the stock tends to move more than the market.
