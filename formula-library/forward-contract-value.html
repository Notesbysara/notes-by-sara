Forward Contract: Fair Price, Value and Payoff

Beginner

Also known as: forward, forward agreement, long forward, short forward

A forward's fair price, value and payoff. Use it for a forward on an asset that pays no income and costs nothing to store. A forward is agreed with no money changing hands, so its fair delivery price makes the value zero at the start. As time passes its value to each side moves with the spot price.

The formula

F0=S0(1+r)T,Vt=St−K(1+r)T−t,Payoff=ST−KF_{0}=S_{0}(1+r)^{T},\quad V_{t}=S_{t}-\frac{K}{(1+r)^{T-t}},\quad \text{Payoff}=S_{T}-K

Fair forward price = Spot x (1 + r)^T; Value of a long forward = Spot now - Delivery price / (1 + r)^(time left); Payoff to the long at expiry = Spot at expiry - Delivery price

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
S_0Spot price of the asset todaymoney
KDelivery price fixed in the forward contractmoney
rRisk-free rate per yearper year
TTime to delivery, in yearsyears
S_TSpot price on the delivery datemoney

Worked example

An asset trades at 100 and the risk-free rate is 4%. You agree to buy it in 1 year at 102. Six months later the spot is 104 with 0.5 years left. What is the fair price at the start, the value of your contract at the six-month point, and your payoff if the spot ends at 108?

  1. Fair forward price today = S0 x (1 + r)^T100 x (1+0.04)^1= 104.00
  2. Value today of the contract at K = S0 - K / (1 + r)^T100-102 / (1+0.04)^1= 1.92
  3. Value of the long later = S - K / (1 + r)^(time left)104-102 / (1+0.04)^0.5= 3.98
  4. Payoff to the long at delivery = ST - K108-102= 6.00

The fair forward price today is 104.00, so a delivery price of 102 is slightly cheap for the buyer. After six months the contract is worth 3.98. If the spot ends at 108 the long side gains 6.00 and the short side loses the same.
