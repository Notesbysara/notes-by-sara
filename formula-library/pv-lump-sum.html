Present Value of a Lump Sum

BeginnerFinance FoundationsAlso known as: discounting, present value, PV, discount formula
← Present Value of a Growing AnnuityPresent Value of an Annuity Due →

Finds: value today of a future amount.

When to use it: Use it to find what a single amount you will receive in the future is worth today, for example to compare it with an amount offered now.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

PV\displaystyle PV=FV(1+r)n\displaystyle = \frac{FV}{(1+r)^{n}}

In words: PV = FV / (1 + r)^n

What each symbol means

PVPresent value, what the future amount is worth todaymoney
FVFuture value, the amount you will receive latermoney
rDiscount rate per period, as a decimalper period
nNumber of periods until you receive the moneyperiods

Worked example

You will receive 10,000 in 5 years. With a discount rate of 8% a year, what is it worth today?

Discount factor(1 + 0.08)^51.4693
PV10,000 ÷ 1.469336,805.83
The answer

The 10,000 you will receive in 5 years is worth 6,805.83 today.
