Present Value of a Lump Sum

Beginner

Also known as: discounting, present value, PV, discount formula

Value today of a future amount. Use it to find what a single amount you will receive in the future is worth today, for example to compare it with an amount offered now.

The formula

PV=FV(1+r)nPV = \frac{FV}{(1+r)^{n}}

PV = FV / (1 + r)^n

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
PVPresent value, what the future amount is worth todaymoney
FVFuture value, the amount you will receive latermoney
rDiscount rate per period, as a decimalper period
nNumber of periods until you receive the moneyperiods

Worked example

You will receive 10,000 in 5 years. With a discount rate of 8% a year, what is it worth today?

  1. Discount factor = (1 + r)^n(1+0.08)^5= 1.4693
  2. PV = FV / discount factor10000 / 1.46933= 6,805.83

The 10,000 you will receive in 5 years is worth 6,805.83 today.
