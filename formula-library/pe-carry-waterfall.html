Private Equity Distribution Waterfall and Carry

IntermediateBanking & Financial InstitutionsAlso known as: private equity firms, carried interest, preferred return, catch-up, TVPI DPI
← Private Credit: Net Yield after Losses and FeesPrivate Fund Metrics: IRR, MOIC, TVPI, DPI, RVPI →

Finds: how a private equity fund splits its profit.

When to use it: Use it to see how profits split between investors (LPs) and the manager (GP) under a European (whole-fund) waterfall. Below the preferred return the manager earns no carry. The catch-up then lets the manager earn carry on all profit, not only on the excess. Multiples show performance: DPI is cash returned per unit invested, and TVPI includes unrealized value. Terms differ by fund and the example uses a single investment and exit for simplicity.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Pref\displaystyle \text{Pref}=C[(1+h)T−1]\displaystyle =C\left[(1+h)^{T}-1\right]Catch-up\displaystyle \text{Catch-up}=c1−c Pref\displaystyle =\frac{c}{1-c}\,\text{Pref}GP\displaystyle GP=Catch-up+c (Profit−Pref−Catch-up)\displaystyle =\text{Catch-up}+c\,(\text{Profit}-\text{Pref}-\text{Catch-up})

In words: Order of payment: (1) return of capital to investors, (2) preferred return, (3) catch-up to the manager until it holds the carry share of all profit, (4) remaining profit split with carry % to the manager. With full catch-up the manager ends with carry % x total profit once profits are large enough

What each symbol means

CCapital invested by the fund's investors (LPs)money
hPreferred return (hurdle), compounded each yearpercent
cCarried interest rate, often 20%percent
TYears investedyears

Worked example

Investors put in 100 and receive 220 of proceeds after 5 years. The preferred return is 8% a year, carry is 20% with a full catch-up.

Profit220 − 100120
Preferred return100 × ((1 + 0.08)^5 − 1)46.93
Full catch-up amount0.2 ÷ (1 − 0.2) × 46.932811.73
Manager carrymin(max(120 − 46.9328, 0), 11.7332) + 0.2 × max(120 − 46.9328 − 11.7332, 0)24
Manager share of profit24 ÷ 12020%
Investors receive220 − 24196
Gross multiple220 ÷ 1002.20
Net multiple to investors196 ÷ 1001.96
Net annual return to investors1.96^(1 ÷ 5) − 114.41%
The answer

Profit is 120. The preferred return is 46.93 and the catch-up is 11.73. The manager receives 24, which is 20.0% of the profit, and investors receive 196. Net multiple to investors is 1.96 (gross 2.20) and net annual return 14.41%.
