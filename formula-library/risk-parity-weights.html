Risk Parity Weights (Inverse Volatility)

Intermediate

Also known as: risk parity, inverse volatility weights, equal risk contribution

Weights that equalize risk. Use it to build a portfolio where each asset contributes about the same amount of risk, instead of the same amount of money. This simple version assumes the assets are uncorrelated. The full version also uses correlations.

The formula

wi=1/σi∑j1/σjw_{i}=\frac{1/\sigma_{i}}{\sum_{j}1/\sigma_{j}}

Weight of asset i = (1 / its volatility) / sum of (1 / volatility) across all assets

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
w_iWeight of asset i in the portfoliopercent
sigma_iVolatility (standard deviation) of asset iper year

Worked example

A portfolio holds shares with a volatility of 15%, government bonds with a volatility of 5% and commodities with a volatility of 8%. What are the inverse-volatility weights?

  1. Inverse volatility = 1 / s= 6.67, 20.00, 12.50
  2. Sum of the inverses= 39.17
  3. Weight = inverse / sum= 17.02%, 51.06%, 31.91%

The weights are 17.02%, 51.06%, 31.91%. The low-risk bonds get the largest share, because each asset is sized so that it adds about the same risk.
