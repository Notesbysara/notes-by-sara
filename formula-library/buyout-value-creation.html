Buyout Returns and Value Creation Bridge

Intermediate

Also known as: PE buyout returns, EBITDA growth multiple expansion deleveraging, LBO value bridge, growth equity returns

Buyout returns and what drove them. Use it to see where a buyout return comes from. A fund buys with a lot of debt, grows earnings, pays debt down with cash flow and sells. Splitting the gain into growth, multiple change and deleveraging shows how much was skill and how much was luck in the exit price. It assumes no payouts between entry and exit.

The formula

MOIC=EexitEentry,IRR=MOIC1/n−1,ΔE=(EBITDA1−EBITDA0)m0⏟growth+(m1−m0)EBITDA1⏟multiple+(D0−D1)⏟deleveragingMOIC=\frac{E_{exit}}{E_{entry}},\quad IRR=MOIC^{1/n}-1,\quad \Delta E=\underbrace{(EBITDA_{1}-EBITDA_{0})m_{0}}_{growth}+\underbrace{(m_{1}-m_{0})EBITDA_{1}}_{multiple}+\underbrace{(D_{0}-D_{1})}_{deleveraging}

Entry equity = EBITDA x entry multiple x (1 - debt share); Exit equity = EBITDA at exit x exit multiple - remaining debt; MOIC = exit equity / entry equity; IRR = MOIC^(1/years) - 1 when there are no interim payouts; Gain splits into EBITDA growth x entry multiple, multiple change x exit EBITDA, and debt paid down

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
m_0, m_1Enterprise value to EBITDA multiple paid at entry and received at exitnumber
D_0, D_1Net debt at entry and at exitmoney
nYears heldyears

Worked example

A fund buys a company with EBITDA of 100 at 8 times, using 50% debt. After 5 years EBITDA is 140, the exit multiple is 9 times and 260 of cash has repaid debt.

  1. Entry enterprise value100 x 8= 800.00
  2. Entry debt800 x 0.5= 400.00
  3. Entry equity800-400= 400.00
  4. Exit enterprise value140 x 9= 1,260.00
  5. Exit debt = entry debt - repayment400-260= 140.00
  6. Exit equity1260-d1= 1,120.00
  7. MOIC1120 / 400= 2.80
  8. IRR2.8^(1 / 5)-1= 22.9%
  9. Total gain1120-400= 720.00
  10. From EBITDA growth(140-100) x 8= 320.00
  11. From multiple change(9-8) x 140= 140.00
  12. From paying down debt260= 260.00

Entry value is 800.00, with 400.00 of equity. Exit value is 1,260.00 and equity is 1,120.00. That is a 2.80 times money multiple and an IRR of 22.9%. Of the 720.00 gain, 320.00 came from earnings growth, 140.00 from the higher multiple and 260.00 from paying down debt.
