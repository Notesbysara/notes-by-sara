Market Ratios: P/B, P/S and EV/EBITDA

Beginner

Also known as: price to book, price to sales, EV to EBITDA, market capitalization, enterprise value

How the market prices earnings, book value, sales and the whole business. Use it to compare what the market pays with what the company reports. P/E, EPS and dividend yield have their own entries. P/B suits asset-heavy firms and banks, P/S suits firms without profits, and EV/EBITDA compares firms with different debt.

The formula

P/B=PBVPS,P/S=Market capSales,EVEBITDA=Market cap+Net debtEBITDAP/B=\frac{P}{BVPS},\quad P/S=\frac{\text{Market cap}}{\text{Sales}},\quad \frac{EV}{EBITDA}=\frac{\text{Market cap}+\text{Net debt}}{EBITDA}

P/B = Price / Book value per share; P/S = Market cap / Sales; EV/EBITDA = (Market cap + Net debt) / EBITDA; P/E = Price / EPS

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
PShare pricemoney
BVPSBook value per share, equity divided by sharesmoney
Market capShare price times shares outstandingmoney
Net debtDebt less cashmoney
EVEnterprise value, the price of the whole businessmoney

Worked example

A share trades at 45. There are 20 million shares, book equity is 520 million, sales are 1,500 million, EBITDA is 260 million, net debt is 450 million and net income is 90 million.

  1. Market cap = price x shares45 x 20= 900.00
  2. P/E = market cap / net income900 / 90= 10.00
  3. P/B = market cap / book equity900 / 520= 1.73
  4. P/S = market cap / sales900 / 1500= 0.60
  5. Enterprise value = market cap + net debt900+450= 1,350.00
  6. EV / EBITDA1350 / 260= 5.19

Market cap is 900.00. P/E is 10.00, P/B is 1.73 and P/S is 0.60. Enterprise value is 1,350.00, which is 5.19 EBITDA.
