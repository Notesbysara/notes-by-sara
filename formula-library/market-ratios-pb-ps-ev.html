Market Ratios: P/B, P/S and EV/EBITDA

BeginnerFinancial Statement AnalysisAlso known as: price to book, price to sales, EV to EBITDA, market capitalization, enterprise value
← Market Manipulation Screens: Order-to-Trade and Cancel RatiosMarket Value Added (MVA) →

Finds: how the market prices earnings, book value, sales and the whole business.

When to use it: Use it to compare what the market pays with what the company reports. P/E, EPS and dividend yield have their own entries. P/B suits asset-heavy firms and banks, P/S suits firms without profits, and EV/EBITDA compares firms with different debt.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

P/B\displaystyle P/B=PBVPS\displaystyle =\frac{P}{BVPS}P/S\displaystyle P/S=Market capSales\displaystyle =\frac{\text{Market cap}}{\text{Sales}}EVEBITDA\displaystyle \frac{EV}{EBITDA}=Market cap+Net debtEBITDA\displaystyle =\frac{\text{Market cap}+\text{Net debt}}{EBITDA}

In words: P/B = Price / Book value per share; P/S = Market cap / Sales; EV/EBITDA = (Market cap + Net debt) / EBITDA; P/E = Price / EPS

What each symbol means

PShare pricemoney
BVPSBook value per share, equity divided by sharesmoney
Market capShare price times shares outstandingmoney
Net debtDebt less cashmoney
EVEnterprise value, the price of the whole businessmoney

Worked example

A share trades at 45. There are 20 million shares, book equity is 520 million, sales are 1,500 million, EBITDA is 260 million, net debt is 450 million and net income is 90 million.

Market cap45 × 20900
P/E900 ÷ 9010
P/B900 ÷ 5201.73
P/S900 ÷ 1,5000.60
Enterprise value900 + 4501,350
EV / EBITDA1,350 ÷ 2605.19
The answer

Market cap is 900. P/E is 10, P/B is 1.73 and P/S is 0.60. Enterprise value is 1,350, which is 5.19 EBITDA.
