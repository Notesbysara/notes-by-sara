Credit Card Payoff Time and Interest

Beginner

Also known as: credit cards, minimum payment trap, card interest, months to pay off

How long a card balance lasts and what it costs. Use it to see the real cost of carrying a balance. If the payment is no more than one month of interest (i x B) the balance never falls. Paying only the minimum, which shrinks as the balance falls, takes far longer than a fixed payment. This assumes no new purchases and a constant rate; promotional rates change that.

The formula

n=−ln⁡(1−iBP)ln⁡(1+i),Pn=iB1−(1+i)−nn=\frac{-\ln\left(1-\dfrac{iB}{P}\right)}{\ln(1+i)},\quad P_{n}=\frac{iB}{1-(1+i)^{-n}}

Months to pay off = -ln(1 - i x Balance / Payment) / ln(1 + i), where i is the monthly rate (APR / 12); Payment needed to clear in n months = i x Balance / (1 - (1 + i)^(-n))

Worked example

A card has a balance of 5,000 at 22% APR. You pay a fixed 200 a month. How long will it take, how much interest will you pay, and what payment would clear it in 24 months?

  1. Monthly rate = APR / 120.22 / 12= 1.833%
  2. Months (exact) = -ln(1 - i B / P) / ln(1 + i)-ln(1-0.0183333 x 5000 / 200) / ln(1+0.0183333)= 33.75
  3. Months rounded up= 34
  4. Final payment(5000 x (1+0.0183333)^(34-1)-200 x ((1+0.0183333)^(34-1)-1) / 0.0183333) x (1+0.0183333)= 149.88
  5. Total paid = P x (m - 1) + final payment200 x (34-1)+last= 6,749.88
  6. Total interest = total paid - balance6749.88-5000= 1,749.88
  7. Payment to clear in the target months0.0183333 x 5000 / (1-(1+0.0183333)^(-24))= 259.39
  8. Interest at that payment259.391 x 24-5000= 1,225.38

With 200 a month it takes 34 months and you pay 1,749.88 in interest. To clear the balance in 24 months you would pay 259.39 a month, with 1,225.38 of interest.
