Credit Card Payoff Time and Interest

BeginnerPersonal FinanceAlso known as: credit cards, minimum payment trap, card interest, months to pay off
← Cox-Ingersoll-Ross Moments and Feller ConditionCredit Default Swap: Spread, Upfront and Payout →

Finds: how long a card balance lasts and what it costs.

When to use it: Use it to see the real cost of carrying a balance. If the payment is no more than one month of interest (i x B) the balance never falls. Paying only the minimum, which shrinks as the balance falls, takes far longer than a fixed payment. This assumes no new purchases and a constant rate; promotional rates change that.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

n\displaystyle n=−ln⁡(1−iBP)ln⁡(1+i)\displaystyle =\frac{-\ln\left(1-\dfrac{iB}{P}\right)}{\ln(1+i)}Pn\displaystyle P_{n}=iB1−(1+i)−n\displaystyle =\frac{iB}{1-(1+i)^{-n}}

In words: Months to pay off = -ln(1 − i x Balance / Payment) / ln(1 + i), where i is the monthly rate (APR / 12); Payment needed to clear in n months = i x Balance / (1 − (1 + i)^(-n))

What each symbol means

BCard balancemoney
iMonthly interest rate (APR / 12)per month
PFixed monthly paymentmoney
nNumber of monthsmonths

Worked example

A card has a balance of 5,000 at 22% APR. You pay a fixed 200 a month. How long will it take, how much interest will you pay, and what payment would clear it in 24 months?

Monthly rate0.22 ÷ 121.833%
Months (exact)−ln(1 − 0.0183333 × 5,000 ÷ 200) ÷ ln(1 + 0.0183333)33.75
Months rounded up34
Final payment(5,000 × (1 + 0.0183333)^(34 − 1) − 200 × ((1 + 0.0183333)^(34 − 1) − 1) ÷ 0.0183333) × (1 + 0.0183333)149.88
Total paid200 × (34 − 1) + last6,749.88
Total interest6,749.88 − 5,0001,749.88
Payment to clear in the target months0.0183333 × 5,000 ÷ (1 − (1 + 0.0183333)^(−24))259.39
Interest at that payment259.391 × 24 − 5,0001,225.38
The answer

With 200 a month it takes 34 months and you pay 1,749.88 in interest. To clear the balance in 24 months you would pay 259.39 a month, with 1,225.38 of interest.
