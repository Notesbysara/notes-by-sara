Enterprise Risk Aggregation with Correlation

Intermediate

Also known as: ERM, diversification of risk types, capital aggregation, correlation matrix of risks

Combined capital after diversification. Use it to combine capital for different risks into one figure. Simply adding them assumes all risks go wrong together. If risks are not perfectly correlated, the total is lower, and the gap is the diversification benefit. The correlations are the most uncertain input and rise in crises.

The formula

Ktotal=∑i∑jρijKiKjK_{total}=\sqrt{\sum_{i}\sum_{j}\rho_{ij}K_{i}K_{j}}

Total capital = square root of the sum over all pairs of (Correlation between risks i and j x Capital for risk i x Capital for risk j)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
K_iStand-alone capital for risk type i (market, credit, operational)money
rho_ijCorrelation between risk types i and j (1 on the diagonal)number
K_totalCapital for all risks togethermoney

Worked example

A firm holds stand-alone capital of 100 for market risk, 150 for credit risk and 60 for operational risk. The correlations are 0.5 (market and credit), 0.3 (market and operational) and 0.2 (credit and operational).

  1. Sum of the stand-alone capital= 310.0
  2. Total capital = sqrt of the sum of rho x Ki x Kj= 241.5
  3. Diversification benefit310-241.454= 68.5
  4. As a share of the simple sum68.5461 / 310= 22.1%

Simply adding the three gives 310.0. Allowing for correlation, the total is 241.5, a diversification benefit of 68.5, or 22.1%.
