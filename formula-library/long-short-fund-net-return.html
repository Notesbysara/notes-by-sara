Long/Short Hedge Fund: Exposure, Gross and Net Return

Intermediate

Also known as: hedge fund strategies, gross and net exposure, 2 and 20, long short equity return, hedge fund fees

Market exposure and the return left after fees. Use it to read what a long/short fund does and what it costs. Net exposure is the market bet; the gap between the long and short books is the manager's skill. Fees take a fixed slice plus a share of gains, so investors keep much less than the gross figure. This sketch has a one-year view and no high-water mark, so multi-year fees may differ.

The formula

Gross=wL+wS, Net=wL−wS,Rg=wLrL−wSrS+wSrreb,Rnet=Rg−f−pmax⁡(Rg−f−h,0)\text{Gross}=w_{L}+w_{S},\ \text{Net}=w_{L}-w_{S},\quad R_{g}=w_{L}r_{L}-w_{S}r_{S}+w_{S}r_{reb},\quad R_{net}=R_{g}-f-p\max(R_{g}-f-h,0)

Gross exposure = long weight + short weight; Net exposure = long weight - short weight; Gross return = long weight x long return - short weight x short stock return + short weight x interest on short proceeds; Fund return after management fee = gross - fee; Incentive fee = share x amount above the hurdle; Net return = after management fee - incentive fee

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
w_L, w_SLong and short positions as a share of investor capitalpercent
r_L, r_SReturn of the long stocks and of the shorted stockspercent
r_rebInterest earned on the cash from short sales (the rebate)percent
f, p, hManagement fee, incentive fee share, and hurdle return before an incentive fee is paidpercent

Worked example

A fund is 130% long and 50% short. Longs rose 12% and the shorted stocks rose 6%. Cash from short sales earns 3%. The fee is 2% plus 20% of returns above a 4% hurdle.

  1. Gross exposure1.3+0.5= 180%
  2. Net exposure1.3-0.5= 80%
  3. Gross return1.3 x 0.12-0.5 x 0.06+0.5 x 0.03= 14.10%
  4. Return after the management fee0.141-0.02= 12.10%
  5. Incentive fee0.2 x max(0.121-0.04, 0)= 1.62%
  6. Net return to the investor0.121-0.0162= 10.48%
  7. Fee drag as a share of gross return(0.141-0.1048) / 0.141= 25.7%

Gross exposure is 180% and net exposure is 80%. The gross return is 14.10%. After the management fee it is 12.10%, the incentive fee is 1.62% and the investor receives 10.48%. Fees took 25.7% of the gross return.
