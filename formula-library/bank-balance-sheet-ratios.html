Bank Balance Sheet Ratios

Beginner

Also known as: bank balance sheet structure, loan to deposit ratio, leverage, funding mix

How a bank is funded and how thin its buffer is. Use it to judge funding and liquidity. A loan-to-deposit ratio well above 100% means the bank relies on wholesale funding, which is less stable than deposits. A thin equity ratio means a small fall in asset values can wipe out equity. Compare ratios across banks of the same type.

The formula

LDR=LoansDeposits,Equity ratio=EA,Assets=Deposits+Wholesale funding+ELDR=\frac{\text{Loans}}{\text{Deposits}},\quad \text{Equity ratio}=\frac{E}{A},\quad \text{Assets}=\text{Deposits}+\text{Wholesale funding}+E

Loan-to-deposit ratio = loans / deposits; Equity ratio = equity / assets; Deposit funding share = deposits / total funding; The balance sheet balances: assets = deposits + wholesale funding + equity

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
Loans, securities, cashThe main asset groups, from least liquid (loans) to most liquid (cash)money
Wholesale fundingBorrowing from markets and other banks, which can disappear quickly in stressmoney
EShareholders' equity: the buffer that absorbs lossesmoney

Worked example

A bank has loans 5,200, securities 1,800, cash 600 and other assets 400. It funds these with deposits 6,600, wholesale funding 1,000 and equity 400.

  1. Total assets5200+1800+600+400= 8,000.00
  2. Loan-to-deposit ratio5200 / 6600= 78.8%
  3. Loans / assets5200 / 8000= 65.0%
  4. Liquid assets / assets(600+1800) / 8000= 30.0%
  5. Equity / assets400 / 8000= 5.00%
  6. Leverage = assets / equity8000 / 400= 20.0
  7. Deposits / total funding6600 / (6600+1000+400)= 82.5%
  8. Balance check = assets - (deposits + wholesale + equity)8000-(6600+1000+400)= 0.00

Total assets are 8,000.00. The loan-to-deposit ratio is 78.8%, loans are 65.0% of assets, and liquid assets (cash and securities) are 30.0%. Equity is 5.00% of assets, a leverage of 20.0, and deposits provide 82.5% of funding. The balance check is 0.00 (0 means it balances).
