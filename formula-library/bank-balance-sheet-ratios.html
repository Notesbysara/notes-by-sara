Bank Balance Sheet Ratios

BeginnerBanking & Financial InstitutionsAlso known as: bank balance sheet structure, loan to deposit ratio, leverage, funding mix
← Balance of Payments IdentityBank Profitability: Income Statement, ROA and ROE →

Finds: how a bank is funded and how thin its buffer is.

When to use it: Use it to judge funding and liquidity. A loan-to-deposit ratio well above 100% means the bank relies on wholesale funding, which is less stable than deposits. A thin equity ratio means a small fall in asset values can wipe out equity. Compare ratios across banks of the same type.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

LDR\displaystyle LDR=LoansDeposits\displaystyle =\frac{\text{Loans}}{\text{Deposits}}Equity ratio\displaystyle \text{Equity ratio}=EA\displaystyle =\frac{E}{A}Assets\displaystyle \text{Assets}=Deposits+Wholesale funding+E\displaystyle =\text{Deposits}+\text{Wholesale funding}+E

In words: Loan-to-deposit ratio = loans / deposits; Equity ratio = equity / assets; Deposit funding share = deposits / total funding; The balance sheet balances: assets = deposits + wholesale funding + equity

What each symbol means

Loans, securities, cashThe main asset groups, from least liquid (loans) to most liquid (cash)money
Wholesale fundingBorrowing from markets and other banks, which can disappear quickly in stressmoney
EShareholders' equity: the buffer that absorbs lossesmoney

Worked example

A bank has loans 5,200, securities 1,800, cash 600 and other assets 400. It funds these with deposits 6,600, wholesale funding 1,000 and equity 400.

Total assets5,200 + 1,800 + 600 + 4008,000
Loan-to-deposit ratio5,200 ÷ 6,60078.8%
Loans / assets5,200 ÷ 8,00065%
Liquid assets / assets(600 + 1,800) ÷ 8,00030%
Equity / assets400 ÷ 8,0005%
Leverage8,000 ÷ 40020
Deposits / total funding6,600 ÷ (6,600 + 1,000 + 400)82.5%
Balance check8,000 − (6,600 + 1,000 + 400)0
The answer

Total assets are 8,000. The loan-to-deposit ratio is 78.8%, loans are 65.0% of assets, and liquid assets (cash and securities) are 30.0%. Equity is 5% of assets, a leverage of 20.0, and deposits provide 82.5% of funding. The balance check is 0 (0 means it balances).
