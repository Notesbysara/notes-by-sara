Hedging a Foreign Currency Receivable

Intermediate

Also known as: currency risk for multinationals, transaction exposure, forward hedge vs money market hedge, natural hedge

What a hedge locks in compared with leaving the exposure open. Use it to compare ways to lock in the value of a foreign receivable. A forward is simplest. A money market hedge borrows the foreign currency now, so the receivable repays the loan, and gives almost the same result when parity holds. The two differ if the quoted forward is off parity or if borrowing costs differ. Staying unhedged leaves the outcome to the future spot rate. Costs and credit limits can shape the choice.

The formula

USDMM=FC1+rfT S (1+rdT),USDfwd=FC×FUSD_{MM}=\frac{FC}{1+r_{f}T}\,S\,(1+r_{d}T),\quad USD_{fwd}=FC\times F

Forward hedge: dollars received = foreign amount x forward rate. Money market hedge: borrow the present value of the receivable in foreign currency (amount / (1 + foreign rate x T)), convert it at spot, and invest the dollars at the domestic rate; dollars at the due date = that amount x (1 + domestic rate x T). Unhedged: dollars = foreign amount x the spot rate at the due date

Worked example

A US company will receive 1,000,000 euros in 6 months. Spot is 1.0850 USD per euro, the 6-month forward is 1.0950, the euro rate is 3% and the dollar rate 5%.

  1. Forward hedge: FC x F1,000,000 x 1.095= 1,095,000.00
  2. Foreign currency to borrow now = FC / (1 + rf T)1,000,000 / (1+0.03 x 0.5)= 985,221.67
  3. Money market hedge: borrow, convert at spot, invest in USD985222 x 1.085 x (1+0.05 x 0.5)= 1,095,689.66
  4. Forward minus money market hedge1,095,000-1,095,690= -689.66
  5. Unhedged, low scenario1,000,000 x 1.05= 1,050,000.00
  6. Unhedged, high scenario1,000,000 x 1.15= 1,150,000.00
  7. Range of unhedged outcomes1,150,000-1,050,000= 100,000.00

The forward hedge locks in 1,095,000.00. The money market hedge gives 1,095,689.66, a difference of -689.66. Unhedged, the receipt would be 1,050,000.00 if the euro falls to 1.05 and 1,150,000.00 if it rises to 1.15. The hedges remove that range of 100,000.00.
