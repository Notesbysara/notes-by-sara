Skewness

Intermediate

Also known as: asymmetry of returns, third moment, fat tail direction

Whether returns are lopsided. Use it to see whether big moves tend to be gains or losses. Negative skewness means occasional large losses, which a standard deviation alone would hide.

The formula

Skew=1n∑i=1n(Ri−Rˉσ)3\text{Skew}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(\frac{R_{i}-\bar{R}}{\sigma}\right)^{3}

Skewness = average of ((Ri - mean) / standard deviation)^3

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
SkewSkewness, a measure of how lopsided the returns arenumber
R_iReturn in period ipercent
R-barMean returnpercent

Worked example

A fund returned 10%, -5%, 15%, 2%, -12% and 8% over six periods. What is the skewness of its returns?

  1. Skewness = average of the cubed standardized returns= -0.362

The skewness is -0.362. It is negative, so the bigger moves lean toward losses, though only mildly.
