Yield to Maturity (YTM)

IntermediateFixed IncomeAlso known as: bond yield, internal rate of return of a bond, promised yield, effective yield
← Yield Curve Slope and CurvatureZ-Score →

Finds: the return from buying a bond and holding it to maturity.

When to use it: Use it to find the return you earn if you buy at the current price, hold to maturity, and reinvest every coupon at the same yield. It has no direct formula, so it is found by trial and error, which the calculator does for you.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

P\displaystyle P=∑k=1NC(1+y/m)k+F(1+y/m)N solved for y\displaystyle =\sum_{k=1}^{N}\frac{C}{(1+y/m)^{k}}+\frac{F}{(1+y/m)^{N}}\ \text{solved for }y

In words: Find the yield y that makes the present value of all coupons and the face value equal to the price. Approximation: y = [C + (F − P) / n] / [(F + P) / 2]

What each symbol means

yYield to maturity, annual, quoted as a nominal rateper year
PPrice paid for the bondmoney

FFace valuemoney
nYears to maturityyears

Worked example

A bond with a face value of 1,000, a 5% coupon paid twice a year and 10 years to maturity costs 950. What is its yield to maturity?

Yield to maturity (solved by trial and error)5.662%
Effective annual yield(1 + 0.0566169 ÷ 2)^2 − 15.742%
Approximation(1,000 × 0.05 + (1,000 − 950) ÷ 10) ÷ ((1,000 + 950) ÷ 2)5.641%
The answer

The yield to maturity is 5.662%, quoted as an annual rate. The effective annual yield is 5.742%. The quick approximation gives 5.641%, which is close.
