Rights Issue: Ex-Rights Price and Value of a Right

Intermediate

Also known as: TERP, theoretical ex-rights price, value of a right, rights offering

Share price after a rights issue. Use it to see what a rights issue does to the share price and what a right is worth. Shareholders who neither buy nor sell their rights lose out, so rights are usually sold or exercised.

The formula

TERP=NP+nSN+n,Value of a right=P−TERPTERP=\frac{N P+n S}{N+n},\quad \text{Value of a right}=P-TERP

TERP = (N x P + n x S) / (N + n), and Value of a right = P - TERP

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
TERPTheoretical ex-rights price, the expected price after the issuemoney
PPrice before the rights issuemoney
SSubscription price of the new sharesmoney
NOld shares needed for each rights bundleshares
nNew shares offered per bundleshares

Worked example

Shares trade at 10. The company offers 1 new share at 8 for every 4 shares held. What is the ex-rights price and the value of one right?

  1. Value of the bundle = N x P + n x S4 x 10+1 x 8= 48.00
  2. TERP = bundle value / (N + n)48 / (4+1)= 9.60
  3. Value of a right = P - TERP10-9.6= 0.40

The theoretical ex-rights price is 9.60. Each right is worth 0.40, which is the amount by which the price falls.
