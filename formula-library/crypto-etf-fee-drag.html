Crypto ETF vs Direct Holding: Fee Drag and Cost

Beginner

Also known as: Bitcoin and crypto ETFs, spot bitcoin ETF, expense ratio, self custody cost, tracking

Which route is cheaper over time. Use it to compare buying a spot crypto ETF with holding the coin yourself. The ETF fee compounds against you every year but removes the need for wallets and keys, and it can be held in ordinary brokerage and retirement accounts. The right choice depends on size, time held and how much you value the convenience.

The formula

VETF=V0 ∏(1+rt−f),Drag=Vspot−VETFVspotV_{ETF}=V_{0}\,\prod(1+r_{t}-f),\quad \text{Drag}=\frac{V_{spot}-V_{ETF}}{V_{spot}}

Value of an ETF after n years = start value x (1 + coin return - fee) multiplied over the years; Value of holding the coin directly = start value x (1 + coin return) multiplied over the years, less custody and trading costs paid; Fee drag = (direct value - ETF value) / direct value; Break-even yearly cost of self-custody = ETF fee

Worked example

You invest 10,000 for 5 years. The coin returns 20% a year. The ETF fee is 0.25% a year. Direct holding costs 0.10% a year in custody plus a 0.50% one-off trading cost to buy.

  1. ETF value10000 x (1+0.2-0.0025)^5= 24,625.08
  2. Direct holding value10000 x (1-0.005) x (1+0.2-0.001)^5= 24,655.79
  3. ETF value minus direct value24625.1-24655.8= -30.72
  4. Sign (1 = ETF worth more, 0 = less)1 if -30.7164>0 else 0= 0
  5. Fee drag of the ETF vs a feeless coin1-24625.1 / (10000 x (1+0.2)^5)= 1.04%

After 5 years the ETF is worth 24,625.08 and direct holding is worth 24,655.79. The ETF is -30.72 0 (positive = ETF worth more, negative = less). Fee drag on the ETF compared with a feeless coin is 1.04%.
