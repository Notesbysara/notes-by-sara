Murabaha Mark-Up and Implied Rate

Intermediate

Also known as: Islamic banking, cost-plus financing, profit rate, sale on deferred payment

The all-in price and the comparable annual rate of a cost-plus sale. Use it to compare an Islamic cost-plus sale with a conventional loan. The bank buys the asset and sells it to the customer at cost plus an agreed mark-up, payable in installments, so the price is fixed and does not accrue interest on a loan. The implied rate is a comparison tool only: the two contracts are structured differently and differ in legal treatment, risk and late-payment rules.

The formula

Price=C(1+m),PMT=Pricen,C=PMT 1−(1+i)−ni\text{Price}=C(1+m),\quad PMT=\frac{\text{Price}}{n},\quad C=PMT\,\frac{1-(1+i)^{-n}}{i}

Sale price = cost x (1 + mark-up); Installment = sale price / number of installments; Implied periodic rate i solves: cost = installment x (1 - (1 + i)^(-n)) / i; Effective annual rate = (1 + i)^12 - 1 for monthly installments

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
CCost the bank pays for the assetmoney
mAgreed mark-up (profit) over cost, disclosed in advancepercent
nNumber of equal monthly installmentsmonths
iMonthly rate implied by the installments, for comparison with a conventional loanper month

Worked example

A bank buys equipment for 100,000 and sells it for a 12% mark-up payable in 36 equal monthly installments.

  1. Sale price = cost x (1 + mark-up)100000 x (1+0.12)= 112,000.00
  2. Monthly installment112000 / 36= 3,111.11
  3. Implied monthly rate (IRR of the cash flows)= 0.6259%
  4. Effective annual rate(1+0.00625888)^12-1= 7.77%
  5. Flat annual rate of the mark-up = mark-up / years0.12 / (36 / 12)= 4.00%

The sale price is 112,000.00 and each installment is 3,111.11. Solving for the rate that links the cost to the installments gives 0.6259% a month, or 7.77% as an effective annual rate. The flat mark-up of 12% over 3 years looks like 4% a year, but the true annual cost is higher because the balance falls with each payment.
