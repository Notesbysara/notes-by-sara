Cash Runway

Beginner

Also known as: burn rate, months of cash, treasury liquidity planning, runway

Months before the cash runs out. Use it to plan liquidity, especially for startups and firms that spend more than they earn. A short runway means funding must be raised soon, or costs cut.

The formula

Runway=CashMonthly burn\text{Runway}=\frac{\text{Cash}}{\text{Monthly burn}}

Runway (months) = Cash balance / Net monthly cash burn

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
CashCash and easily sold investments on handmoney
Monthly burnNet cash the company spends each month (cash out minus cash in)money per month
RunwayMonths the company can keep operating with no new fundingmonths

Worked example

A company has 2,400,000 in cash and burns 300,000 a month. How long can it keep going, and what if it cuts its burn to 240,000?

  1. Runway = cash / burn2,400,000 / 300000= 8.0
  2. Runway with the reduced burn2,400,000 / 240000= 10.0

At the current burn the company has 8.0 months of cash. If it cuts the burn, the runway rises to 10.0 months.
