Margin Call Price

Intermediate

Also known as: maintenance margin, margin call, liquidation price

The price that triggers a margin call. Use it to know how far a stock can fall before a margin call on a long position. Your equity is the position value minus the loan, and the call comes when equity falls to the maintenance share of the position value.

The formula

P∗=P0 1−mi1−mmP^{*}=P_{0}\,\frac{1-m_{i}}{1-m_{m}}

Margin call price = Start price x (1 - Initial margin) / (1 - Maintenance margin)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
P*Price at which the broker asks for more moneymoney
P_0Price you paidmoney
m_iInitial margin, the share of the purchase paid with your own moneypercent
m_mMaintenance margin, the lowest share of the position's value that must be your own equitypercent

Worked example

You buy a stock at 50 with an initial margin of 50%. The maintenance margin is 30%. At what price will you get a margin call?

  1. Call price = P0 x (1 - initial) / (1 - maintenance)50 x (1-0.5) / (1-0.3)= 35.71
  2. Fall from the purchase price = 1 - call price / P01-35.7143 / 50= 28.57%

A margin call comes if the price falls to 35.71, a fall of 28.57% from the purchase price.
