Letter of Credit and Discounting: All-In Cost

BeginnerInternational FinanceAlso known as: trade finance, letter of credit fees, banker's acceptance, receivables discounting
← Lease Liability and First-Year Expense (IFRS 16 / ASC 842)Life Insurance Needs Analysis →

Finds: what early payment under a letter of credit really costs.

When to use it: Use it to see what a seller pays to be paid early on an export under a letter of credit. The bank pays the seller now, less a discount, and collects the buyer's bank payment later. A bank-quoted discount rate understates the true cost, since it applies to face value and not to the amount actually advanced, and fees add to it. Compare all-in rates, not quoted rates.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Discount\displaystyle \text{Discount}=F d t360\displaystyle =F\,d\,\frac{t}{360}All-in rate\displaystyle \text{All-in rate}=CostsF−Costs×365t\displaystyle =\frac{\text{Costs}}{F-\text{Costs}}\times\frac{365}{t}

In words: Discount = face value x discount rate x days / 360; Total cost = discount + issuance fee (% of face) + confirmation fee (% a year x days / 360 x face); Net proceeds = face − total cost; All-in annualized cost = total cost / net proceeds x 365 / days

What each symbol means

FFace value of the letter of credit or acceptancemoney
dBank discount rate (quoted on face value, 360-day year)per year
tDays until payment (usance period)days
feesIssuance fee and confirmation feepercent

Worked example

A seller discounts a 90-day, 500,000 letter of credit at a 6% bank discount rate, with a 0.75% issuance fee and a confirmation fee of 0.30% a year.

Discount500,000 × 0.06 × 90 ÷ 3607,500
Issuance and confirmation fees500,000 × 0.0075 + 500,000 × 0.003 × 90 ÷ 3604,125
Total cost7,500 + 4,12511,625
Net proceeds500,000 − 11,625488,375
All-in annualized cost11,625 ÷ 488,375 × 365 ÷ 909.65%
The answer

The discount is 7,500. Adding the issuance fee and confirmation fee brings total costs to 11,625, so the seller receives 488,375. The all-in cost is 9.65% annualized, against the quoted 6%.
