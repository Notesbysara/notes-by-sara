DuPont Analysis (3-Step and 5-Step)

Intermediate

Also known as: DuPont identity, ROE decomposition, tax burden, interest burden

What drives ROE: margin, efficiency or leverage. Use it to find out why ROE is high or low. It splits ROE into profitability, efficiency and leverage. Two companies with the same ROE can be very different: one a high-margin business and the other a thin-margin business using lots of debt.

The formula

ROE=NIS×STA×TAE=NIEBT×EBTEBIT×EBITS×STA×TAEROE=\frac{NI}{S}\times\frac{S}{TA}\times\frac{TA}{E}=\frac{NI}{EBT}\times\frac{EBT}{EBIT}\times\frac{EBIT}{S}\times\frac{S}{TA}\times\frac{TA}{E}

ROE = Net margin x Asset turnover x Equity multiplier. Five-step: ROE = Tax burden x Interest burden x EBIT margin x Asset turnover x Equity multiplier

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
NI/SNet profit marginpercent
S/TAAsset turnover, sales per unit of assetstimes
TA/EEquity multiplier, assets per unit of equity (leverage)times
NI/EBTTax burden, the share of pre-tax profit kept after taxpercent
EBT/EBITInterest burden, the share of operating profit left after interestpercent

Worked example

A company has net income of 90, pre-tax profit of 120, EBIT of 160, sales of 1,500, average assets of 1,200 and average equity of 500.

  1. Net margin = NI / sales90 / 1500= 6.00%
  2. Asset turnover = sales / assets1500 / 1200= 1.25
  3. Equity multiplier = assets / equity1200 / 500= 2.40
  4. ROE = net margin x turnover x multiplier0.06 x 1.25 x 2.4= 18.00%
  5. Tax burden = NI / EBT90 / 120= 75.00%
  6. Interest burden = EBT / EBIT120 / 160= 75.00%
  7. EBIT margin = EBIT / sales160 / 1500= 10.67%
  8. ROE from five steps0.75 x 0.75 x 0.106667 x 1.25 x 2.4= 18.00%

ROE is 18.00%. Net margin is 6.00%, asset turnover is 1.25 and the equity multiplier is 2.40. In the five-step version the tax burden is 75.00%, the interest burden 75.00% and the EBIT margin 10.67%. Their product also gives 18.00%. Leverage is doing a lot of the work: the company earns 6 cents on each unit of sales.
