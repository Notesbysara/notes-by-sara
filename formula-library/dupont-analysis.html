DuPont Analysis (3-Step and 5-Step)

IntermediateFinancial Statement AnalysisAlso known as: DuPont identity, ROE decomposition, tax burden, interest burden
← Dual-Class Shares: Voting Power vs. OwnershipDuration Gap and Equity Sensitivity →

Finds: what drives ROE: margin, efficiency or leverage.

When to use it: Use it to find out why ROE is high or low. It splits ROE into profitability, efficiency and leverage. Two companies with the same ROE can be very different: one a high-margin business and the other a thin-margin business using lots of debt.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

ROE\displaystyle ROE=NIS×STA×TAE=NIEBT×EBTEBIT×EBITS×STA×TAE\displaystyle =\frac{NI}{S}\times\frac{S}{TA}\times\frac{TA}{E}=\frac{NI}{EBT}\times\frac{EBT}{EBIT}\times\frac{EBIT}{S}\times\frac{S}{TA}\times\frac{TA}{E}

In words: ROE = Net margin x Asset turnover x Equity multiplier. Five-step: ROE = Tax burden x Interest burden x EBIT margin x Asset turnover x Equity multiplier

What each symbol means

NI/SNet profit marginpercent
S/TAAsset turnover, sales per unit of assetstimes
TA/EEquity multiplier, assets per unit of equity (leverage)times
NI/EBTTax burden, the share of pre-tax profit kept after taxpercent
EBT/EBITInterest burden, the share of operating profit left after interestpercent

Worked example

A company has net income of 90, pre-tax profit of 120, EBIT of 160, sales of 1,500, average assets of 1,200 and average equity of 500.

Net margin90 ÷ 1,5006%
Asset turnover1,500 ÷ 1,2001.25
Equity multiplier1,200 ÷ 5002.40
ROE0.06 × 1.25 × 2.418%
Tax burden90 ÷ 12075%
Interest burden120 ÷ 16075%
EBIT margin160 ÷ 1,50010.67%
ROE from five steps0.75 × 0.75 × 0.106667 × 1.25 × 2.418%
The answer

ROE is 18%. Net margin is 6%, asset turnover is 1.25 and the equity multiplier is 2.40. In the five-step version the tax burden is 75%, the interest burden 75% and the EBIT margin 10.67%. Their product also gives 18%. Leverage is doing a lot of the work: the company earns 6 cents on each unit of sales.
