CDO Equity Tranche Return

IntermediateFixed IncomeAlso known as: collateralized debt obligation, arbitrage CDO, equity tranche, leverage in structured credit
← Catastrophe Bond: Expected Loss and Risk MultipleCentral Counterparty Initial Margin (Value-at-Risk Method) →

Finds: the equity return of a collateralized debt obligation.

When to use it: Use it to see how a CDO magnifies returns for its equity holders. The equity tranche borrows cheaply from the debt tranches, so it keeps the spread between the asset yield and the liability costs, and also takes the first losses. That makes it high-return and high-risk.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Requity\displaystyle R_{equity}=A y−∑Li ki−f A−LossesE\displaystyle =\frac{A\,y-\sum L_{i}\,k_{i}-f\,A-\text{Losses}}{E}

In words: Equity return = (Interest earned on the assets − Interest paid on each debt tranche − Fees − Credit losses) / Equity invested

What each symbol means

ATotal assets in the poolmoney
yAverage yield on the poolper year
L_i, k_iSize and interest rate of each debt tranchemoney
fAnnual fees as a share of assetsper year
EEquity tranche invested, the first-loss piecemoney

Worked example

A CDO holds assets of 100 yielding 7.0%. It issues 75 of senior notes at 4.5% and 15 of mezzanine notes at 6.0%, leaving 10 of equity. Fees are 0.4% of assets. What is the equity return with no losses, and with 1% of assets lost?

Equity100 − 75 − 1510
Income100 × 0.077.000
Interest on notes75 × 0.045 + 15 × 0.064.275
Income after interest and fees7 − 4.275 − 0.004 × 1002.325
Equity return with no losses2.325 ÷ 1023.25%
Credit losses100 × 0.011
Equity return after losses(2.325 − 1) ÷ 1013.25%
The answer

Income after costs is 2.325, a return of 23.25% on the equity of 10. With credit losses of 1 the equity return falls to 13.25%. A loss of just 1% of the assets cuts the equity return by about a third.
