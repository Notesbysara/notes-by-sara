Annual Report Scorecard

Intermediate

Also known as: analyze a public company annual report, ratio dashboard, profitability, leverage, cash conversion, company health check

A quick read of profit, leverage and cash. Use it as the first pass through an annual report. Pull seven numbers from the statements, compute the ratios, then read the notes to find out why they are what they are. No ratio stands alone; compare each with the company's earlier years and with its peers.

The formula

ROE=NIE,Interest cover=EBITInterest,FCF=CFO−CapEx,Conversion=FCFNIROE=\frac{NI}{E},\quad \text{Interest cover}=\frac{EBIT}{\text{Interest}},\quad FCF=CFO-CapEx,\quad \text{Conversion}=\frac{FCF}{NI}

Operating margin = operating profit / revenue; Net margin = net income / revenue; ROE = net income / equity; ROA = net income / assets; Net debt / EBITDA is common but needs depreciation, so net debt / operating profit is used here; Net debt = debt - cash; Interest cover = operating profit / interest; Free cash flow = operating cash flow - capital spending; Cash conversion = free cash flow / net income

Worked example

A company reports revenue 8,200, operating profit 980, net income 610, total assets 9,400, equity 3,900, debt 2,800, cash 720, operating cash flow 1,150, capital spending 540 and interest 160.

  1. Operating margin980 / 8200= 12.0%
  2. Net margin610 / 8200= 7.4%
  3. Return on equity610 / 3900= 15.6%
  4. Return on assets610 / 9400= 6.5%
  5. Net debt2800-720= 2,080.00
  6. Net debt / operating profit2080 / 980= 2.12
  7. Interest cover980 / 160= 6.1
  8. Free cash flow1150-540= 610.00
  9. Cash conversion610 / 610= 100%

Operating margin is 12.0% and net margin 7.4%. Return on equity is 15.6% and on assets 6.5%. Net debt is 2,080.00, or 2.12 times operating profit, and interest is covered 6.1 times. Free cash flow is 610.00, which is 100% of net income.
