Effective Spread and Price Impact

Intermediate

Also known as: realized spread, market microstructure, trade execution quality, adverse selection

The true cost of a trade and who kept it. Use it to measure what a trade really cost, since many trades happen inside or outside the quoted spread. The realized spread is what the liquidity provider keeps. The rest is the price impact, the permanent move caused by the information in the trade.

The formula

ES=2 D (P−M),RS=2 D (P−M+5)ES=2\,D\,(P-M),\quad RS=2\,D\,(P-M_{+5})

Effective spread = 2 x D x (Trade price - Mid price before the trade); Realized spread uses the mid price some minutes later; D = +1 for a buy and -1 for a sell

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
D+1 for a buyer-initiated trade, -1 for a seller-initiated tradenumber
PPrice at which the trade happenedmoney
MMid price just before the trademoney
M+5Mid price a few minutes after the trademoney

Worked example

A buy order executes at 50.04. The mid price was 50.00 before the trade and 50.03 five minutes later. What are the effective and realized spreads?

  1. Effective spread = 2 x D x (P - M)2 x 1 x (50.04-50)= 0.0800
  2. Realized spread = 2 x D x (P - M5)2 x 1 x (50.04-50.03)= 0.0200
  3. Price impact = effective - realized0.08-0.02= 0.0600

The effective spread is 0.0800. The realized spread is 0.0200, so the liquidity provider kept little, and 0.0600 of the cost was price impact, the market moving against the provider.
