Dividend Payout Ratio

BeginnerCorporate FinanceAlso known as: payout ratio, retention ratio, plowback ratio, dividend cover
← Dividend Discount Model (Gordon Growth)Dividend Reinvestment (DRIP) →

Finds: how much profit is paid out versus kept.

When to use it: Use it to see how much of its profit a company pays out and how much it keeps to reinvest. Mature firms tend to pay out more and growth firms less.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Payout\displaystyle \text{Payout}=DividendsNet income\displaystyle =\frac{\text{Dividends}}{\text{Net income}}b\displaystyle b=1−Payout\displaystyle =1-\text{Payout}

In words: Payout ratio = Dividends / Net income, and Retention ratio b = 1 − Payout ratio

What each symbol means

PayoutShare of profit paid to shareholderspercent
DividendsTotal dividends paidmoney
Net incomeProfit for the periodmoney
bRetention ratio, the share of profit kept in the businesspercent

Worked example

A company earned 800 and paid dividends of 300. What are its payout and retention ratios?

Payout ratio300 ÷ 80037.50%
Retention ratio1 − 0.37562.50%
The answer

The payout ratio is 37.50% and the retention ratio is 62.50%.
