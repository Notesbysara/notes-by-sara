Beneish M-Score

IntermediateFinancial Statement AnalysisAlso known as: earnings manipulation, accounting fraud warning signs, fraud detection model
← Bayes' TheoremBest Execution: Price Improvement and Execution Cost →

Finds: A flag for possible earnings manipulation.

When to use it: Use it as a screen for earnings manipulation. Eight ratios, mostly comparing this year with last year, are combined into one score. It is a statistical flag, not proof of fraud, and gives false alarms, especially for fast-growing firms. Use it to decide where to look harder.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

M\displaystyle M=−4.84+0.920 DSRI+0.528 GMI+0.404 AQI+0.892 SGI+0.115 DEPI−0.172 SGAI+4.679 TATA−0.327 LVGI\displaystyle =-4.84+0.920\,DSRI+0.528\,GMI+0.404\,AQI+0.892\,SGI+0.115\,DEPI-0.172\,SGAI+4.679\,TATA-0.327\,LVGI

In words: M = −4.84 + 0.920 x DSRI + 0.528 x GMI + 0.404 x AQI + 0.892 x SGI + 0.115 x DEPI − 0.172 x SGAI + 4.679 x TATA − 0.327 x LVGI. A score above about −1.78 flags a likely manipulator

What each symbol means

DSRIDays sales in receivables index: this year's DSO / last year'snumber
GMIGross margin index: last year's gross margin / this year'snumber
AQIAsset quality index: share of non-current assets other than PP&E, this year / last yearnumber
SGISales growth index: sales this year / last yearnumber
DEPIDepreciation index: depreciation rate last year / this yearnumber
SGAISG&A expense index: SG&A to sales, this year / last yearnumber
TATATotal accruals to total assets: (Income from continuing operations - CFO) / total assetsnumber
LVGILeverage index: (debt / assets) this year / last yearnumber

Worked example

A company's indexes are DSRI 1.20, GMI 1.05, AQI 1.10, SGI 1.25, DEPI 1, SGAI 0.95, TATA 0.03 and LVGI 1.05.

M−4.84 + 0.920 × 1.2 + 0.528 × 1.05 + 0.404 × 1.1 + 0.892 × 1.25 + 0.115 × 1 − 0.172 × 0.95 + 4.679 × 0.03 − 0.327 × 1.05−1.87
Distance below (negative) or above (positive) the -1.78 cut-off−1.87358 + 1.78−0.09
The answer

The M-score is −1.87. The usual cut-off is −1.78, and this score is on the borderline, so the company is not clearly flagged, but receivables growing faster than sales (DSRI) and strong sales growth (SGI) are the items to look into first.
