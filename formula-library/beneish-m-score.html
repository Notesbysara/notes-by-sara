Beneish M-Score

Intermediate

Also known as: earnings manipulation, accounting fraud warning signs, fraud detection model

A flag for possible earnings manipulation. Use it as a screen for earnings manipulation. Eight ratios, mostly comparing this year with last year, are combined into one score. It is a statistical flag, not proof of fraud, and gives false alarms, especially for fast-growing firms. Use it to decide where to look harder.

The formula

M=−4.84+0.920 DSRI+0.528 GMI+0.404 AQI+0.892 SGI+0.115 DEPI−0.172 SGAI+4.679 TATA−0.327 LVGIM=-4.84+0.920\,DSRI+0.528\,GMI+0.404\,AQI+0.892\,SGI+0.115\,DEPI-0.172\,SGAI+4.679\,TATA-0.327\,LVGI

M = -4.84 + 0.920 x DSRI + 0.528 x GMI + 0.404 x AQI + 0.892 x SGI + 0.115 x DEPI - 0.172 x SGAI + 4.679 x TATA - 0.327 x LVGI. A score above about -1.78 flags a likely manipulator

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
DSRIDays sales in receivables index: this year's DSO / last year'snumber
GMIGross margin index: last year's gross margin / this year'snumber
AQIAsset quality index: share of non-current assets other than PP&E, this year / last yearnumber
SGISales growth index: sales this year / last yearnumber
DEPIDepreciation index: depreciation rate last year / this yearnumber
SGAISG&A expense index: SG&A to sales, this year / last yearnumber
TATATotal accruals to total assets: (Income from continuing operations - CFO) / total assetsnumber
LVGILeverage index: (debt / assets) this year / last yearnumber

Worked example

A company's indexes are DSRI 1.20, GMI 1.05, AQI 1.10, SGI 1.25, DEPI 1.00, SGAI 0.95, TATA 0.03 and LVGI 1.05.

  1. M = -4.84 + 0.920 DSRI + 0.528 GMI + 0.404 AQI + 0.892 SGI + 0.115 DEPI - 0.172 SGAI + 4.679 TATA - 0.327 LVGI-4.84+0.920 x 1.2+0.528 x 1.05+0.404 x 1.1+0.892 x 1.25+0.115 x 1-0.172 x 0.95+4.679 x 0.03-0.327 x 1.05= -1.87
  2. Distance below (negative) or above (positive) the -1.78 cut-off-1.87358+1.78= -0.09

The M-score is -1.87. The usual cut-off is -1.78, and this score is on the borderline, so the company is not clearly flagged, but receivables growing faster than sales (DSRI) and strong sales growth (SGI) are the items to look into first.
