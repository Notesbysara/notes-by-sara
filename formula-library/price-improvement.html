Price Improvement (Order Flow)

Beginner

Also known as: payment for order flow, PFOF, NBBO, retail execution

How much better than the best quote your order filled. Use it to judge the quality of a broker's execution. A wholesaler that pays for retail orders must usually fill them at or better than the best public quote, and the gain is price improvement.

The formula

PI=(PNBBO−Pexec)×Q (for a buy)PI=(P_{NBBO}-P_{exec})\times Q\ \text{(for a buy)}

Price improvement = (Best quoted price - Price you got) x Shares; for a sell it is (Price you got - Best quoted price) x Shares

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
P_NBBOBest quoted price across exchanges: the lowest ask when buying, the highest bid when sellingmoney
P_execPrice at which your order actually executedmoney
QNumber of sharesnumber

Worked example

You buy 300 shares. The best ask across exchanges is 20.02, but your order fills at 20.015. What did you save?

  1. Saving per share = quote - execution20.02-20.015= 0.0050
  2. Total price improvement = saving x shares0.005 x 300= 1.50

Your order saved 1.50 in total, or 0.0050 per share, compared with the best public quote.
