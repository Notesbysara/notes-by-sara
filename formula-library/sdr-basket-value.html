SDR Basket Valuation

Beginner

Also known as: IMF, World Bank and BIS, special drawing right, IMF reserve asset, basket currency

The dollar value and composition of the SDR. Use it to see how the IMF's unit of account is valued. The SDR is a basket of five currencies with fixed amounts, so its value moves with the exchange rates, and the weights drift between reviews. It is a reserve asset the IMF allocates to members and is used for IMF loans. The amounts shown are believed to be those set at the 2022 review and the exchange rates are illustrative, so check the IMF's current published values before relying on the result.

The formula

VSDRUSD=∑iai ei,wi=aieiVSDRV_{SDR}^{USD}=\sum_{i}a_{i}\,e_{i},\qquad w_{i}=\frac{a_{i}e_{i}}{V_{SDR}}

Value of 1 SDR in dollars = sum over the basket currencies of (fixed amount of the currency in the basket x its dollar exchange rate); Weight of currency i = its dollar value in the basket / total value

Worked example

Basket amounts: 0.57813 USD, 0.37379 EUR, 1.0993 CNY, 13.452 JPY, 0.08087 GBP. Illustrative dollar rates: 1.00, 1.08, 0.138, 0.0066, 1.27.

  1. Dollar value of each component= 0.5781, 0.4037, 0.1517, 0.0888, 0.1027
  2. Value of one SDR in dollars= 1.3250
  3. Weight of USD[0.57813, 0.403693, 0.151703, 0.0887832, 0.102705][0] / 1.32501= 43.6%
  4. Weight of EUR[0.57813, 0.403693, 0.151703, 0.0887832, 0.102705][1] / 1.32501= 30.5%
  5. Weight of CNY[0.57813, 0.403693, 0.151703, 0.0887832, 0.102705][2] / 1.32501= 11.4%
  6. Weight of JPY[0.57813, 0.403693, 0.151703, 0.0887832, 0.102705][3] / 1.32501= 6.7%
  7. Weight of GBP[0.57813, 0.403693, 0.151703, 0.0887832, 0.102705][4] / 1.32501= 7.8%
  8. Change in the SDR value if the euro falls 10%-0.10 x [0.57813, 0.403693, 0.151703, 0.0887832, 0.102705][1] / 1.32501= -3.05%

One SDR is worth 1.3250 dollars. The dollar share of the basket is 43.6%, the euro 30.5%, the yuan 11.4%, the yen 6.7% and the sterling 7.8%. A 10% fall in the euro would lower the SDR by -3.05%.
