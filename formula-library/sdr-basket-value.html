SDR Basket Valuation

BeginnerInternational FinanceAlso known as: IMF, World Bank and BIS, special drawing right, IMF reserve asset, basket currency
← Sanctions: 50% Ownership Aggregation RuleSecondaries: Price Versus NAV and Buyer IRR →

Finds: the dollar value and composition of the SDR.

When to use it: Use it to see how the IMF's unit of account is valued. The SDR is a basket of five currencies with fixed amounts, so its value moves with the exchange rates, and the weights drift between reviews. It is a reserve asset the IMF allocates to members and is used for IMF loans. The amounts shown are believed to be those set at the 2022 review and the exchange rates are illustrative, so check the IMF's current published values before relying on the result.

The formula

VSDRUSD\displaystyle V_{SDR}^{USD}=∑iai ei\displaystyle =\sum_{i}a_{i}\,e_{i}wi\displaystyle w_{i}=aieiVSDR\displaystyle =\frac{a_{i}e_{i}}{V_{SDR}}

In words: Value of 1 SDR in dollars = sum over the basket currencies of (fixed amount of the currency in the basket x its dollar exchange rate); Weight of currency i = its dollar value in the basket / total value

What each symbol means

a_iFixed number of units of currency i in the SDR basket (set at review, every five years)units
e_iExchange rate: dollars per unit of currency irate
VValue of one SDR in dollarsmoney

The answer

One SDR is worth 1.3250 dollars. The dollar share of the basket is 43.6%, the euro 30.5%, the yuan 11.4%, the yen 6.7% and the sterling 7.8%. A 10% fall in the euro would lower the SDR by −3.05%.
