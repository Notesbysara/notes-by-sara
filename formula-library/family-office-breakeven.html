Family Office Break-Even Size

Beginner

Also known as: family offices, single family office, multi-family office, cost of wealth management

The wealth level where a family office pays for itself. Use it to judge when a private family office is worth running. Its cost is mostly fixed, so the cost per unit of wealth falls as wealth grows. Below the break-even size an outside provider is cheaper. Cost is not the only factor: control, privacy, and bespoke services such as tax planning and philanthropy also matter.

The formula

AUM∗=Ffalt−v,Cost%=FAUM+vAUM^{*}=\frac{F}{f_{alt}-v},\quad \text{Cost}\%=\frac{F}{AUM}+v

Break-even wealth = fixed annual cost / (fee rate of the outside alternative - variable cost rate); Cost as a share of wealth = fixed cost / wealth + variable cost rate

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
FFixed yearly running cost: staff, systems, offices, compliancemoney
vVariable cost rate on assets (custody, advisers)percent
f_altFee rate of the alternative, such as a multi-family office or private bankpercent
AUMWealth to be managedmoney

Worked example

A single family office costs 1.2 million a year to run plus 0.15% of assets. A multi-family office charges 0.80%. The family has 300 million.

  1. Break-even wealth = F / (alt - v)1.2 / (0.008-0.0015)= 184.6
  2. Family office cost as a share of assets1.2 / 300+0.0015= 0.550%
  3. Family office total cost0.0055 x 300= 1.65
  4. Alternative total cost0.008 x 300= 2.40
  5. Saving by running the family office2.4-1.65= 0.75

The break-even wealth is 184.6 million. At 300 million, the family office costs 0.550% of assets, or 1.65 a year, against 2.40 with the alternative, saving 0.75 a year.
