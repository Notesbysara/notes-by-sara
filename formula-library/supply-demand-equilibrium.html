Linear Supply and Demand Equilibrium

BeginnerEconomics for FinanceAlso known as: supply, demand and prices, market clearing price, price ceiling shortage, demand shift
← Limits to Arbitrage: Holding Cost and Adverse MoveLinking the Three Statements: Cash Flow and Balance Check →

Finds: the market price, and the effect of a shift or a ceiling.

When to use it: Use it to see how a price is set where buyers and sellers agree. A rise in demand (a larger a) raises both the price and the quantity. A price ceiling below the equilibrium price creates a shortage, because buyers want more than sellers will supply. Real curves are not straight lines, so treat this as an illustration of direction and rough size.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Qd\displaystyle Q_{d}=a−bP,  Qs=c+dP  ⇒  P∗=a−cb+d,  Q∗=a−bP∗\displaystyle =a-bP,\; Q_{s}=c+dP\;\Rightarrow\; P^{*}=\frac{a-c}{b+d},\; Q^{*}=a-bP^{*}

In words: Demand: Qd = a − b x P. Supply: Qs = c + d x P. Equilibrium price P* = (a − c) / (b + d); equilibrium quantity Q* = a − b x P*

What each symbol means

aQuantity demanded when the price is zero (the demand intercept)units
bHow much quantity demanded falls for each 1 rise in priceunits per price
cQuantity supplied when the price is zero (can be negative)units
dHow much quantity supplied rises for each 1 rise in priceunits per price

Worked example

Demand is Qd = 100 - 2P and supply is Qs = 10 + 3P. Find the equilibrium. Then demand rises so the intercept becomes 120, and a ceiling of 15 is set on the original market.

Equilibrium price(100 − 10) ÷ (2 + 3)18
Equilibrium quantity100 − 2 × 1864
Price after demand shifts to a2(120 − 10) ÷ (2 + 3)22
Quantity after the shift10 + 3 × 2276
Change in price22 ÷ 18 − 122.2%
Quantity demanded at the ceiling100 − 2 × 1570
Quantity supplied at the ceiling10 + 3 × 1555
Shortage at the ceilingmax(70 − 55, 0)15
The answer

The equilibrium price is 18 and the quantity is 64. After the demand rise the price is 22 and the quantity 76, a price rise of 22.2%. A ceiling of 15 in the original market makes buyers want 70 but sellers offer only 55, a shortage of 15.
