Covered Bond Overcollateralization

Beginner

Also known as: cover pool, asset cover test, pfandbrief, dual recourse

The cushion behind covered bonds. Use it to see how much protection a covered bond holder has. Holders have a claim on the issuing bank and a first claim on the cover pool, which stays on the bank's balance sheet. The pool must exceed the bonds by a set margin.

The formula

OC=Cover poolCovered bonds−1OC=\frac{\text{Cover pool}}{\text{Covered bonds}}-1

Overcollateralization = Cover pool value / Covered bonds outstanding - 1

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
Cover poolMortgages or public-sector loans set aside to back the bonds. The issuer must keep it topped upmoney
Covered bondsFace value of bonds outstandingmoney
MinimumLegal or rating-agency minimum coverpercent

Worked example

A bank has a cover pool of 1,150 backing covered bonds of 1,000. The legal minimum is 105% cover. What is the overcollateralization and the spare cover?

  1. Cover ratio = pool / bonds1150 / 1000= 115.0%
  2. Overcollateralization = ratio - 11.15-1= 15.0%
  3. Spare cover = pool - minimum x bonds1150-1.05 x 1000= 100.0

The overcollateralization is 15.0%. The pool is 115.0% of the bonds against a minimum of 105%, leaving 100.0 of spare cover.
