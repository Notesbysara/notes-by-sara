Conflicted Advice: Long-Run Cost of a Hidden Commission

BeginnerRegulation, Ethics & ESGAlso known as: ethics, fiduciary standards and conflicts of interest, fiduciary vs suitability, commission cost, best interest standard
← Confidence Interval for a MeanConsumer Price Index (Laspeyres) and Substitution Bias →

Finds: what a conflict of interest costs over time.

When to use it: Use it to see why fiduciary duty (acting in the client's best interest) matters. A suitability standard allows an adviser to sell a product that is suitable but pays them more. A one-percentage-point difference in yearly cost looks small but compounds into a large share of the final amount over many years.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

C\displaystyle C=V0[(1+r−f)n−(1+r−f−c)n]\displaystyle =V_{0}\left[(1+r-f)^{n}-(1+r-f-c)^{n}\right]

In words: Value with a fiduciary adviser who charges f = V0 x (1 + r − f)^n; Value with a product that also pays the adviser a yearly commission c = V0 x (1 + r − f − c)^n; Cost of the conflict = difference between the two; Share of ending value lost = 1 − (1 + r − f − c)^n / (1 + r − f)^n

What each symbol means

V_0Amount investedmoney
rReturn before costsper year
fCost of the cleaner productper year
cExtra yearly commission or fee paid to the adviserper year
nYearsyears

Worked example

A client invests 100,000 for 20 years at 7% before costs. A low-cost fund charges 0.25%. A product recommended by a commission-paid adviser charges an extra 1% a year.

Ending value, low-cost product100,000 × (1 + 0.07 − 0.0025)^20369,281.60
Ending value, commission product100,000 × (1 + 0.07 − 0.0025 − 0.01)^20305,919.75
Cost of the conflict369,282 − 305,92063,361.85
Share of ending value lost63,361.9 ÷ 369,28217.2%
Cost as a multiple of the amount invested63,361.9 ÷ 100,0000.63
The answer

The low-cost fund grows to 369,281.60 and the commission product to 305,919.75, so the conflict costs the client 63,361.85, or 17.2% of what they would have had. That is 0.63 times the amount they invested in the first place that they gave up.
