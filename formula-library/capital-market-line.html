Capital Market Line (CML)

Intermediate

Also known as: CML, market portfolio line, efficient portfolios with a risk-free asset

Efficient return for a given total risk. Use it to find the best expected return available for a given level of total risk, by mixing the market portfolio with the risk-free asset. It applies to efficient, well-diversified portfolios only.

The formula

E(Rp)=rf+E(Rm)−rfσm σpE(R_{p})=r_{f}+\frac{E(R_{m})-r_{f}}{\sigma_{m}}\,\sigma_{p}

Expected return = rf + (E(Rm) - rf) / sm x sp

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit

Worked example

The risk-free rate is 3%, the market is expected to return 8% with a standard deviation of 16%. What expected return should an efficient portfolio with a risk of 20% offer?

  1. Market price of risk = (rm - rf) / sm(0.08-0.03) / 0.16= 0.3125
  2. Expected return = rf + price of risk x sp0.03+0.3125 x 0.2= 9.25%

The market price of risk is 0.3125, so a portfolio with 20% risk should offer 9.25%. It needs borrowing at the risk-free rate, because its risk is above the market's.
