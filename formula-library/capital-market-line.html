Capital Market Line (CML)

IntermediateInvestments & Portfolio TheoryAlso known as: CML, market portfolio line, efficient portfolios with a risk-free asset
← Capital Gains and Dividend TaxCarbon Markets: Abate or Buy Permits →

Finds: efficient return for a given total risk.

When to use it: Use it to find the best expected return available for a given level of total risk, by mixing the market portfolio with the risk-free asset. It applies to efficient, well-diversified portfolios only.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

E(Rp)\displaystyle E(R_{p})=rf+E(Rm)−rfσm σp\displaystyle =r_{f}+\frac{E(R_{m})-r_{f}}{\sigma_{m}}\,\sigma_{p}

In words: Expected return = rf + (E(Rm) − rf) / sm x sp

Worked example

The risk-free rate is 3%, the market is expected to return 8% with a standard deviation of 16%. What expected return should an efficient portfolio with a risk of 20% offer?

Market price of risk(0.08 − 0.03) ÷ 0.160.3125
Expected return0.03 + 0.3125 × 0.29.25%
The answer

The market price of risk is 0.3125, so a portfolio with 20% risk should offer 9.25%. It needs borrowing at the risk-free rate, because its risk is above the market's.
