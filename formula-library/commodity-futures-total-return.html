Commodity Futures: Roll Yield and Total Return

IntermediateAlternative InvestmentsAlso known as: commodities and commodity futures, contango, backwardation, roll return, convenience yield
← Commodity Futures: Basis and Roll YieldCommodity Hedge: Effective Price, Basis and Hedge Ratio →

Finds: what rolling futures costs or earns and what the curve implies.

When to use it: Use it to see why returns on a commodity futures fund can differ from the spot price. A futures investor sells the contract that is expiring and buys the next one. When the curve slopes up (contango), each roll costs money; when it slopes down (backwardation), each roll adds money. Over years roll yield can matter as much as the price change.

The formula

Roll\displaystyle \text{Roll}=Fnear−FfarFnear\displaystyle =\frac{F_{near}-F_{far}}{F_{near}}Rtotal\displaystyle R_{total}=Rspot+Roll+Rcollateral\displaystyle =R_{spot}+\text{Roll}+R_{collateral}y\displaystyle y=r+u−1Tln⁡FS\displaystyle =r+u-\frac{1}{T}\ln\frac{F}{S}

In words: Roll yield per roll = (near futures price − next futures price) / near futures price (negative in contango, positive in backwardation); Annualized roll = (1 + roll)^(12 / months between contracts) − 1; Total return of a futures investment = spot price change + roll yield + interest on the cash collateral; Implied convenience yield y = interest rate + storage cost − (1/T) x ln(F / S)

What each symbol means

F_near, F_farFutures prices of the contract being sold and the next one boughtmoney
SSpot price of the commoditymoney

TYears to the futures delivery dateyears

Worked example

Spot is 80. The near contract (1 month) is 81.0 and the next (4 months) is 82.4. Cash collateral earns 4%. Assume spot is flat over the year and the curve stays the same.

Roll yield per roll(81 − 82.4) ÷ 81−1.73%
Annualized roll yield(1+−0.017284)^(12 ÷ 3) − 1−6.74%
Total return0+−0.067364 + 0.04−2.74%
Implied convenience yield0.04 + 0.02 − ln(82.4 ÷ 80) ÷ (4 ÷ 12)−2.87%
The answer

Each roll gives −1.73%, which is a cost because the curve is in contango. Annualized, the roll is −6.74%. With spot flat and 4% on collateral, the total return is −2.74%. The implied convenience yield, with storage costs of 2% and a 4-month contract, is −2.87%.
