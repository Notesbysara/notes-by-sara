Equity Research Pitch: Target Price and Risk/Reward

Intermediate

Also known as: write an equity research pitch, target price, bull base bear, upside downside ratio, implied multiple

The numbers at the core of a stock recommendation. Use it to put a recommendation on a few clear numbers. State what you expect earnings to be, what multiple you think is fair, and what happens in good and bad cases. A pitch should say what would make it wrong. The probabilities are your judgment, so say why you chose them.

The formula

Ptarget=EPS×PE,E[P]=∑psPs,RR=Pbull−P0P0−PbearP_{target}=EPS\times PE,\quad E[P]=\sum p_{s}P_{s},\quad RR=\frac{P_{bull}-P_{0}}{P_{0}-P_{bear}}

Base target price = forecast earnings per share x target price-to-earnings multiple; Expected value = sum of probability x price over bull, base and bear; Upside to expected value = expected price / current price - 1; Risk/reward = (bull price - current price) / (current price - bear price); Implied multiple at the current price = price / forecast EPS

Worked example

A stock trades at 78. Forecast EPS is 5.20 and the fair multiple is 19. The bull price is 118, the bear price is 52, and the probabilities are 25% bull, 50% base and 25% bear.

  1. Base target price5.2 x 19= 98.80
  2. Upside to the base target98.8 / 78-1= 26.7%
  3. Probability-weighted value0.25 x 118+(1-0.25-0.25) x 98.8+0.25 x 52= 91.90
  4. Upside to expected value91.9 / 78-1= 17.8%
  5. Risk/reward ratio(118-78) / (78-52)= 1.54
  6. Current multiple on forecast EPS78 / 5.2= 15.0

The base target is 98.80, or 26.7% above the current price. The probability-weighted value is 91.90, an upside of 17.8%. Risk/reward is 1.54: for each 1 you could lose in the bear case you could gain that much in the bull case. At 78 the stock trades on 15.0 times forecast earnings.
