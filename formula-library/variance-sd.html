Variance and Standard Deviation of Returns

IntermediateFinance FoundationsAlso known as: standard deviation, volatility, risk, variance, sample standard deviation
← VaR Backtest: Exceptions and Kupiec TestVariance of a Probability Distribution →

Finds: how much returns vary (risk).

When to use it: Use it to measure how widely returns vary around their average. A larger standard deviation means a riskier, more volatile investment.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

s2\displaystyle s^{2}=∑i=1n(Ri−Rˉ)2n−1\displaystyle = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(R_{i}-\bar{R})^{2}}{n-1}s\displaystyle s=s2\displaystyle = \sqrt{s^{2}}

In words: s^2 = sum of (Ri − mean)^2 / (n − 1), and s = square root of s^2

What each symbol means

s^2Sample variance of returnspercent squared
sSample standard deviation, the usual measure of volatilitypercent
R_iReturn in period ipercent
R-barMean returnpercent
nNumber of observationsnumber

Worked example

An investment returned 10%, −5% and 15% in three years. What are the sample variance and standard deviation?

Mean return6.67%
Deviation of each return from the mean3.33%, −11.67%, 8.33%
Square each deviation0.0011, 0.0136, 0.0069
Variance0.0108
Standard deviationsqrt(var)10.41%
The answer

The standard deviation is 10.41%. This is a sample figure, so it divides by n - 1.
