T-Bill Discount Yield and Money Market Yields

IntermediateFixed IncomeAlso known as: bank discount yield, bond equivalent yield, commercial paper, certificate of deposit, T-bill
← Swap Gains from Comparative Advantaget-Statistic for a Mean (Hypothesis Test) →

Finds: the true return on a short-term discount instrument.

When to use it: Use it to compare short-term instruments that pay no coupon, such as T-bills, commercial paper and many CDs. The quoted discount yield understates the true return because it divides by the face value, not the price paid.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

BDY\displaystyle BDY=F−PF×360t\displaystyle =\frac{F-P}{F}\times\frac{360}{t}MMY\displaystyle MMY=F−PP×360t\displaystyle =\frac{F-P}{P}\times\frac{360}{t}BEY\displaystyle BEY=F−PP×365t\displaystyle =\frac{F-P}{P}\times\frac{365}{t}

In words: Discount yield = (F − P) / F x 360 / days; Money market yield = (F − P) / P x 360 / days; Bond equivalent yield = (F − P) / P x 365 / days

What each symbol means

FFace value paid at maturitymoney
PPrice paid todaymoney
tDays to maturitydays
BDYBank discount yield, the quoting convention for T-billsper year
MMYMoney market yield on a 360-day yearper year
BEYBond equivalent yield on a 365-day year, comparable with a coupon bondper year

Worked example

A 91-day T-bill with a face value of 100,000 costs 98,750. What are its yields?

Discount100,000 − 98,7501,250
Bank discount yield1,250 ÷ 100,000 × 360 ÷ 914.945%
Money market yield1,250 ÷ 98,750 × 360 ÷ 915.008%
Bond equivalent yield1,250 ÷ 98,750 × 365 ÷ 915.077%
Effective annual yield(1 + 1,250 ÷ 98,750)^(365 ÷ 91) − 15.175%
The answer

The discount yield is 4.945%, but the money market yield is 5.008% and the bond equivalent yield is 5.077%. The effective annual yield, with compounding, is 5.175%.
