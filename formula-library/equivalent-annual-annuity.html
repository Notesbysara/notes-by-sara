Equivalent Annual Annuity (EAA)

Intermediate

Also known as: EAA, equivalent annual cost, unequal project lives, EAC

A yearly figure to compare projects of different lengths. Use it to compare projects with different lives. Comparing raw NPVs favors the longer project, so convert each to a yearly equivalent and pick the higher one.

The formula

EAA=NPV[1−(1+r)−n]/rEAA=\frac{NPV}{\left[1-(1+r)^{-n}\right]/r}

EAA = NPV / ((1 - (1 + r)^-n) / r)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
EAAThe steady yearly amount that has the same NPV as the projectmoney per year
NPVNet present value of the projectmoney
rDiscount rate per year, as a decimalper year
nLife of the project in yearsyears

Worked example

A project with a life of 3 years has an NPV of 500 at a discount rate of 10%. What is its equivalent annual annuity?

  1. Annuity factor = (1 - (1 + r)^-n) / r(1-(1+0.1)^(-3)) / 0.1= 2.4869
  2. EAA = NPV / annuity factornpv / 2.48685= 201.06

The equivalent annual annuity is 201.06 a year for 3 years.
