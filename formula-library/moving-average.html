Simple Moving Average

Beginner

Also known as: SMA, rolling average, trend smoothing, time series average

A smoothed trend value. Use it to smooth out day-to-day noise in a price or other series so the trend is easier to see.

The formula

SMAk=1k∑i=0k−1Pt−iSMA_{k}=\frac{1}{k}\sum_{i=0}^{k-1}P_{t-i}

SMA = (sum of the last k values) / k

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
SMA_kSimple moving average over the last k periodsmoney
P_tValue in the latest periodmoney
kWindow length, the number of periods averagedperiods

Worked example

A share closed at 100, 102, 101, 105, 107 and 110 over six days. What is the 3-day moving average on the latest day?

  1. The last k values= 105.00, 107.00, 110.00
  2. SMA = sum of the window / k= 107.33

The 3-day moving average is 107.33, below the latest price of 110, which shows the recent upward move.
