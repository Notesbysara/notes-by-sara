Tranche Loss Allocation (Attachment and Detachment)

Intermediate

Also known as: tranching, waterfall losses, attachment point, detachment point, equity mezzanine senior tranche

Which tranche absorbs how much of a pool loss. Use it to see who bears credit losses in a securitization. Losses hit the lowest (equity) tranche first, then the middle (mezzanine), and the senior tranche last. A tranche is like a slice of the pool, so it only loses when pool losses pass its attachment point.

The formula

Tranche loss=min⁡(max⁡(L−A,0), D−A)D−A\text{Tranche loss}=\frac{\min\left(\max(L-A,0),\,D-A\right)}{D-A}

Share of the tranche lost = min( max(Pool loss - Attachment point, 0), Detachment point - Attachment point ) / (Detachment point - Attachment point)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
LLoss on the whole pool, as a share of the poolpercent
AAttachment point, the pool loss at which this tranche starts to lose moneypercent
DDetachment point, the pool loss at which the tranche is wiped outpercent

Worked example

A pool loses 8% of its value. Tranche A covers losses from 0% to 5%, tranche B from 5% to 15% and tranche C from 15% to 100%. The pool is 1,000 in size. How are the losses shared?

  1. Equity tranche loss share (0% to 5%)min(max(0.08-0, 0), 0.05) / 0.05= 100.0%
  2. Mezzanine tranche loss share (5% to 15%)min(max(0.08-0.05, 0), 0.10) / 0.10= 30.0%
  3. Senior tranche loss share (15% to 100%)min(max(0.08-0.15, 0), 0.85) / 0.85= 0.0%
  4. Equity tranche loss in money1 x 0.05 x 1000= 50.00
  5. Mezzanine tranche loss in money0.3 x 0.10 x 1000= 30.00
  6. Senior tranche loss in money0 x 0.85 x 1000= 0.00
  7. Total losses allocated50+30+0= 80.00

The equity tranche (0% to 5%) loses 100.0% of its value, the mezzanine tranche (5% to 15%) loses 30.0%, and the senior tranche loses 0.0%. In money that is 50.00, 30.00 and 0.00, adding up to the pool loss of 80.00.
