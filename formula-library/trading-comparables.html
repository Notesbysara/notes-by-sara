Valuation from Trading Comparables

Intermediate

Also known as: comps, relative valuation, peer multiples, comparable companies

Value from peer multiples. Use it to value a company by what the market pays for similar ones. Pick peers with similar size, growth, risk and business, and prefer the median to the average.

The formula

EV=Metric×Median peer multipleEV=\text{Metric}\times\text{Median peer multiple}

Implied value = Target metric (such as EBITDA) x Median multiple of peer companies

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
MetricThe target's own EBITDA, sales or earningsmoney
Peer multipleEV divided by the same metric for each comparable companynumber
MedianThe middle value of the peer multiples, less affected by outliers than the averagenumber

Worked example

Five peer companies trade at EV/EBITDA multiples of 8.5, 10.2, 9.1, 11.0 and 7.8. The target has EBITDA of 200. What is its implied enterprise value?

  1. Median peer multiple= 9.10
  2. Average peer multiple= 9.32
  3. Implied EV = EBITDA x median multiple200 x 9.1= 1,820.00
  4. Implied EV using the average200 x 9.32= 1,864.00

The median multiple is 9.10 and the average is 9.32. The target's implied enterprise value is 1,820.00, or 1,864.00 using the average.
