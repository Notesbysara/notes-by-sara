Net Change in Cash

BeginnerFinance FoundationsAlso known as: cash flow statement total, cash flow from operations investing financing, ending cash
← Net Book ValueNet Income →

Finds: how cash moved over the period.

When to use it: Use it to reconcile the cash flow statement. The three sections add up to the change in the cash balance on the balance sheet.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

ΔCash\displaystyle \Delta\text{Cash}=CFO+CFI+CFF\displaystyle =CFO+CFI+CFFCashend\displaystyle \text{Cash}_{end}=Cashbegin+ΔCash\displaystyle =\text{Cash}_{begin}+\Delta\text{Cash}

In words: Change in cash = CFO + CFI + CFF, and Ending cash = Beginning cash + Change in cash

What each symbol means

CFOCash flow from operating activities, cash from running the businessmoney
CFICash flow from investing activities, usually negative when buying assetsmoney
CFFCash flow from financing activities, such as borrowing, repaying and paying dividendsmoney
Cash_beginCash at the start of the periodmoney

Worked example

A company generated 900 from operations, spent 500 on investing, and paid out 200 through financing. It began the year with 300 of cash. What is its ending cash?

Net change900+−500+−200200
Ending cash300 + 200500
The answer

The net change in cash is 200, so ending cash is 500.
