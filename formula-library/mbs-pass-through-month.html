MBS Pass-Through Cash Flow for One Month

Intermediate

Also known as: mortgage backed securities, pass through rate, servicing fee, scheduled principal, prepayment

A mortgage pool's monthly cash flow to investors. Use it to see where the monthly cash from a mortgage pool goes. Borrowers pay interest at the WAC and a level payment. Servicers keep a fee, and investors get the pass-through interest, the scheduled principal and any prepayments.

The formula

Investor cash flow=Interest at pass-through rate+Scheduled principal+Prepayment\text{Investor cash flow}=\text{Interest at pass-through rate}+\text{Scheduled principal}+\text{Prepayment}

Investor cash flow = Balance x Pass-through rate / 12 + Scheduled principal + SMM x (Balance - Scheduled principal)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
WACWeighted average coupon of the mortgages in the poolper year
Pass-through rateRate paid to investors, which is the WAC less servicing and guarantee feesper year
SMMSingle monthly mortality, the share of the remaining balance prepaid this monthpercent
nRemaining monthsmonths

Worked example

A pool has a balance of 300,000,000, a WAC of 6.5%, a pass-through rate of 6.0%, 360 months remaining and a prepayment speed of 8% CPR. What does the investor receive this month?

  1. Monthly mortgage rate = WAC / 120.065 / 12= 0.5417%
  2. Level payment300,000,000 x 0.00541667 / (1-(1+0.00541667)^(-360))= 1,896,204.07
  3. Interest paid by borrowers = balance x monthly rate300,000,000 x 0.00541667= 1,625,000.00
  4. Scheduled principal = payment - interest1,896,204-1,625,000= 271,204.07
  5. SMM = 1 - (1 - CPR)^(1/12)1-(1-0.08)^(1 / 12)= 0.692%
  6. Prepayment = SMM x (balance - scheduled principal)0.00692438 x (300,000,000-271204)= 2,075,436.87
  7. Interest to investors = balance x pass-through rate / 12300,000,000 x 0.06 / 12= 1,500,000.00
  8. Servicing and guarantee fee kept = interest - investor interest1,625,000-1,500,000= 125,000.00
  9. Total to investors1,500,000+271204+2,075,437= 3,846,640.94
  10. New balance300,000,000-271204-2,075,437= 297,653,359.06

Borrowers pay 1,896,204.07, of which 1,625,000.00 is interest. The servicer keeps 125,000.00. Investors receive 3,846,640.94: 1,500,000.00 of interest, 271,204.07 of scheduled principal and 2,075,436.87 of prepayments (SMM of 0.692%). The balance falls to 297,653,359.06.
