Duration Gap and Equity Sensitivity

Intermediate

Also known as: asset liability management, ALM, interest rate risk of a bank, DGAP

A bank's equity risk from a parallel rate move. Use it to see how a rise in rates changes a bank's equity. A bank that funds long assets with short deposits has a positive duration gap, so higher rates cut asset values by more than they cut liabilities and equity falls. It shows value risk, whereas the repricing gap shows effects on income. It assumes a parallel yield shift and uses approximations that are less accurate for large moves.

The formula

DGAP=DA−LADL,ΔE≈−DGAP×A×Δy1+yDGAP=D_{A}-\frac{L}{A}D_{L},\quad \Delta E\approx-DGAP\times A\times\frac{\Delta y}{1+y}

Duration gap = duration of assets - (liabilities / assets) x duration of liabilities; Change in equity value = - duration gap x assets x (change in yield) / (1 + yield)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
D_A, D_LDuration of assets and of liabilities, in yearsyears
A, LMarket value of assets and liabilitiesmoney

DGAPDuration gap: positive means assets are more rate-sensitive than fundingyears

Worked example

Assets are 8,000 with a duration of 4.5 years, liabilities 7,600 with a duration of 2.0 years. Yields are 5% and rise by 1 percentage point.

  1. Duration gap = D_A - (L / A) x D_L4.5-(7600 / 8000) x 2= 2.600
  2. Change in equity = -gap x A x dy / (1 + y)-2.6 x 8000 x 0.01 / (1+0.05)= -198.10
  3. Equity = A - L8000-7600= 400.00
  4. Change as a share of equity-198.095 / 400= -49.5%
  5. Liability duration that gives zero gap = D_A x A / L4.5 x 8000 / 7600= 4.74

The duration gap is 2.600 years. A 1 point rise in yields lowers equity value by -198.10, which is -49.5% of the book equity of 400.00. To fully immunize, liability duration would need to be 4.74 years.
