Implementation Shortfall and Market Impact

Intermediate

Also known as: trading and market structure, transaction cost analysis, TCA, square-root impact, arrival price, delay cost

What a trade cost versus the price at the decision. Use it to measure what a trade really cost against the price you wanted. The gap between the paper price at the decision and what you actually paid includes waiting, your own price impact and fees. Large orders move the price, and the square-root rule gives a rough guide to how much. It should be used to choose how fast to trade: faster trading costs more impact but less risk of the price drifting away.

The formula

IS=(Pdec−Parr)q⏟delay+(Pavg−Pdec)q⏟impact+fees,Impact≈k σqVIS=\underbrace{(P_{dec}-P_{arr})q}_{\text{delay}}+\underbrace{(P_{avg}-P_{dec})q}_{\text{impact}}+\text{fees},\quad \text{Impact}\approx k\,\sigma\sqrt{\frac{q}{V}}

For a buy: Delay cost = (price when trading started - price at the decision) x shares; Trading cost = (average fill price - price at the start of trading) x shares; Total shortfall = delay cost + trading cost + fees; In basis points = total / (arrival price x shares) x 10,000; Typical impact model: impact as a fraction of price = k x daily volatility x square root of (shares traded / daily volume), with k about 0.5 to 1

Worked example

A manager decides to buy 200,000 shares when the price is 50.00. Trading starts at 50.08 and the average fill is 50.31. Fees are 5 basis points. Daily volume is 4 million shares with volatility of 2.2%.

  1. Delay cost(50.08-50) x 200000= 16,000.00
  2. Trading cost (impact and spread)(50.31-50.08) x 200000= 46,000.00
  3. Fees50.31 x 200000 x 0.05= 5,031.00
  4. Total shortfall16000+46000+5031= 67,031.00
  5. Shortfall in basis points of order value67031 / (50 x 200000) x 10000= 67.0
  6. Order as a share of daily volume200000 / 4,000,000= 5.0%
  7. Square-root model impact0.7 x 0.022 x sqrt(200000 / 4,000,000)= 0.344%
  8. Model impact in basis points0.00344354 x 10000= 34.4
  9. Actual trading cost in basis points46000 / (50.08 x 200000) x 10000= 45.9

Delay cost is 16,000.00, trading cost 46,000.00 and fees 5,031.00, so the shortfall is 67,031.00, or 67.0 basis points of the order value. The square-root model would predict an impact of 0.344% of price (34.4 basis points) for an order of 5.0% of daily volume. The actual trading cost was 45.9 basis points.
