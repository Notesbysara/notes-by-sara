Profitability Index

IntermediateCorporate FinanceAlso known as: PI, benefit cost ratio, value per unit invested
← Profit MarginsProgressive Income Tax: Marginal and Effective Rates →

Finds: value per unit invested.

When to use it: Use it to rank projects when money is limited. A PI above 1 means NPV is positive. Choose the projects with the highest PI until the budget runs out.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

PI\displaystyle PI=PV of future cash flowsInitial investment\displaystyle =\frac{PV\text{ of future cash flows}}{\text{Initial investment}}

In words: PI = PV of future cash flows / Initial investment

What each symbol means

PIProfitability index, value received per 1 investednumber
PV of future cash flowsCash flows after today, each discounted to todaymoney
Initial investmentThe amount spent at the startmoney

Worked example

A project costs 1,000 today and returns 500 at the end of each of the next 3 years. With a discount rate of 10%, what is the profitability index?

PV of future cash flows1,243.43
PI1,243.43 ÷ 1,0001.243
NPV1,243.43 − 1,000243.43
The answer

The profitability index is 1.243. Each 1 invested returns 1.243 in present value, and the NPV is 243.43.
