Enterprise Value to Equity Value

Beginner

Also known as: EV bridge, equity bridge, net debt, value per share

Value belonging to ordinary shareholders. Use it to turn the value of the business into the value that belongs to ordinary shareholders, and then into a value per share. Subtract claims ahead of the shareholders and add cash they own.

The formula

Equity=EV−Debt+Cash−Minority interest−Preferred\text{Equity}=EV-\text{Debt}+\text{Cash}-\text{Minority interest}-\text{Preferred}

Equity value = Enterprise value - Debt + Cash - Minority interest - Preferred stock

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
EVEnterprise value, the value of the whole businessmoney
DebtBorrowings owed to lendersmoney
CashCash and cash equivalentsmoney
Minority interestValue of subsidiaries owned by othersmoney
PreferredPreferred stock, a claim ahead of ordinary shareholdersmoney

Worked example

A firm has an enterprise value of 5,000, debt of 1,500, cash of 400, minority interest of 100 and preferred stock of 50. It has 500 shares. What is the equity value per share?

  1. Net debt = debt - cash1500-400= 1,100.00
  2. Equity value = EV - net debt - minority interest - preferred5000-1100-100-50= 3,750.00
  3. Value per share = equity value / shares3750 / 500= 7.50

Net debt is 1,100.00 and the equity value is 3,750.00, which is 7.50 per share.
