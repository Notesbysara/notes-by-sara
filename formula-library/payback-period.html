Payback Period

BeginnerCorporate FinanceAlso known as: simple payback, years to recover investment
← Pairs Trade Sizing and Reversion ProfitPayment Rails: Percentage Fee vs Flat Fee Crossover →

Finds: how quickly the investment is recovered.

When to use it: Use it as a quick measure of how fast a project returns its cost. It ignores cash flows after the payback and ignores the time value of money, so use it alongside NPV, not instead of it.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Payback\displaystyle \text{Payback}=t−1+Unrecovered cost at start of year tCFt\displaystyle =t-1+\frac{\text{Unrecovered cost at start of year }t}{CF_{t}}

In words: Payback = last full year before recovery + unrecovered cost / cash flow in the recovery year

What each symbol means

PaybackYears needed to get the initial investment backyears
tThe year in which the cumulative cash flow turns positiveyear number
CF_tCash flow in year tmoney

Worked example

A project costs 1,000 and returns 300, 400, 500 and 200 in years 1 to 4. What is the payback period?

Payback2.60
The answer

The payback period is 2.60 years. By the end of year 2 you have recovered 700, and year 3 supplies the remaining 300.
