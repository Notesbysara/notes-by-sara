Student Loan Interest Accrual and Capitalization

BeginnerPersonal FinanceAlso known as: student loans, unsubsidized loan interest, capitalized interest, deferment cost
← Stress Test Loss and Capital BufferSum-of-the-Parts Valuation →

Finds: how much unpaid study-period interest adds to a loan.

When to use it: Use it for loans that charge interest while you study (many unsubsidized loans). If you do not pay it, it is typically added to the principal and then earns interest itself. Paying the interest as it accrues keeps the repayment payment lower. Rules differ by country and loan type; some loans are interest-free during study, so check your terms. Repayment here uses standard level payments.

The formula

Accrued\displaystyle \text{Accrued}=P r t\displaystyle =P\,r\,tNew principal\displaystyle \text{New principal}=P+Accrued\displaystyle =P+\text{Accrued}PMT\displaystyle PMT=iL1−(1+i)−N\displaystyle =\frac{iL}{1-(1+i)^{-N}}

In words: Interest accrued while studying = Principal x annual rate x years; if added to the balance (capitalized) the new principal = Principal + accrued interest; the payment on it = i x New principal / (1 − (1 + i)^(-N)), with i the monthly rate and N the number of payments

What each symbol means

rAnnual interest rateper year
tYears of study plus any grace period during which interest accrues but nothing is paidyears

Worked example

You borrow 20,000 at 5.5% simple interest for 4 years of study plus a 6 month grace period (4.5 years), then repay over 10 years.

Interest accrued20,000 × 0.055 × 4.54,950
New principal if capitalized20,000 + 4,95024,950
Monthly rate0.055 ÷ 120.458%
Payment on the capitalized balance24,950 × 0.00458333 ÷ (1 − (1 + 0.00458333)^(−10 × 12))270.77
Payment if no interest had accrued20,000 × 0.00458333 ÷ (1 − (1 + 0.00458333)^(−10 × 12))217.05
Extra monthly payment270.773 − 217.05353.72
Extra paid over the repayment term53.7205 × 10 × 126,446.46
Monthly interest if paid during study20,000 × 0.055 ÷ 1291.67
The answer

By the time repayment starts, 4,950 of interest has accrued, giving a new balance of 24,950. The monthly payment is 270.77 instead of 217.05, so not paying during study costs 53.72 a month, or 6,446.46 over the repayment. Paying the interest as you go would cost about 91.67 a month while studying.
