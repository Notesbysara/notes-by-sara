Jensen's Alpha

Intermediate

Also known as: alpha, manager skill, risk adjusted excess return, CAPM alpha

Value added beyond market risk. Use it to measure whether a manager added value after allowing for market risk. A positive alpha means the portfolio beat what its beta predicted.

The formula

α=Rp−[rf+βp(Rm−rf)]\alpha=R_{p}-\left[r_{f}+\beta_{p}(R_{m}-r_{f})\right]

Alpha = Portfolio return - (rf + beta x (Market return - rf))

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
alphaJensen's alpha, the return above what the portfolio's beta explainsper year
R_pPortfolio returnper year
r_fRisk-free rateper year
beta_pPortfolio betanumber
R_mMarket returnper year

Worked example

A fund returned 12% with a beta of 1.1. The risk-free rate was 3% and the market returned 9%. What is its alpha?

  1. Required return = rf + beta x (rm - rf)0.03+1.1 x (0.09-0.03)= 9.60%
  2. Alpha = Rp - required return0.12-0.096= 2.40%

Given its beta, the fund should have returned 9.60%. It returned 12%, so its alpha is 2.40%.
