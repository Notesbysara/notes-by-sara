Multi-Stage Dividend Discount Model (Two Stages)

Intermediate

Also known as: two stage DDM, H model alternative, high growth then stable growth, multi stage dividend

Value of a company with a growth stage then steady growth. Use it for a company expected to grow fast for a while and then settle at a steady rate. It splits the value into the growth stage and the stable stage after it.

The formula

P0=D1r−g1[1−(1+g11+r)n]+Dn(1+g2)(r−g2)(1+r)nP_{0}=\frac{D_{1}}{r-g_{1}}\left[1-\left(\frac{1+g_{1}}{1+r}\right)^{n}\right]+\frac{D_{n}(1+g_{2})}{(r-g_{2})(1+r)^{n}}

Price = PV of the high-growth dividends for n years + PV of the terminal value at year n

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
D_1Dividend expected next year, D0 x (1 + g1)money
g_1High growth rate for the first n yearsper year
g_2Stable growth rate after year nper year
nLength of the high-growth stageyears
rRequired returnper year
D_nDividend in year n, D0 x (1 + g1)^nmoney

Worked example

A company just paid a dividend of 2. Dividends grow 12% a year for 5 years and then 4% a year forever. The required return is 9%. What is the share worth?

  1. D1 = D0 x (1 + g1)2 x (1+0.12)= 2.24
  2. PV of the high-growth dividendsd1 / (0.09-0.12) x (1-((1+0.12) / (1+0.09))^5)= 10.86
  3. Dividend in year n = D0 x (1 + g1)^n2 x (1+0.12)^5= 3.52
  4. Terminal value at year n = Dn x (1 + g2) / (r - g2)3.52468 x (1+0.04) / (0.09-0.04)= 73.31
  5. PV of the terminal value = TV / (1 + r)^n73.3134 / (1+0.09)^5= 47.65
  6. Share value = PV stage 1 + PV stage 210.8566+47.6487= 58.51

The high-growth dividends are worth 10.86 and the stable stage is worth 47.65 today, so the share is worth 58.51.
