Cointegration: Hedge Ratio, Spread Z-Score and Half-Life

Intermediate

Also known as: cointegration, Engle Granger, mean reversion of spread, half-life of mean reversion

Whether a pair behaves like a stable spread and how fast it reverts. Use it to test whether two prices share a long-run link. Each price may wander, but if a mix of them is stable the pair is cointegrated and the gap tends to close. The half-life says how fast. A full test also needs a unit-root test on the spread (Engle-Granger), which this calculator does not run, so treat the result as a screen and not proof.

The formula

β=Cov(Y,X)Var(X),  st=Yt−βXt−α,  st=ϕst−1+ε,  t1/2=−ln⁡2ln⁡ϕ\beta=\frac{Cov(Y,X)}{Var(X)},\; s_{t}=Y_{t}-\beta X_{t}-\alpha,\; s_{t}=\phi s_{t-1}+\varepsilon,\; t_{1/2}=\frac{-\ln 2}{\ln\phi}

Hedge ratio beta = Covariance(Y, X) / Variance(X); Spread = Y - beta x X - alpha; Fit spread today = phi x spread yesterday; Half-life = -ln 2 / ln(phi)

Worked example

Two price series of 30 observations each are given in the calculator. Regress Y on X, build the spread, find its latest z-score and the half-life.

  1. Hedge ratio = Cov(Y, X) / Var(X)= 1.3940
  2. Intercept = mean(Y) - beta x mean(X)= 14.7347
  3. Spread series= 0.56, -0.32, -0.37, -0.77, -0.93, 0.00, -0.38, -0.05, 0.59, 0.12, 0.17, 0.35, 0.43, -0.41, -0.14, 0.90, -0.31, 0.33, 0.17, -0.39, 0.70, 0.84, -0.38, -0.32, -0.00, -0.26, -0.17, -0.41, -0.12, 0.55
  4. Latest z-score = (last spread - mean) / sd= 1.181
  5. phi = Cov(spread_t, spread_t-1) / Var(spread_t-1)= 0.1321
  6. Half-life = -ln 2 / ln(phi)-ln(2) / ln(0.132143)= 0.34

The hedge ratio is 1.3940 and the intercept is 14.7347. The latest spread sits 1.181 standard deviations from its mean. The spread's persistence is 0.1321, so a gap closes by half in 0.34 periods.
