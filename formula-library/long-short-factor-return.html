Long-Short Factor Return

BeginnerInvestments & Portfolio TheoryAlso known as: factor premium, smart beta, factor investing, winners minus losers
← Loan Payment (Amortization)Long/Short Hedge Fund: Exposure, Gross and Net Return →

Finds: what a factor earned.

When to use it: Use it to measure a factor such as value, size or momentum. A factor return is what you earn by buying one group of stocks and selling short the other, so it is independent of the market's direction.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Factor return\displaystyle \text{Factor return}=Rlong−Rshort\displaystyle =R_{long}-R_{short}

In words: Factor return = Return of the long portfolio − Return of the short portfolio

What each symbol means

R_longReturn of the portfolio of stocks with the characteristic (for example, winners or cheap stocks)per period
R_shortReturn of the portfolio of stocks without itper period

Worked example

Over a year the top 30% of stocks by past return gained 14% and the bottom 30% gained 4%. What was the momentum factor return?

Factor return0.14 − 0.0410%
The answer

The momentum factor return was 10%, earned by buying winners and selling losers.
