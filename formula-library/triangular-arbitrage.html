Cross Rates and Triangular Arbitrage

Intermediate

Also known as: foreign exchange market structure, bid ask spread in pips, three currency arbitrage, implied cross rate

Whether three currency quotes are consistent. Use it to see how the three prices in a currency triangle stay in line. Dealers quote a bid and ask, so you always buy dearer and sell cheaper, and the gap is their spread and your trading cost. Arbitrage profits are tiny and vanish quickly in liquid markets, and a profit only exists if it exceeds the spreads, which is what the calculator tests.

The formula

XEUR/JPY=XEUR/USD×XUSD/JPY,Pips=Ask−Bid0.0001X_{EUR/JPY}=X_{EUR/USD}\times X_{USD/JPY},\quad \text{Pips}=\frac{Ask-Bid}{0.0001}

Implied cross rate = EUR/USD rate x USD/JPY rate. Triangular arbitrage trades around the three currencies at the dealer's prices (buy at the ask, sell at the bid) and profits if the quoted cross differs from the implied cross by more than the spread; Spread in pips = (ask - bid) / pip size

What each symbol means

Worked example

Quotes (bid/ask): EUR/USD 1.0850/1.0852, USD/JPY 150.20/150.22, EUR/JPY 162.90/163.10. Start with 1,000,000 USD and test both directions.

  1. EUR/USD spread in pips(1.0852-1.085) / 0.0001= 2.0
  2. Implied EUR/JPY bid = EUR/USD bid x USD/JPY bid1.085 x 150.2= 162.967
  3. Implied EUR/JPY ask = EUR/USD ask x USD/JPY ask1.0852 x 150.22= 163.019
  4. Loop A: USD to EUR (at the ask), to JPY (at the bid), back to USD (at the ask)1,000,000 / 1.0852 x 162.9 / 150.22= 999,271.59
  5. Profit, loop A999272-1,000,000= -728.41
  6. Loop B: USD to JPY (bid), to EUR (at the ask), back to USD (bid)1,000,000 x 150.2 / 163.1 x 1.085= 999,184.55
  7. Profit, loop B999185-1,000,000= -815.45

The EUR/USD spread is 2.0 pips. The implied EUR/JPY cross is 162.967 bid and 163.019 ask against the quoted 162.90/163.10. Going USD to EUR to JPY to USD ends with 999,271.59, a result of -728.41; going USD to JPY to EUR to USD ends with 999,184.55, a result of -815.45. A positive number is a profit; here neither loop earns one after the spreads.
