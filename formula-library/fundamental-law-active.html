Fundamental Law of Active Management

Intermediate

Also known as: portfolio construction and evaluation, information coefficient, breadth, transfer coefficient, expected active return

How much skill and breadth an active manager needs. Use it to see what is needed to add value. A small forecasting edge (IC = 0.05 is good) pays off if applied across many independent bets. Constraints such as no short selling lower the transfer coefficient. It does not prove skill: the same arithmetic applied to a manager's record needs enough data to separate skill from luck.

The formula

IR=TC×IC×BR,α=IR×TEIR=TC\times IC\times\sqrt{BR},\quad \alpha=IR\times TE

Information ratio (expected) = transfer coefficient x information coefficient x square root of breadth; Expected active return (alpha) = information ratio x tracking error; Information coefficient is the correlation between forecasts and outcomes; Breadth is the number of independent bets per year; Transfer coefficient is how much of the forecast makes it into the portfolio (1 = fully, below 1 with constraints); Breadth needed for a target IR = (IR / (TC x IC))^2

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
ICInformation coefficient: correlation between predicted and actual returnsnumber
BRBreadth: independent bets per yearcount
TCTransfer coefficient: how well the portfolio reflects the forecasts after constraints and costsnumber
TETracking error: planned risk against the benchmarkpercent

Worked example

A manager has an IC of 0.05, makes 100 independent bets a year, has a transfer coefficient of 0.8 and runs 4% tracking error.

  1. Expected information ratio0.8 x 0.05 x sqrt(100)= 0.40
  2. Expected active return0.4 x 0.04= 1.60%
  3. IR with double the breadth0.8 x 0.05 x sqrt(2 x 100)= 0.57
  4. Breadth needed for the target IR(0.75 / (0.8 x 0.05))^2= 352

The expected information ratio is 0.40, giving an expected active return of 1.60% a year. With the same skill, doubling breadth to 200 bets raises the IR to 0.57. To reach an IR of 0.75 the manager would need 352 independent bets.
