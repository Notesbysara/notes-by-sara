Performance Attribution (Brinson)

IntermediateInvestments & Portfolio TheoryAlso known as: Brinson Fachler, allocation effect, selection effect, attribution analysis
← PEG RatioPerpetuity →

Finds: sources of a portfolio's outperformance.

When to use it: Use it to explain why a portfolio beat or lagged its benchmark. Allocation shows the effect of over- or underweighting sectors, selection shows the effect of picking better stocks within sectors, and interaction is the combined leftover.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Ai\displaystyle A_{i}=(wp,i−wb,i)(Rb,i−Rb)\displaystyle =(w_{p,i}-w_{b,i})(R_{b,i}-R_{b})Si\displaystyle S_{i}=wb,i(Rp,i−Rb,i)\displaystyle =w_{b,i}(R_{p,i}-R_{b,i})Ii\displaystyle I_{i}=(wp,i−wb,i)(Rp,i−Rb,i)\displaystyle =(w_{p,i}-w_{b,i})(R_{p,i}-R_{b,i})

In words: Allocation = (Portfolio weight − Benchmark weight) x (Benchmark sector return − Total benchmark return); Selection = Benchmark weight x (Portfolio sector return − Benchmark sector return); Interaction = (Portfolio weight − Benchmark weight) x (Portfolio sector return − Benchmark sector return)

What each symbol means

w_(p,i), w_(b,i)Weight of sector i in the portfolio and in the benchmarkpercent
R_(p,i), R_(b,i)Return of sector i in the portfolio and in the benchmarkpercent
R_bTotal benchmark returnpercent
A, S, IAllocation, selection and interaction effects, which add up to the active returnpercent

Worked example

A portfolio and its benchmark hold three sectors. The portfolio weights are 50%, 30% and 20% and the benchmark weights are 40%, 40% and 20%. The portfolio sector returns are 8%, 5% and 2% and the benchmark sector returns are 6%, 6% and 1%. Where did the active return come from?

Portfolio return5.90%
Benchmark return5%
Active return0.059 − 0.050.90%
Allocation by sector0.0010, −0.0010, −0.0000
Selection by sector0.0080, −0.0040, 0.0020
Interaction by sector0.0020, 0.0010, 0.0000
Total allocation0%
Total selection0.60%
Total interaction0.30%
The answer

The portfolio returned 5.90% against 5% for the benchmark, an active return of 0.90%. Allocation contributed −0%, selection 0.60% and interaction 0.30%, which together add up to the active return.
