Performance Attribution (Brinson)

Intermediate

Also known as: Brinson Fachler, allocation effect, selection effect, attribution analysis

Sources of a portfolio's outperformance. Use it to explain why a portfolio beat or lagged its benchmark. Allocation shows the effect of over- or underweighting sectors, selection shows the effect of picking better stocks within sectors, and interaction is the combined leftover.

The formula

Ai=(wp,i−wb,i)(Rb,i−Rb),Si=wb,i(Rp,i−Rb,i),Ii=(wp,i−wb,i)(Rp,i−Rb,i)A_{i}=(w_{p,i}-w_{b,i})(R_{b,i}-R_{b}),\quad S_{i}=w_{b,i}(R_{p,i}-R_{b,i}),\quad I_{i}=(w_{p,i}-w_{b,i})(R_{p,i}-R_{b,i})

Allocation = (Portfolio weight - Benchmark weight) x (Benchmark sector return - Total benchmark return); Selection = Benchmark weight x (Portfolio sector return - Benchmark sector return); Interaction = (Portfolio weight - Benchmark weight) x (Portfolio sector return - Benchmark sector return)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
w_(p,i), w_(b,i)Weight of sector i in the portfolio and in the benchmarkpercent
R_(p,i), R_(b,i)Return of sector i in the portfolio and in the benchmarkpercent
R_bTotal benchmark returnpercent
A, S, IAllocation, selection and interaction effects, which add up to the active returnpercent

Worked example

A portfolio and its benchmark hold three sectors. The portfolio weights are 50%, 30% and 20% and the benchmark weights are 40%, 40% and 20%. The portfolio sector returns are 8%, 5% and 2% and the benchmark sector returns are 6%, 6% and 1%. Where did the active return come from?

  1. Portfolio return = sum of weight x return= 5.90%
  2. Benchmark return = sum of weight x return= 5.00%
  3. Active return = portfolio - benchmark0.059-0.05= 0.90%
  4. Allocation by sector= 0.0010, -0.0010, -0.0000
  5. Selection by sector= 0.0080, -0.0040, 0.0020
  6. Interaction by sector= 0.0020, 0.0010, 0.0000
  7. Total allocation= -0.00%
  8. Total selection= 0.60%
  9. Total interaction= 0.30%

The portfolio returned 5.90% against 5.00% for the benchmark, an active return of 0.90%. Allocation contributed -0.00%, selection 0.60% and interaction 0.30%, which together add up to the active return.
