Owner Earnings (Buffett)

IntermediateEquity MarketsAlso known as: Warren Buffett, free cash to owners, intrinsic value Buffett
← Output Gap and Annualized GrowthOwnership Dilution from a New Share Issue →

Finds: cash earnings available to owners.

When to use it: Use it to estimate the cash a business generates for its owners. Buffett prefers it to reported profit because depreciation is not cash but maintenance spending is. Value the stream as a perpetuity to estimate what the business is worth.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Owner earnings\displaystyle \text{Owner earnings}=NI+D&A−CapEx−ΔWC\displaystyle =NI+D\&A-\text{CapEx}-\Delta WC

In words: Owner earnings = Net income + Depreciation and amortization − Capital spending needed to maintain the business − Increase in working capital

What each symbol means

NINet incomemoney
D&ADepreciation and amortization, non-cash charges added backmoney
CapExCapital spending needed to keep the business runningmoney
Delta WCIncrease in working capital, cash tied up in the businessmoney

Worked example

A company earns 80 million, has 25 million of depreciation, needs 30 million of maintenance capital spending and increased working capital by 5 million. Growth is 3% and the required return is 9%. What are its owner earnings and a rough value?

Owner earnings80 + 25 − 30 − 570
Value70 × (1 + 0.03) ÷ (0.09 − 0.03)1,201.7
The answer

Owner earnings are 70.0 million. Valued as a growing perpetuity this is worth about 1,201.7 million.
