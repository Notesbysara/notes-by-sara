Peer Comparison: Median, Z-Score and Premium

IntermediateFinancial Statement AnalysisAlso known as: peer analysis, relative valuation, benchmarking a ratio, peer median
← Payment Rails: Percentage Fee vs Flat Fee CrossoverPeer-to-Peer Lending: Investor Net Return →

Finds: how far a company is from its peers on a ratio.

When to use it: Use it to see whether a company's ratio is unusually high or low against comparable firms. Use the median as well as the mean, since a single odd peer can distort the mean.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

z\displaystyle z=x−xˉpeersspeers\displaystyle =\frac{x-\bar{x}_{peers}}{s_{peers}}Premium\displaystyle \text{Premium}=xMedianpeers−1\displaystyle =\frac{x}{\text{Median}_{peers}}-1

In words: Z-score = (Company ratio − Peer mean) / Peer standard deviation; Premium to the median = Company ratio / Peer median − 1

What each symbol means

xThe company's ratio, such as P/E or operating marginnumber
Peer mean, median, sAverage, middle value and standard deviation of the peers' ratiosnumber
zNumber of peer standard deviations the company sits from the peer meannumber

Worked example

A company trades at a P/E of 12.5. Five peers trade at 9.8, 11.2, 14.0, 10.5 and 13.1.

Peer median11.20
Peer mean11.72
Peer standard deviation1.77
Z-score(12.5 − mean) ÷ 1.771160.44
Premium to median12.5 ÷ 11.2 − 111.6%
The answer

The peer median is 11.20 and the mean is 11.72. The company is at a z-score of 0.44, which is within the normal range for the group, and sits at a premium of 11.6% to the median.
