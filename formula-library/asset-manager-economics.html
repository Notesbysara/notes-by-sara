Asset Manager Revenue and Fee Sensitivity

BeginnerBanking & Financial InstitutionsAlso known as: asset management firms, AUM growth, fee compression, operating margin
← Asian Option PayoffBacktest Performance Metrics →

Finds: how AUM, fees and margin drive profit.

When to use it: Use it to see what drives an asset manager's earnings. Revenue rises with markets and net inflows, and falls with fee cuts, since costs are largely fixed, so profit moves more than revenue. The shift to cheaper passive funds has been compressing fee rates for years, so the fee sensitivity is worth checking.

The formula

In words: Ending AUM = starting AUM x (1 + market return) + net flows; Revenue = average AUM x fee rate; Operating profit = revenue x operating margin

Worked example

The answer

Ending AUM is 54.50, up 9.0%, and average AUM is 52.25. Revenue is 0.235 and operating profit 0.082. If the fee rate fell to 0.40% with costs unchanged, revenue would be 0.209 and profit 0.056, a fall of −31.7% in profit.
