Current Ratio

BeginnerFinance FoundationsAlso known as: liquidity ratio, working capital ratio, short-term liquidity
← Currency Swap Cash Flows and ValueCurrent Yield and Yield to Call →

Finds: short-term liquidity.

When to use it: Use it to judge whether a company can pay its short-term bills. A ratio above 1 means current assets cover current liabilities.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Current ratio\displaystyle \text{Current ratio}=Current assetsCurrent liabilities\displaystyle = \frac{\text{Current assets}}{\text{Current liabilities}}

In words: Current ratio = Current assets / Current liabilities

What each symbol means

Current assetsCash and items expected to turn into cash within a yearmoney
Current liabilitiesDebts and bills due within a yearmoney

Worked example

A company has current assets of 600 and current liabilities of 400. What is its current ratio?

Current ratio600 ÷ 4001.50
The answer

The current ratio is 1.50, so each 1 of short-term debt is covered by 1.50 of current assets.
