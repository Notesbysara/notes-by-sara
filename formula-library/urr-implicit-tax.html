Capital Controls: Reserve Requirement Implicit Tax

Intermediate

Also known as: capital controls, unremunerated reserve requirement, Tobin tax, cost of controls, Chile encaje

The effective annual cost of a capital control. Use it to see why short-term capital is hit hardest by controls. A requirement to deposit part of an inflow for a year is the same as a fixed fee, and that fee is far heavier per year on a 3-month loan than on a 3-year one. A small transaction tax works in the same way. These measures aim to discourage hot money but can also raise costs for firms, and they tend to be evaded over time.

The formula

τURR=i k TresTloan,τTobin=t 365h\tau_{URR}=i\,k\,\frac{T_{res}}{T_{loan}},\quad \tau_{Tobin}=t\,\frac{365}{h}

Implicit tax of an unremunerated reserve requirement (annualized) = interest rate x share of the inflow that must be deposited x (years the deposit is held / years of the loan); Transaction tax annualized = tax per transaction x 365 / holding days

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
iMarket interest rateper year
kShare of each capital inflow that must be deposited with the central bank at no interestpercent
T_res, T_loanYears the deposit must be held and years the funding lastsyears
t, hTax rate per transaction and the holding period in dayspercent, days

Worked example

A rule requires 30% of inflows to be deposited for one year at no interest. Market rates are 5%. Compare a 3-month loan with a 3-year loan, and a 0.1% transaction tax on a 5-day trade.

  1. Implicit tax, short loan0.05 x 0.3 x 1 / 0.25= 6.00%
  2. Implicit tax, long loan0.05 x 0.3 x 1 / 3= 0.50%
  3. Transaction tax annualized for the short hold0.001 x 365 / 5= 7.3%
  4. Transaction tax annualized for a one-year hold0.001 x 365 / 365= 0.10%

For a 3-month loan the implicit tax is 6.00% a year, and for a 3-year loan it is 0.50%. A 0.1% tax on a 5-day trade is 7.3% annualized, and on a 365-day holding it is 0.10%.
