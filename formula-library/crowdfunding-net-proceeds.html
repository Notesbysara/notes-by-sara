Crowdfunding: Net Proceeds after Fees

Beginner

Also known as: reward crowdfunding, platform fee, payment processing fee, campaign goal

What a crowdfunding campaign must raise to fund a project. Use it to set a campaign goal. Fees and failed payments mean you receive less than the headline total, and you still have to pay for rewards and delivery. Set the goal from the amount you need after fees and costs, not from the amount you hope to see on the page.

The formula

Net=R (1−p−c)−f N,Goalgross=G+fN1−p−c\text{Net}=R\,(1-p-c)-f\,N,\quad \text{Goal}_{gross}=\frac{G+fN}{1-p-c}

Net proceeds = money raised x (1 - platform fee - payment fee) - fixed fee per backer x number of backers; Raise needed to receive a target G = (G + fixed fees) / (1 - platform fee - payment fee)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
RTotal pledgedmoney
p, cPlatform fee and payment processing fee as a share of the amountpercent
f, NFixed fee per payment and number of backersmoney, count

Worked example

A campaign raises 62,000 from 620 backers. The platform takes 5%, payments cost 2.9% plus 0.30 each. The creator needs 50,000 after fees.

  1. Total fees62000 x (0.05+0.029)+0.3 x 620= 5,084.00
  2. Net proceeds62000-5084= 56,916.00
  3. Raise needed for the target(50000+0.3 x 620) / (1-0.05-0.029)= 54,490.77
  4. Net at or above target? (1 = yes)1 if 56916>=50000 else 0= 1

Fees total 5,084.00, so net proceeds are 56,916.00. To receive 50,000 after fees from 620 backers, the campaign needs to raise 54,490.77. The net proceeds are 1 the target (1 = at or above, 0 = below).
