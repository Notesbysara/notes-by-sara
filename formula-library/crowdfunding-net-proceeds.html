Crowdfunding: Net Proceeds after Fees

BeginnerFintech & Digital FinanceAlso known as: reward crowdfunding, platform fee, payment processing fee, campaign goal
← Cross-Border Payment All-In CostCrypto ETF vs Direct Holding: Fee Drag and Cost →

Finds: what a crowdfunding campaign must raise to fund a project.

When to use it: Use it to set a campaign goal. Fees and failed payments mean you receive less than the headline total, and you still have to pay for rewards and delivery. Set the goal from the amount you need after fees and costs, not from the amount you hope to see on the page.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Net\displaystyle \text{Net}=R (1−p−c)−f N\displaystyle =R\,(1-p-c)-f\,NGoalgross\displaystyle \text{Goal}_{gross}=G+fN1−p−c\displaystyle =\frac{G+fN}{1-p-c}

In words: Net proceeds = money raised x (1 − platform fee − payment fee) − fixed fee per backer x number of backers; Raise needed to receive a target G = (G + fixed fees) / (1 − platform fee − payment fee)

What each symbol means

RTotal pledgedmoney
p, cPlatform fee and payment processing fee as a share of the amountpercent
f, NFixed fee per payment and number of backersmoney, count

Worked example

A campaign raises 62,000 from 620 backers. The platform takes 5%, payments cost 2.9% plus 0.30 each. The creator needs 50,000 after fees.

Total fees62,000 × (0.05 + 0.029) + 0.3 × 6205,084
Net proceeds62,000 − 5,08456,916
Raise needed for the target(50,000 + 0.3 × 620) ÷ (1 − 0.05 − 0.029)54,490.77
Net at or above target? (11 if 56,916>=50,000 else 01
The answer

Fees total 5,084, so net proceeds are 56,916. To receive 50,000 after fees from 620 backers, the campaign needs to raise 54,490.77. The net proceeds are 1 the target (1 = at or above, 0 = below).
