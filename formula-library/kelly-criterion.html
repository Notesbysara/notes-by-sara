Kelly Criterion

Intermediate

Also known as: bet sizing, optimal fraction, position sizing, growth optimal, half Kelly

How much of your capital to put on a bet with an edge. Use it to size repeated bets when you think you have an edge. Betting more than Kelly lowers long-run growth and raises the risk of ruin; betting twice Kelly gives zero growth. Because the win probability is only an estimate, most practitioners use half Kelly or less. A zero or negative answer means there is no edge and the right stake is zero.

The formula

f∗=p−1−pb,g(f)=pln⁡(1+bf)+(1−p)ln⁡(1−f)f^{*}=p-\frac{1-p}{b},\quad g(f)=p\ln(1+bf)+(1-p)\ln(1-f)

Best fraction of capital to stake = p - (1 - p) / b, where p is the chance of winning and b is the net payoff per 1 staked; Growth rate = p x ln(1 + b x f) + (1 - p) x ln(1 - f)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
pProbability of winningpercent
bNet odds: profit per 1 staked if you win (you lose the 1 otherwise)number
f*Fraction of capital that maximizes long-run growthpercent
gExpected log growth per betpercent

Worked example

A trade wins 55% of the time. A win pays 1 for each 1 staked and a loss costs the 1 staked.

  1. Kelly fraction = p - (1 - p) / b0.55-(1-0.55) / 1= 10.00%
  2. Growth per bet at Kelly0.55 x ln(1+1 x 0.1)+(1-0.55) x ln(1-0.1)= 0.501%
  3. Half Kelly0.1 / 2= 5.00%
  4. Growth per bet at half Kelly0.55 x ln(1+1 x 0.05)+(1-0.55) x ln(1-0.05)= 0.375%
  5. Share of Kelly growth kept by half Kelly0.00375261 / 0.00500837= 74.9%
  6. Double Kelly2 x 0.1= 20.00%
  7. Growth per bet at double Kelly0.55 x ln(1+1 x 0.2)+(1-0.55) x ln(1-0.2)= -0.014%

The Kelly fraction is 10.00%, so stake 10.00% of capital each time. That gives expected growth of 0.501% per bet. Half Kelly (5.00%) gives 0.375%, which is 74.9% of the growth with much smaller swings. Betting 20.00% (double) gives -0.014%, no growth at all.
