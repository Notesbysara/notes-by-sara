Maximum Drawdown

BeginnerInvestments & Portfolio TheoryAlso known as: max drawdown, peak to trough, worst loss, drawdown
← Martingale Check for Discounted PricesMBS Pass-Through Cash Flow for One Month →

Finds: the worst peak-to-trough loss.

When to use it: Use it to see the worst loss an investor would have suffered by buying at a peak and selling at the next low. It shows pain that an average or a standard deviation hides.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

MDD\displaystyle MDD=max⁡t(Peakt−VtPeakt)\displaystyle =\max_{t}\left(\frac{\text{Peak}_{t}-V_{t}}{\text{Peak}_{t}}\right)

In words: Maximum drawdown = the largest fall from a previous peak, as a percentage of that peak

What each symbol means

V_tValue of the investment at time tmoney
Peak_tHighest value reached up to time tmoney
MDDLargest peak-to-trough loss over the periodpercent

Worked example

A fund's value over seven dates was 100, 110, 105, 120, 90, 100 and 130. What was its maximum drawdown?

Maximum drawdown25%
The answer

The worst fall was 25%, from the peak of 120 to the low of 90. The fund then recovered to a new high.
