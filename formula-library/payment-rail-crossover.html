Payment Rails: Percentage Fee vs Flat Fee Crossover

Beginner

Also known as: card networks, UPI, real-time payments, instant payment cost, flat fee vs percentage fee

Which rail is cheaper at a given payment size. Use it to choose a rail by payment size. Card networks charge mostly a percentage; many real-time rails charge a small flat fee or nothing, so they are cheaper for large payments, while a flat fee can be worse for tiny ones. Some systems, such as UPI in India, are free to the user, which changes who bears the cost.

The formula

Ccard=a mc+fc,Crtp=a mr+fr,a∗=fr−fcmc−mrC_{card}=a\,m_{c}+f_{c},\quad C_{rtp}=a\,m_{r}+f_{r},\quad a^{*}=\frac{f_{r}-f_{c}}{m_{c}-m_{r}}

Cost on a card network = amount x percentage fee + fixed fee; Cost on a real-time (account-to-account) rail = amount x percentage fee + fixed fee (often a small flat fee or zero); Crossover amount a* = (flat fee on rail B - flat fee on rail A) / (percentage on A - percentage on B)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
m_c, f_cPercentage and fixed fee on the card networkpercent and money
m_r, f_rPercentage and fixed fee on the real-time railpercent and money
a*Payment size where the two cost the samemoney

Worked example

Cards cost 1.8% plus 0.10. A real-time bank transfer costs 0.25 flat. At what amount do they cost the same, and what are the costs on 20 and 200?

  1. Crossover amount(0.25-0.1) / (0.018-0)= 8.33
  2. Card cost on 2020 x 0.018+0.1= 0.46
  3. Real-time cost on 2020 x 0+0.25= 0.25
  4. Card cost on 200200 x 0.018+0.1= 3.70
  5. Real-time cost on 200200 x 0+0.25= 0.25

The two cost the same at an amount of 8.33. On a 20 payment the card costs 0.46 and the transfer 0.25; on 200 the card costs 3.70 and the transfer 0.25. The transfer is cheaper for amounts above the crossover.
