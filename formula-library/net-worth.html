Net Worth and Balance Sheet Ratios

Beginner

Also known as: personal balance sheet, assets minus liabilities, solvency ratio, debt to asset ratio

What you own minus what you owe. Use it once or twice a year to see where you stand. Net worth is a snapshot, so watch the direction over time, not one number. A young person with student debt may start negative. Count assets at realistic sale value, not what you paid.

The formula

Net worth=Assets−Liabilities,Debt-to-asset=LiabilitiesAssets\text{Net worth}=\text{Assets}-\text{Liabilities},\quad \text{Debt-to-asset}=\frac{\text{Liabilities}}{\text{Assets}}

Net worth = total assets - total liabilities; Debt-to-asset ratio = total liabilities / total assets; Solvency ratio = net worth / total assets

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
AssetsEverything you own, at what it could be sold for: cash, investments, retirement accounts, home, carmoney
LiabilitiesEverything you owe: mortgage, loans, card balancesmoney

Worked example

Assets: cash 6,000, investments 24,000, retirement accounts 41,000, home 210,000, car 9,000. Liabilities: mortgage 160,000, car loan 6,000, card balance 2,000.

  1. Total assets= 290,000.00
  2. Total liabilities= 168,000.00
  3. Net worth = assets - liabilities290000-168000= 122,000.00
  4. Debt-to-asset ratio168000 / 290000= 57.9%
  5. Solvency ratio = net worth / assets122000 / 290000= 42.1%

Total assets are 290,000.00 and total liabilities are 168,000.00, so net worth is 122,000.00. Debts are 57.9% of assets, and 42.1% of what you own is truly yours.
