Simple Interest

Beginner

Also known as: simple interest formula, interest on principal only

Interest earned and total amount. Use it when interest is paid only on the original amount and never on interest already earned. Common for short loans and some bonds.

The formula

I=P×r×tI = P \times r \times t
A=P(1+rt)A = P(1 + rt)

I = P x r x t

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
IInterest earnedmoney
PPrincipal, the amount you start withmoney
rInterest rate per year, as a decimalper year
tTimeyears
ATotal amount at the end (principal plus interest)money

Worked example

You deposit 1,000 at 5% a year simple interest for 10 years. How much interest do you earn, and what is the total?

  1. Interest = P x r x t1000 x 0.05 x 10= 500.00
  2. Total = P + interest1000+500= 1,500.00

Interest earned is 500.00 and the total is 1,500.00.
