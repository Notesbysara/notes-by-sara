Personal Budget and Net Worth Dashboard

Beginner

Also known as: build a personal budget, net worth tracker, savings rate, months of runway, debt to assets

Where you stand financially this month. Use it as the monthly check on your finances. The savings rate shows how much you add, the net worth shows where you stand, and the runway shows how long you could cover costs without income. Update it once a month with real numbers, in the same categories each time, so the trend is clear.

The formula

Savings rate=I−EI,NW=A−L,Runway=AliquidE\text{Savings rate}=\frac{I-E}{I},\quad NW=A-L,\quad \text{Runway}=\frac{A_{liquid}}{E}

Savings rate = (income - spending) / income; Net worth = total assets - total liabilities; Runway in months = liquid assets / monthly spending; Debt-to-assets = liabilities / assets; Change in net worth = savings + investment gains - debt interest and fees

Worked example

Monthly income is 5,200. Spending items are 1,400, 520, 380, 260, 310, 640 and 190. Assets are cash 3,200, savings 14,500, investments 22,000 and other 9,800. Debts are 11,200 (loan) and 2,400 (card).

  1. Total spending= 3,700.00
  2. Monthly savings5200-3700= 1,500.00
  3. Savings rate1500 / 5200= 28.8%
  4. Net worth= 35,900.00
  5. Liquid assets (cash and savings)[3200, 14500, 22000, 9800][0]+[3200, 14500, 22000, 9800][1]= 17,700.00
  6. Runway in months17700 / 3700= 4.8
  7. Debts as a share of assets= 27.5%

Spending is 3,700.00 and savings are 1,500.00, a savings rate of 28.8%. Net worth is 35,900.00. The cash and savings of 17,700.00 would cover 4.8 months of spending. Debts are 27.5% of assets.
