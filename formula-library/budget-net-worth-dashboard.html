Personal Budget and Net Worth Dashboard

BeginnerPractical Skills & CapstoneAlso known as: build a personal budget, net worth tracker, savings rate, months of runway, debt to assets
← PerpetuityPMI Diffusion Index and Data Surprise →

Finds: where you stand financially this month.

When to use it: Use it as the monthly check on your finances. The savings rate shows how much you add, the net worth shows where you stand, and the runway shows how long you could cover costs without income. Update it once a month with real numbers, in the same categories each time, so the trend is clear.

The formula

Savings rate\displaystyle \text{Savings rate}=I−EI\displaystyle =\frac{I-E}{I}NW\displaystyle NW=A−L\displaystyle =A-LRunway\displaystyle \text{Runway}=AliquidE\displaystyle =\frac{A_{liquid}}{E}

In words: Savings rate = (income − spending) / income; Net worth = total assets − total liabilities; Runway in months = liquid assets / monthly spending; Debt-to-assets = liabilities / assets; Change in net worth = savings + investment gains − debt interest and fees

What each symbol means

I, EMonthly income and total monthly spendingmoney
A, A_liquidTotal assets and the part that is easy to turn into cashmoney
LTotal debtsmoney

Worked example

Monthly income is 5,200. Spending items are 1,400, 520, 380, 260, 310, 640 and 190. Assets are cash 3,200, savings 14,500, investments 22,000 and other 9,800. Debts are 11,200 (loan) and 2,400 (card).

Debts as a share of assets27.5%
The answer

Spending is 3,700 and savings are 1,500, a savings rate of 28.8%. Net worth is 35,900. The cash and savings of 17,700 would cover 4.8 months of spending. Debts are 27.5% of assets.
