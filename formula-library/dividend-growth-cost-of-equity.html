Cost of Equity (Dividend Growth)

Intermediate

Also known as: dividend discount cost of equity, Gordon cost of equity, D1/P0 + g

Required return on equity from dividends. Use it for a stable dividend-paying company as a cross-check on CAPM. It rests on the Gordon growth model, so it only suits companies with steady dividend growth.

The formula

re=D1P0+gr_{e}=\frac{D_{1}}{P_{0}}+g

Cost of equity = Next dividend / Current price + Growth rate

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
r_eCost of equityper year
D_1Dividend expected next yearmoney per share
P_0Current share pricemoney per share
gExpected steady growth rate of dividendsper year

Worked example

A share costs 40, is expected to pay 2 next year, and dividends are expected to grow 4% a year. What is the cost of equity?

  1. Dividend yield = D1 / P0d1 / 40= 5.00%
  2. Cost of equity = dividend yield + g0.05+0.04= 9.00%

The cost of equity is 9.00%: a dividend yield of 5.00% plus growth of 4%.
