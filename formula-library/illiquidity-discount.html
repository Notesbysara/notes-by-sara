Discount for Lack of Marketability (Private Companies)

Intermediate

Also known as: DLOM, private company discount, illiquidity discount, control premium

Value of a private firm from listed comparables. Use it to value a private company from public comparables. Private shares are harder to sell, so they are worth less than similar listed ones. A control premium may be added back if the buyer gets control.

The formula

Vprivate=Vmarketable×(1−DLOM)V_{private}=V_{marketable}\times(1-DLOM)

Private company value = Marketable minority value x (1 - Discount for lack of marketability)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
V_marketableValue if the shares could be freely traded, from comparable public companiesmoney
DLOMDiscount for lack of marketability, reflecting how hard private shares are to sellpercent
V_privateValue of the private company sharesmoney

Worked example

A private company would be worth 1,000 if it were listed. A discount for lack of marketability of 25% applies. A buyer obtaining control would pay a 20% control premium on the discounted value. What is the value?

  1. Minority private value = V x (1 - DLOM)1000 x (1-0.25)= 750.00
  2. Value with control = minority value x (1 + control premium)750 x (1+0.2)= 900.00

The minority private value is 750.00. A buyer who gets control would pay about 900.00.
