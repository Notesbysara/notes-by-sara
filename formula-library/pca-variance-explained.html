Principal Component Analysis: Variance Explained (Two Assets)

Intermediate

Also known as: PCA, eigenvalues, factor models, first principal component

How much of the risk one common factor explains. Use it to find how many independent sources of risk drive a set of assets. The first component is the direction of largest variance, often a market or level factor. The eigenvalues add up to total variance, so each one divided by the sum is the share explained. This closed form is for two assets; larger sets use software but the reading is the same.

The formula

λ1,2=σ12+σ222±(σ12−σ222)2+σ122,Share1=λ1λ1+λ2\lambda_{1,2}=\frac{\sigma_{1}^{2}+\sigma_{2}^{2}}{2}\pm\sqrt{\left(\frac{\sigma_{1}^{2}-\sigma_{2}^{2}}{2}\right)^{2}+\sigma_{12}^{2}},\quad \text{Share}_{1}=\frac{\lambda_{1}}{\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}}

Eigenvalues of the covariance matrix = (var1 + var2) / 2 plus or minus sqrt(((var1 - var2) / 2)^2 + cov^2); Share of variance explained by the first component = first eigenvalue / (sum of eigenvalues)

Worked example

Two assets have volatilities of 20% and 12% and a correlation of 0.6.

  1. Covariance = rho x s1 x s20.6 x 0.2 x 0.12= 0.014400
  2. First eigenvalue(0.2^2+0.12^2) / 2+sqrt(((0.2^2-0.12^2) / 2)^2+cov^2)= 0.046467
  3. Second eigenvalue(0.2^2+0.12^2) / 2-sqrt(((0.2^2-0.12^2) / 2)^2+cov^2)= 0.007933
  4. Variance share of the first component0.0464666 / (0.0464666+0.00793345)= 85.42%
  5. Variance share of the second component0.00793345 / (0.0464666+0.00793345)= 14.58%
  6. Angle of the first component = 0.5 x atan2(2 cov, var1 - var2), in degrees0.5 x atan2(2 x cov, 0.2^2-0.12^2) x 180 / 3.141592653589793= 24.2

The covariance is 0.014400. The two eigenvalues are 0.046467 and 0.007933, so the first component explains 85.42% of total variance and the second 14.58%. The loading angle of the first component is 24.2 degrees from asset 1, tilted toward the riskier asset.
