Infrastructure Project: DSCR and Loan Life Cover

Intermediate

Also known as: infrastructure investing, project finance cover ratios, LLCR, DSCR lock-up, CFADS

Whether project cash flows cover the debt. Use it to test whether an infrastructure project (a toll road, solar farm, pipeline) can carry its debt. Lenders look at the cheapest year (minimum DSCR) and at the whole loan life (LLCR). A margin above 1 means income covers payments even if revenue falls short; a cover below 1 means equity must top up or lenders take a loss.

The formula

DSCRt=CFADStDSt,LLCR=∑tCFADSt/(1+r)tDebtDSCR_{t}=\frac{CFADS_{t}}{DS_{t}},\quad LLCR=\frac{\sum_{t}CFADS_{t}/(1+r)^{t}}{\text{Debt}}

Cash flow available for debt service (CFADS) = operating cash flow after tax and capex; DSCR in year t = CFADS / (interest + principal due); LLCR = present value of CFADS over the loan life at the loan rate / debt outstanding; A project typically needs a minimum DSCR near 1.2 to 1.4 for stable assets, and pays no dividends below a lock-up level

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
CFADS_tCash flow available to pay lenders in year tmoney
DS_tDebt service (interest plus principal) due in year tmoney
rInterest rate on the loan, used to discountpercent

Worked example

A project expects CFADS of 120, 125, 130, 135, 140 and 145 over 6 years against debt service of 90 a year. The loan rate is 6%.

  1. DSCR per year= 1.33, 1.39, 1.44, 1.50, 1.56, 1.61
  2. Minimum DSCRmin([1.33333, 1.38889, 1.44444, 1.5, 1.55556, 1.61111])= 1.33
  3. Average DSCR= 1.47
  4. Debt (present value of debt service at the loan rate)= 442.56
  5. LLCR = PV of CFADS / debt= 1.46
  6. Minimum DSCR if CFADS falls 20%1.33333 x 0.8= 1.07
  7. Still at least 1.0? (1 = yes)1 if 1.06667>=1 else 0= 1

The yearly DSCRs are 1.33, 1.39, 1.44, 1.50, 1.56, 1.61. The lowest is 1.33, the average is 1.47. Debt is worth 442.56 at 6% and the LLCR is 1.46. A cut of 20% in CFADS gives a minimum DSCR of 1.07, which is 1 (1 = still at least 1.0).
