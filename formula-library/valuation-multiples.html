Valuation Multiples (EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales, P/E)

Beginner

Also known as: EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales, P/E ratio, price earnings, multiples

What the market pays for earnings and sales. Use them to compare what the market pays for a company's earnings and sales. EV multiples suit comparing firms with different debt levels, and P/E is a quick measure of what investors pay for each unit of profit.

The formula

EVEBITDA,EVSales,PE=PriceEPS\frac{EV}{EBITDA},\quad \frac{EV}{Sales},\quad \frac{P}{E}=\frac{\text{Price}}{EPS}

EV/EBITDA = Enterprise value / EBITDA; EV/Sales = Enterprise value / Sales; P/E = Share price / Earnings per share

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
EVEnterprise value, equity plus net debtmoney
EBITDAEarnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortizationmoney
SalesRevenue for the yearmoney
PriceShare pricemoney
EPSEarnings per sharemoney

Worked example

A company has an enterprise value of 5,000, EBITDA of 500, sales of 4,000, a share price of 40 and EPS of 2.50. What are its three multiples?

  1. EV/EBITDA = EV / EBITDA5000 / 500= 10.00
  2. EV/Sales = EV / sales5000 / 4000= 1.25
  3. P/E = price / EPS40 / 2.5= 16.00

The EV/EBITDA multiple is 10.00, EV/Sales is 1.25 and the P/E ratio is 16.00.
