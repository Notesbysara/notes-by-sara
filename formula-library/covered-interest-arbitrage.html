Covered Interest Parity and Arbitrage

IntermediateInternational FinanceAlso known as: interest rate parity, CIP, covered arbitrage, forward premium deviation
← Covered Bond OvercollateralizationCox-Ingersoll-Ross Moments and Feller Condition →

Finds: whether a forward rate offers a free profit.

When to use it: Use it to test whether the forward rate is in line with interest rates. If the market forward is higher than parity, buying the foreign currency spot, investing it and selling it forward locks in more than the domestic deposit pays, a risk-free profit. In real markets the gap is usually smaller than costs, except in stress, when funding frictions can open a persistent gap (the cross-currency basis).

The formula

FS\displaystyle \frac{F}{S}=1+rdT1+rfT\displaystyle =\frac{1+r_{d}T}{1+r_{f}T}Profit\displaystyle \text{Profit}=KS(1+rfT)Fmkt−K(1+rdT)\displaystyle =\frac{K}{S}(1+r_{f}T)F_{mkt}-K(1+r_{d}T)

In words: Parity says the forward rate F = S x (1 + domestic rate T) / (1 + foreign rate T). If the market forward differs, borrow where it is cheaper, convert at spot, invest abroad, and sell the proceeds forward: profit = foreign proceeds at the market forward − domestic proceeds. Compare: invest K in domestic currency = K x (1 + r_d T)

Worked example

EUR/USD spot is 1.0850, the 180-day USD rate is 5%, the EUR rate 3%, and the market 180-day forward is 1.0950. You have 1,000,000 USD.

Parity forward1.085 × (1 + 0.05 × 180 ÷ 360) ÷ (1 + 0.03 × 180 ÷ 360)1.09569
Market forward minus parity, in pips(1.095 − 1.09569) × 10,000−6.9
USD deposit proceeds1,000,000 × (1 + 0.05 × 180 ÷ 360)1,025,000
Covered foreign deposit: convert, invest, sell forward1,000,000 ÷ 1.085 × (1 + 0.03 × 180 ÷ 360) × 1.0951,024,354.84
Profitcov − 1,025,000−645.16
The answer

Parity gives a forward of 1.09569, but the market quotes 1.0950, −6.9 pips higher. Investing in USD returns 1,025,000. Converting to euros, investing at 3% and selling forward gives 1,024,354.84, a risk-free profit of −645.16 (a negative number would mean the opposite loop is the profitable one).
