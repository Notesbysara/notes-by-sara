Regular Saving Needed to Reach a Goal

IntermediateFinance FoundationsAlso known as: sinking fund, how much to save each year, savings goal payment, required deposit
← Regression Line (Alpha and Beta)Reinsurance: Quota Share and Excess of Loss →

Finds: the regular deposit needed for a savings goal.

When to use it: Use it when you have a savings goal and a deadline and want to know the regular deposit that gets you there. It is the future value of an annuity formula turned around.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

PMT\displaystyle PMT=FV r(1+r)n−1\displaystyle = \frac{FV\,r}{(1+r)^{n}-1}

In words: PMT = FV x r / ((1 + r)^n − 1)

What each symbol means

PMTDeposit needed at the end of each periodmoney
FVTarget amount you want to reachmoney
rInterest rate per period, as a decimalper period
nNumber of depositsperiods

Worked example

You want 50,000 in 10 years. You can earn 6% a year and will deposit at the end of each year. How much must you deposit each year?

Annuity factor((1 + 0.06)^10 − 1) ÷ 0.0613.1808
PMT50,000 ÷ 13.18083,793.40
Total deposited3,793.4 × 1037,933.98
Interest earned50,000 − 37,93412,066.02
The answer

You need to deposit 3,793.40 each year. That is 37,933.98 in total, and the interest covers the other 12,066.02.
