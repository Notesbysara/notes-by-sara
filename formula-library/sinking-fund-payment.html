Regular Saving Needed to Reach a Goal

Intermediate

Also known as: sinking fund, how much to save each year, savings goal payment, required deposit

The regular deposit needed for a savings goal. Use it when you have a savings goal and a deadline and want to know the regular deposit that gets you there. It is the future value of an annuity formula turned around.

The formula

PMT=FV r(1+r)n−1PMT = \frac{FV\,r}{(1+r)^{n}-1}

PMT = FV x r / ((1 + r)^n - 1)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
PMTDeposit needed at the end of each periodmoney
FVTarget amount you want to reachmoney
rInterest rate per period, as a decimalper period
nNumber of depositsperiods

Worked example

You want 50,000 in 10 years. You can earn 6% a year and will deposit at the end of each year. How much must you deposit each year?

  1. Annuity factor = ((1 + r)^n - 1) / r((1+0.06)^10-1) / 0.06= 13.1808
  2. PMT = FV / annuity factor50000 / 13.1808= 3,793.40
  3. Total deposited = PMT x n3793.4 x 10= 37,933.98
  4. Interest earned = FV - total deposited50000-37934= 12,066.02

You need to deposit 3,793.40 each year. That is 37,933.98 in total, and the interest covers the other 12,066.02.
