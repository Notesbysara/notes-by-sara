Corporate Bond Yield Build-Up

Beginner

Also known as: risk free rate plus spread, corporate bond pricing, yield components

Yield and price of a corporate bond built from the government curve. Use it to understand what makes up a corporate bond yield, or to price a new corporate bond from the government curve. The price then follows from the bond price formula at that yield.

The formula

ycorp=ygov+Credit spread+Liquidity premiumy_{corp}=y_{gov}+\text{Credit spread}+\text{Liquidity premium}

Corporate bond yield = Government yield for the same maturity + Credit spread + Liquidity premium

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
y_govYield on a government bond of the same maturityper year
Credit spreadExtra yield for the risk of defaultper year
Liquidity premiumExtra yield for being harder to sellper year

Worked example

A 10-year government bond yields 4.1%. A 10-year corporate bond needs a credit spread of 1.4% and a liquidity premium of 0.2%. A 1,000 face, 5.5% annual-coupon bond of that issuer is being priced. What yield and price?

  1. Required yield = government + spread + premium0.041+0.014+0.002= 5.70%
  2. Price with annual coupons= 985.07

The required yield is 5.70%. At that yield the bond is worth 985.07, below its face value because the coupon is lower than the required yield.
