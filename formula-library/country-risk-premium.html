Country Risk Premium

IntermediateCorporate FinanceAlso known as: CRP, sovereign spread method, emerging market risk premium
← Counterparty Exposure, Netting and Potential Future ExposureCovariance →

Finds: extra return for country risk.

When to use it: Use it to add a premium for political and economic risk when valuing a company in a riskier country. The volatility ratio scales a bond spread up to an equity-sized premium.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

CRP\displaystyle CRP=Sovereign spread×σequityσbond\displaystyle =\text{Sovereign spread}\times\frac{\sigma_{equity}}{\sigma_{bond}}re\displaystyle r_{e}=rf+β ERP+CRP\displaystyle =r_{f}+\beta\,ERP+CRP

In words: CRP = Sovereign default spread x (Equity volatility / Bond volatility); Cost of equity = rf + beta x ERP + CRP

What each symbol means

CRPExtra return required for the risk of operating in a countryper year
Sovereign spreadYield gap between the country's government bond and a safe benchmarkper year
sigma_equityVolatility of the country's stock marketper year
sigma_bondVolatility of the country's government bondper year

Worked example

A country's sovereign spread is 3%, its equity market volatility is 24% and its bond volatility is 16%. The risk-free rate is 3%, the equity risk premium is 5.5% and beta is 1.2. What is the cost of equity?

Volatility ratio0.24 ÷ 0.161.50
CRP0.03 × 1.54.50%
Cost of equity0.03 + 1.2 × 0.055 + 0.04514.10%
The answer

The country risk premium is 4.50%, so the cost of equity is 14.10%.
