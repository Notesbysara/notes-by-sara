Country Risk Premium

Intermediate

Also known as: CRP, sovereign spread method, emerging market risk premium

Extra return for country risk. Use it to add a premium for political and economic risk when valuing a company in a riskier country. The volatility ratio scales a bond spread up to an equity-sized premium.

The formula

CRP=Sovereign spread×σequityσbond,re=rf+β ERP+CRPCRP=\text{Sovereign spread}\times\frac{\sigma_{equity}}{\sigma_{bond}},\quad r_{e}=r_{f}+\beta\,ERP+CRP

CRP = Sovereign default spread x (Equity volatility / Bond volatility); Cost of equity = rf + beta x ERP + CRP

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
CRPExtra return required for the risk of operating in a countryper year
Sovereign spreadYield gap between the country's government bond and a safe benchmarkper year
sigma_equityVolatility of the country's stock marketper year
sigma_bondVolatility of the country's government bondper year

Worked example

A country's sovereign spread is 3%, its equity market volatility is 24% and its bond volatility is 16%. The risk-free rate is 3%, the equity risk premium is 5.5% and beta is 1.2. What is the cost of equity?

  1. Volatility ratio = equity / bond0.24 / 0.16= 1.50
  2. CRP = spread x ratio0.03 x 1.5= 4.50%
  3. Cost of equity = rf + beta x ERP + CRP0.03+1.2 x 0.055+0.045= 14.10%

The country risk premium is 4.50%, so the cost of equity is 14.10%.
