Loan Payment (Amortization)

Intermediate

Also known as: mortgage payment, monthly payment formula, amortization formula, PMT

The regular payment on a loan. Use it to find the fixed payment on a loan that is repaid in equal installments, such as a mortgage or a car loan.

The formula

PMT=P r1−(1+r)−nPMT = \frac{P\,r}{1-(1+r)^{-n}}

PMT = P x r / (1 - (1 + r)^-n)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
PMTEqual payment made every periodmoney
PLoan amount, the principal borrowedmoney
rInterest rate per payment period, as a decimalper period
nTotal number of paymentsperiods

Worked example

You borrow 200,000 at 6% a year for 30 years, with monthly payments. What is each payment, and how much interest do you pay in total?

  1. Rate per period = annual rate / payments per year0.06 / 12= 0.5000%
  2. Number of payments = years x payments per year30 x 12= 360
  3. PMT = P x r / (1 - (1 + r)^-n)200000 x 0.005 / (1-(1+0.005)^(-360))= 1,199.10
  4. Total paid = PMT x n1199.1 x 360= 431,676.38
  5. Total interest = total paid - loan431676-200000= 231,676.38

Each monthly payment is 1,199.10. Over 30 years you pay 431,676.38 in total, of which 231,676.38 is interest.
