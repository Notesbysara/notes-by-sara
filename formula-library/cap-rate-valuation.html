Net Operating Income and Cap-Rate Valuation

BeginnerAlternative InvestmentsAlso known as: NOI, capitalization rate, direct capitalization, property value from income
← Net Interest MarginNet Present Value (NPV) →

Finds: property value from its income.

When to use it: Use it for a quick market value of an income-producing building. Dividing a steady year of income by a yield gives the price, the property-world version of a perpetuity. Cap rates move with interest rates and risk, so a small change in the rate moves value a lot. It ignores growth, so it fits stable properties best.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

NOI\displaystyle NOI=GPI(1−v)−OpEx\displaystyle =GPI(1-v)-OpExV\displaystyle V=NOIc\displaystyle =\frac{NOI}{c}c\displaystyle c=NOIV\displaystyle =\frac{NOI}{V}

In words: Net operating income = potential rent x (1 − vacancy) − operating expenses (before debt service, tax and depreciation); Value = NOI / cap rate; Cap rate = NOI / value; Debt service coverage = NOI / annual loan payment

What each symbol means

GPIGross potential income if fully letmoney
vVacancy and collection losspercent
OpExProperty tax, insurance, repairs, managementmoney
cCapitalization rate: income yield the market demands for this type of propertypercent

Worked example

A building could collect 500,000 in rent when full. Vacancy is 6% and operating expenses are 180,000. Comparable buildings sell at a 6.5% cap rate. A 60% loan at 5.5% interest-only is offered.

NOI500,000 × (1 − 0.06) − 180,000290,000
Value290,000 ÷ 0.0654,461,538.46
Value if the cap rate rises by 1 percentage point290,000 ÷ (0.065 + 0.01)3,866,666.67
Fall in value3,866,667 ÷ 4,461,538 − 1−13.3%
Loan0.6 × 4,461,5382,676,923.08
Annual interest2,676,923 × 0.055147,230.77
Debt service coverage290,000 ÷ 147,2311.97
The answer

NOI is 290,000. At a 6.5% cap rate the value is 4,461,538.46. A rise of one percentage point in the cap rate cuts the value to 3,866,666.67, a fall of −13.3%. The loan is 2,676,923.08 and interest costs 147,230.77, so the debt service coverage is 1.97.
