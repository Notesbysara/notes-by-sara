After-Tax Return and Tax-Equivalent Yield

Beginner

Also known as: tax equivalent yield, municipal bond yield, tax aware investing, after tax yield

Which of a taxable and a tax-free investment pays more after tax. Use it to compare taxable and tax-free investments on the same footing. The higher your tax rate, the more a tax-free yield is worth.

The formula

rAT=r(1−t),TEY=ymuni1−tr_{AT}=r(1-t),\quad TEY=\frac{y_{muni}}{1-t}

After-tax return = Pre-tax return x (1 - tax rate); Tax-equivalent yield = Tax-free yield / (1 - tax rate)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
rPre-tax return on a taxable investmentper year
tInvestor's tax rate, as a decimaldecimal
y_muniYield on a tax-free investment such as a municipal bondper year
TEYTaxable yield that would leave the investor with the same after-tax incomeper year

Worked example

A taxable bond yields 5% and a tax-free municipal bond yields 3.6%. The investor's tax rate is 32%. Which is better?

  1. After-tax yield of the taxable bond = y x (1 - t)0.05 x (1-0.32)= 3.40%
  2. Tax-equivalent yield of the tax-free bond = m / (1 - t)0.036 / (1-0.32)= 5.29%

The taxable bond leaves 3.40% after tax. The municipal bond is equal to a taxable yield of 5.29%. The municipal bond is better.
