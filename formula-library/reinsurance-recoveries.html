Reinsurance: Quota Share and Excess of Loss

IntermediateBanking & Financial InstitutionsAlso known as: reinsurance, ceded loss, retention, layer
← Regular Saving Needed to Reach a GoalREIT Funds From Operations (FFO) and AFFO →

Finds: what the insurer keeps after reinsurance.

When to use it: Use it to see how reinsurance changes the loss an insurer keeps. A quota share reduces every loss in proportion and gives capital relief. Excess of loss protects against large single losses and leaves small ones with the insurer, and a loss beyond retention plus limit comes back to the insurer. Real programs add reinstatement premiums and exclusions.

The formula

CededQS\displaystyle \text{Ceded}_{QS}=q L\displaystyle =q\,LRecoveryXL\displaystyle \text{Recovery}_{XL}=min⁡ ⁣(max⁡(L−R,0), Limit)\displaystyle =\min\!\left(\max(L-R,0),\,\text{Limit}\right)

In words: Quota share: the reinsurer takes a fixed share q of every loss and premium; Excess of loss: the reinsurer pays the part of a loss above the retention R, up to the limit; Net retained loss = gross loss − recovery

What each symbol means

LGross loss from one eventmoney
qQuota share cededpercent
RRetention: the loss the insurer keeps before the cover paysmoney
LimitMost the excess-of-loss cover paysmoney

Worked example

A 8.0 million loss hits an insurer. It has a 30% quota share, and separately an excess-of-loss cover paying 5.0 million above a 2.0 million retention.

Quota share recovery0.3 × 82.40
Net retained, quota share only8 − 2.45.60
Excess-of-loss recoverymin(max(8 − 2, 0), 5)5
Net retained, excess of loss only8 − 53
Loss above the layer (retention + limit)max(8 − 2 − 5, 0)1
Net retained with XL first, then quota share on what remains(8 − 5) × (1 − 0.3)2.10
The answer

Under the quota share the reinsurer pays 2.40 and the insurer keeps 5.60. Under the excess-of-loss cover the reinsurer pays 5 and the insurer keeps 3. The XL leaves 1 above the layer with the insurer. If both apply, with the quota share on the excess cover's retained loss, the insurer keeps 2.10.
