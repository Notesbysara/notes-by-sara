Financial Independence Number and the 4% Rule

BeginnerPersonal FinanceAlso known as: financial independence, FIRE, 4 percent rule, safe withdrawal rate, 25 times expenses
← Family Office Break-Even SizeFinite Difference: Explicit Scheme Step and Stability →

Finds: the portfolio size that supports your spending and the years to reach it.

When to use it: Use it to estimate how large a portfolio must be to fund spending, and how long saving will take. The 4% rule comes from historical US data for a 30-year retirement; a longer retirement, lower expected returns or high fees point to a lower rate such as 3 to 3.5%. It is a guide, not a guarantee, and it does not include pensions or taxes. Working in real terms keeps the arithmetic free of inflation.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

FI\displaystyle FI=Ew\displaystyle =\frac{E}{w}n\displaystyle n=ln⁡ ⁣[(FI r+s)/(P r+s)]ln⁡(1+r)\displaystyle =\frac{\ln\!\left[(FI\,r+s)/(P\,r+s)\right]}{\ln(1+r)}

In words: Financial independence number = annual spending / safe withdrawal rate (4% gives 25 x spending); Years to get there = ln[(FI x r + s) / (current portfolio x r + s)] / ln(1 + r), where s is the yearly saving and r is the real return

What each symbol means

EAnnual spending in retirementmoney
wSafe withdrawal rate: share of the starting portfolio taken out in year one, then adjusted for inflationpercent
sAmount saved each yearmoney
rReal (after-inflation) return on the portfolioper year
PCurrent portfoliomoney

Worked example

Annual spending is 36,000 and you assume a 4% withdrawal rate. You have 150,000, save 24,000 a year from an income of 80,000, and earn a real return of 5%.

FI number36,000 ÷ 0.04900,000
Progress150,000 ÷ 900,00016.7%
Savings rate24,000 ÷ 80,00030%
Years to reach FImax(ln((900,000 × 0.05 + 24,000) ÷ (150,000 × 0.05 + 24,000)) ÷ ln(1 + 0.05), 0)16.1
FI number at a 3.5% withdrawal rate36,000 ÷ 0.0351,028,571.43
The answer

The financial independence number is 900,000. You are 16.7% of the way there, saving 30.0% of your income. At this pace it takes 16.1 years. At a 3.5% withdrawal rate the number would be 1,028,571.43.
