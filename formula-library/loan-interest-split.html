Interest and Principal in a Loan Payment

Beginner

Also known as: amortization schedule row, how much of my payment is interest, principal portion

Interest versus principal in one payment. Use it to split one loan payment into the part that is interest and the part that reduces the debt. Repeating it row by row builds a full amortization schedule.

The formula

Interest=Bstart×r,Principal=PMT−Interest\text{Interest} = B_{start}\times r, \quad \text{Principal} = PMT-\text{Interest}

Interest = starting balance x r, and Principal repaid = PMT - Interest

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
B_startBalance owed at the start of the periodmoney
rInterest rate per payment period, as a decimalper period
PMTRegular paymentmoney

Worked example

A loan has a balance of 200,000 at 6% a year with monthly payments of 1,199.10. How much of the first payment is interest, and how much repays the debt?

  1. Rate per period = annual rate / payments per year0.06 / 12= 0.5000%
  2. Interest = balance x r200000 x 0.005= 1,000.00
  3. Principal repaid = payment - interest1199.1-1000= 199.10
  4. New balance = balance - principal200000-199.1= 199,800.90

The first payment has 1,000.00 of interest and 199.10 of principal. The new balance is 199,800.90.
