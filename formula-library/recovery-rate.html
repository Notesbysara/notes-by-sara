Recovery Rate and Loss Given Default

Beginner

Also known as: LGD, loss given default, bankruptcy recovery, creditor recovery

What lenders get back after default. Use it to measure what lenders get back when a borrower fails. Senior secured creditors usually recover more than junior or unsecured ones.

The formula

Recovery=Amount recoveredClaim,LGD=1−Recovery\text{Recovery}=\frac{\text{Amount recovered}}{\text{Claim}},\quad LGD=1-\text{Recovery}

Recovery rate = Amount recovered / Claim; Loss given default = 1 - Recovery rate

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
ClaimAmount owed to the creditor at defaultmoney
Amount recoveredValue the creditor actually receives from liquidation or reorganizationmoney
LGDLoss given default, the share of the claim that is lostpercent

Worked example

A bank is owed 100 by a company that goes bankrupt. It recovers 40 in the liquidation. What are the recovery rate and the loss given default?

  1. Recovery rate = recovered / claim40 / 100= 40.00%
  2. LGD = 1 - recovery rate1-0.4= 60.00%
  3. Loss in money = claim - recovered100-40= 60.00

The bank recovers 40.00% and loses 60.00% of its claim, which is 60.00 in money terms.
