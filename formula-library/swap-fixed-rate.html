Interest Rate Swap: Fair Fixed Rate

Intermediate

Also known as: swap rate, par swap rate, plain vanilla swap, pay fixed receive floating

The fixed rate that makes a swap worth zero. Use it to find the fixed rate at which a swap costs nothing to enter. In a plain vanilla swap one side pays a fixed rate and the other pays a floating rate such as SOFR on the same notional. Only the net difference is paid on each date.

The formula

c=1−DFn∑i=1nDFic=\frac{1-DF_{n}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}DF_{i}}

Fair fixed rate = (1 - Discount factor for the last payment date) / Sum of all the discount factors

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
DF_iDiscount factor for payment date i, the value today of 1 received on that datenumber
cFixed rate that gives the swap zero value at the startper year
NotionalAmount on which payments are calculated. It is never exchangedmoney

Worked example

Annual discount factors for years 1 to 4 are 0.9615, 0.9246, 0.8890 and 0.8548. What is the fair fixed rate on a 4-year annual swap with a notional of 10,000,000? If the floating rate for the first year turns out to be 4.5%, what is the first net payment?

  1. Sum of discount factors= 3.6299
  2. Fixed rate = (1 - last discount factor) / sum= 4.000%
  3. First net payment to the fixed payer = notional x (floating - fixed)10,000,000 x (0.045-0.0400011)= 49,988.98

The fair fixed rate is 4.000%. Against a first-year floating rate of 4.5% the fixed payer receives a net 49,988.98 (floating received less fixed paid). If the net were negative, the fixed payer would owe it.
