Impact Investing: Social Return on Investment

IntermediateRegulation, Ethics & ESGAlso known as: impact investing, SROI, impact multiple of money, social value, blended return
← Immunization: Matching Duration to a LiabilityImpermanent Loss for Liquidity Providers →

Finds: the social value created for each unit invested.

When to use it: Use it to compare impact investments with a common yardstick. Social benefits (jobs, health, emissions avoided) are turned into money values, which requires judgment about prices and about additionality, so the ratio is only as good as these assumptions. Show the financial and the impact parts separately so readers can see what they depend on.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

SROI\displaystyle SROI=∑tBt/(1+r)tI\displaystyle =\frac{\sum_{t}B_{t}/(1+r)^{t}}{I}Blended\displaystyle \text{Blended}=PVfin+PVsocialI\displaystyle =\frac{PV_{fin}+PV_{social}}{I}

In words: Social return on investment = present value of social benefits (valued in money, after removing what would have happened anyway) / amount invested; Financial multiple = present value of cash returned / amount invested; Blended multiple = (PV of cash returns + PV of social benefits) / amount invested; Net social value = PV of benefits − investment

What each symbol means

B_tValue of social benefit in year t, net of what would have happened anywaymoney
IAmount investedmoney
rDiscount ratepercent

Worked example

An investor puts in 2,000,000. The program produces social benefits (valued) of 600,000, 700,000, 800,000, 800,000 and 800,000 over 5 years, and returns 200,000 a year in cash. The discount rate is 5%.

PV of social benefits3,182,402.20
SROI3,182,402 ÷ 2,000,0001.59
PV of cash returns865,895.33
Financial multiple865,895 ÷ 2,000,0000.43
Blended multiple(3,182,402 + 865,895) ÷ 2,000,0002.02
Net social value3,182,402 − 2,000,0001,182,402.20
The answer

The present value of social benefits is 3,182,402.20, giving an SROI of 1.59. The financial return has a present value of 865,895.33, a multiple of 0.43. The blended multiple is 2.02, and net social value is 1,182,402.20.
