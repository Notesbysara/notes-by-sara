Venture Capital Method (Required Ownership)

IntermediateCorporate FinanceAlso known as: VC method, target return ownership, venture capital return multiple
← Venture Capital Dilution and Required Exit ValueVIX-Style Variance from Option Prices →

Finds: stake needed to hit a target venture return.

When to use it: Use it to find the stake a venture investor needs today to reach a target return, allowing for dilution from future funding rounds.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Required stake at exit\displaystyle \text{Required stake at exit}=I×MVexit\displaystyle =\frac{I\times M}{V_{exit}}Stake today\displaystyle \text{Stake today}=Required stakeRetention\displaystyle =\frac{\text{Required stake}}{\text{Retention}}

In words: Required stake at exit = Investment x Target multiple / Exit value; Stake needed today = Required stake / Retention

What each symbol means

IInvestment made todaymoney
MTarget return multiple (10 means the investor wants 10 times their money back)number
V_exitExpected value of the company at exitmoney
RetentionShare of the investor's stake that remains after later funding rounds dilute itpercent

Worked example

An investor puts in 2,000,000 and wants 10 times their money back. The company is expected to be worth 100,000,000 at exit and the stake will keep 80% of its size after future dilution. What stake is needed today?

Target proceeds2,000,000 × 1020,000,000
Required stake at exit20,000,000 ÷ 100,000,00020%
Stake needed today0.2 ÷ 0.825%
The answer

The investor needs 20% at exit, so they must own 25% today.
