Venture Capital Method (Required Ownership)

Intermediate

Also known as: VC method, target return ownership, venture capital return multiple

Stake needed to hit a target venture return. Use it to find the stake a venture investor needs today to reach a target return, allowing for dilution from future funding rounds.

The formula

Required stake at exit=I×MVexit,Stake today=Required stakeRetention\text{Required stake at exit}=\frac{I\times M}{V_{exit}},\quad \text{Stake today}=\frac{\text{Required stake}}{\text{Retention}}

Required stake at exit = Investment x Target multiple / Exit value; Stake needed today = Required stake / Retention

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
IInvestment made todaymoney
MTarget return multiple (10 means the investor wants 10 times their money back)number
V_exitExpected value of the company at exitmoney
RetentionShare of the investor's stake that remains after later funding rounds dilute itpercent

Worked example

An investor puts in 2,000,000 and wants 10 times their money back. The company is expected to be worth 100,000,000 at exit and the stake will keep 80% of its size after future dilution. What stake is needed today?

  1. Target proceeds = investment x multiple2,000,000 x 10= 20,000,000.00
  2. Required stake at exit = target proceeds / exit value20,000,000 / 100,000,000= 20.00%
  3. Stake needed today = required stake / retention0.2 / 0.8= 25.00%

The investor needs 20.00% at exit, so they must own 25.00% today.
