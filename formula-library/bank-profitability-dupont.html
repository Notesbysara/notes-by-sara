Bank Profitability: Income Statement, ROA and ROE

Beginner

Also known as: how commercial banks make money, efficiency ratio, net interest income, DuPont for banks

A bank's profit, efficiency and return on equity. Use it to read a bank's results. Banks earn mostly from the spread between lending and funding rates, plus fees. Because assets are far larger than equity, a small ROA becomes a decent ROE, but the same leverage multiplies losses. Compare the efficiency ratio across banks to see who runs more cheaply.

The formula

NII=II−IE,Efficiency=Non-interest expenseNII+Fees,ROE=ROA×AENII=II-IE,\quad \text{Efficiency}=\frac{\text{Non-interest expense}}{NII+\text{Fees}},\quad ROE=ROA\times\frac{A}{E}

Net interest income = interest income - interest expense; Revenue = NII + fee income; Net income = (revenue - non-interest expense - loan loss provisions) x (1 - tax rate); Efficiency ratio = non-interest expense / revenue (lower is better); ROE = ROA x (assets / equity)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
NIINet interest income: what the bank earns on loans less what it pays on deposits and borrowingsmoney
ProvisionsCharge for expected loan lossesmoney
A/EAssets divided by equity: the leverage that magnifies ROA into ROEnumber

Worked example

A bank has interest income 520, interest expense 220, fee income 130, non-interest expense 300, provisions 60, tax rate 25%, average assets 8,000 and average equity 700.

  1. Net interest income520-220= 300.00
  2. Revenue = NII + fees300+130= 430.00
  3. Pretax profit = revenue - costs - provisions430-300-60= 70.00
  4. Net income70 x (1-0.25)= 52.50
  5. Efficiency ratio = costs / revenue300 / 430= 69.8%
  6. ROA = net income / assets52.5 / 8000= 0.66%
  7. Leverage = assets / equity8000 / 700= 11.4
  8. ROE = net income / equity52.5 / 700= 7.5%

Net interest income is 300.00 and revenue 430.00. After costs and provisions pretax profit is 70.00 and net income 52.50. The efficiency ratio is 69.8%, ROA 0.66% and ROE 7.5%, which is ROA times a leverage of 11.4.
