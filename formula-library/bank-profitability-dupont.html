Bank Profitability: Income Statement, ROA and ROE

BeginnerBanking & Financial InstitutionsAlso known as: how commercial banks make money, efficiency ratio, net interest income, DuPont for banks
← Bank Balance Sheet RatiosBank Run: Liquidity Shortfall and Fire-Sale Loss →

Finds: A bank's profit, efficiency and return on equity.

When to use it: Use it to read a bank's results. Banks earn mostly from the spread between lending and funding rates, plus fees. Because assets are far larger than equity, a small ROA becomes a decent ROE, but the same leverage multiplies losses. Compare the efficiency ratio across banks to see who runs more cheaply.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

NII\displaystyle NII=II−IE\displaystyle =II-IEEfficiency\displaystyle \text{Efficiency}=Non-interest expenseNII+Fees\displaystyle =\frac{\text{Non-interest expense}}{NII+\text{Fees}}ROE\displaystyle ROE=ROA×AE\displaystyle =ROA\times\frac{A}{E}

In words: Net interest income = interest income − interest expense; Revenue = NII + fee income; Net income = (revenue − non-interest expense − loan loss provisions) x (1 − tax rate); Efficiency ratio = non-interest expense / revenue (lower is better); ROE = ROA x (assets / equity)

What each symbol means

NIINet interest income: what the bank earns on loans less what it pays on deposits and borrowingsmoney
ProvisionsCharge for expected loan lossesmoney
A/EAssets divided by equity: the leverage that magnifies ROA into ROEnumber

Worked example

A bank has interest income 520, interest expense 220, fee income 130, non-interest expense 300, provisions 60, tax rate 25%, average assets 8,000 and average equity 700.

Net interest income520 − 220300
Revenue300 + 130430
Pretax profit430 − 300 − 6070
Net income70 × (1 − 0.25)52.50
Efficiency ratio300 ÷ 43069.8%
ROA52.5 ÷ 8,0000.66%
Leverage8,000 ÷ 70011.4
ROE52.5 ÷ 7007.5%
The answer

Net interest income is 300 and revenue 430. After costs and provisions pretax profit is 70 and net income 52.50. The efficiency ratio is 69.8%, ROA 0.66% and ROE 7.5%, which is ROA times a leverage of 11.4.
