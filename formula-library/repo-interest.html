Repo: Interest, Haircut and Repurchase Price

IntermediateFixed IncomeAlso known as: repurchase agreement, repo rate, haircut, collateral, reverse repo
← Replicating Portfolio (One-Step Binomial)Repricing Gap and Net Interest Income →

Finds: the cash, interest and repayment on a repo.

When to use it: Use it to price a repo, which is a short-term secured loan. One side sells securities and agrees to buy them back later at a higher price. The difference is the interest. The haircut protects the lender if the collateral falls in value.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Cash\displaystyle \text{Cash}=V(1−h)\displaystyle =V(1-h)Interest\displaystyle \text{Interest}=Cash×r×d360\displaystyle =\text{Cash}\times r\times\frac{d}{360}Repurchase price\displaystyle \text{Repurchase price}=Cash+Interest\displaystyle =\text{Cash}+\text{Interest}

In words: Cash lent = Collateral value x (1 − Haircut); Interest = Cash x Repo rate x Days / 360; Repurchase price = Cash + Interest

What each symbol means

VMarket value of the collateral, such as bondsmoney
hHaircut, the safety margin between the collateral value and the cash lentpercent
rRepo rate, the annual interest rate on the loanper year
dDays until the bonds are bought backdays

Worked example

A dealer sells bonds worth 10,000,000 with a 2% haircut under a 7-day repo at 5.3%. How much cash does the dealer receive and what is the repurchase price?

Cash lent10,000,000 × (1 − 0.02)9,800,000
Interest9,800,000 × 0.053 × 7 ÷ 36010,099.44
Repurchase price9,800,000 + 10,099.49,810,099.44
Extra collateral held10,000,000 − 9,800,000200,000
The answer

The dealer receives 9,800,000 and repays 9,810,099.44 after 7 days. The interest is 10,099.44. The haircut means the lender holds 200,000 more collateral than the cash it lent.
