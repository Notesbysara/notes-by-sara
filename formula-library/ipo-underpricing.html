IPO Underpricing (Initial Return)

Beginner

Also known as: IPO first day return, money left on the table, initial return

First-day gain to IPO buyers. Use it to measure how much value the issuing company left with first-day buyers. A large first-day jump means the company could have raised more by pricing higher.

The formula

Underpricing=Pclose−PofferPoffer\text{Underpricing}=\frac{P_{close}-P_{offer}}{P_{offer}}

Underpricing = (First-day closing price - Offer price) / Offer price

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
P_offerPrice at which shares were sold in the IPOmoney
P_closePrice at the end of the first trading daymoney
SharesNumber of shares soldshares

Worked example

A company sells 10,000,000 shares at 20 each in an IPO. The shares close at 26 on the first day. What is the underpricing, and how much money was left on the table?

  1. Underpricing = (close - offer) / offer(26-20) / 20= 30.00%
  2. Money left on the table = (close - offer) x shares(26-20) x 10,000,000= 60,000,000.00

The first-day return was 30.00%. The company left 60,000,000.00 on the table.
