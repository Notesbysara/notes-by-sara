Venture Capital Dilution and Required Exit Value

IntermediateAlternative InvestmentsAlso known as: VC economics, pre-money post-money, dilution across funding rounds, venture return multiple
← Vasicek Model Zero-Coupon Bond PriceVenture Capital Method (Required Ownership) →

Finds: how much a venture stake shrinks and the exit it needs.

When to use it: Use it to judge what a venture investment must return. Early stakes shrink with each round and employee option pool, so a 20% stake can end at 10% or less. Because most startups fail, the winners must return many times the fund, so the exit value needed is large. Work it backwards from the multiple you need.

The formula

Stake\displaystyle \text{Stake}=IVpre+I\displaystyle =\frac{I}{V_{pre}+I}sexit\displaystyle s_{exit}=s0∏(1−dk)\displaystyle =s_{0}\prod(1-d_{k})Vreq\displaystyle V_{req}=M×Isexit\displaystyle =\frac{M\times I}{s_{exit}}

In words: Post-money value = pre-money value + investment; Ownership bought = investment / post-money; Each later round dilutes existing holders by (new investment / new post-money value); Stake at exit = initial stake x product of (1 − dilution) x (1 − option pool share); Exit value needed for a multiple M = M x investment / stake at exit

What each symbol means

V_prePre-money valuation: company value before the new moneymoney
IAmount invested in the roundmoney
d_kDilution in later round kpercent

Worked example

A seed round invests 2m at an 8m pre-money value. Later rounds are 10m at 30m pre, then 30m at 100m pre, and an option pool takes 10%. The investor wants 10 times the 2m.

Stake bought at seed2 ÷ (8 + 2)20%
Series A dilution10 ÷ (30 + 10)25%
Series B dilution30 ÷ (100 + 30)23.1%
Stake at exit0.2 × (1 − 0.25) × (1 − 0.230769) × (1 − 0.1)10.38%
Proceeds at the test exit value0.103846 × 50051.92
Multiple of money at that exit51.9231 ÷ 225.96
Exit value needed for the target multiple10 × 2 ÷ 0.103846192.59
The answer

The seed round buys 20% of the company. After the later rounds and the option pool the stake is 10.38%. A 500m exit would pay 51.92, which is 25.96 times the 2m invested. To earn 10 times the investment the exit value must be at least 192.59.
