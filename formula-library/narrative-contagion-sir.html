Narrative Contagion (SIR Model)

Intermediate

Also known as: narrative economics, Shiller epidemic of ideas, R0 of a story, spread of a rumor

Whether a narrative takes off and how far it spreads. Use it to think about how popular narratives, such as a boom story or a panic story, spread like an epidemic. The same mathematics as disease models gives the threshold for a story to take off and how many people it eventually reaches. Shiller argues such narratives drive markets. The parameters are not directly measured, so the model gives intuition about thresholds, not forecasts.

The formula

R0=βγ,imax=1−1+ln⁡R0R0,z=1−e−R0zR_{0}=\frac{\beta}{\gamma},\quad i_{max}=1-\frac{1+\ln R_{0}}{R_{0}},\quad z=1-e^{-R_{0}z}

Basic reproduction number R0 = contagion rate / forgetting (recovery) rate. If R0 > 1 the story spreads. Peak share actively spreading = 1 - (1 + ln R0) / R0; Share who ever hear and believe it, z, solves z = 1 - e^(-R0 x z). A story dies out if R0 is 1 or less

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
betaRate at which each active believer passes the story on, per weekper week
gammaRate at which people lose interest and stop spreading it, per weekper week
R0Average number of new believers per active believer at the startnumber

Worked example

A story spreads at 0.45 per week among active believers, and interest fades at 0.30 per week.

  1. R0 = contagion / fading0.45 / 0.3= 1.500
  2. Peak share actively spreading = 1 - (1 + ln R0) / R01-(1+ln(1.5)) / 1.5= 6.30%
  3. Newton step 1 for the final share (start at 0.5)0.5-(0.5-1+exp(-1.5 x 0.5)) / (1-1.5 x exp(-1.5 x 0.5))= 0.59481
  4. Newton step 20.594814-(0.594814-1+exp(-1.5 x 0.594814)) / (1-1.5 x exp(-1.5 x 0.594814))= 0.582985
  5. Newton step 30.582985-(0.582985-1+exp(-1.5 x 0.582985)) / (1-1.5 x exp(-1.5 x 0.582985))= 0.582812
  6. Newton step 40.582812-(0.582812-1+exp(-1.5 x 0.582812)) / (1-1.5 x exp(-1.5 x 0.582812))= 0.582812
  7. Final share who believe the story0.582812= 58.3%
  8. Share who never hear it1-0.582812= 41.7%

R0 is 1.500, above 1, so the story spreads. At its peak 6.30% of people are actively spreading it, and in the end 58.3% of the population will have heard and believed it. The share who never hear it is 41.7%.
