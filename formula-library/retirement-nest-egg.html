Retirement Nest Egg and Required Saving

Intermediate

Also known as: retirement accounts, pension target, retirement savings goal, how much to save for retirement

The nest egg you need and what to save each year. Use it to turn a retirement income goal into a savings target. Income rises with inflation in retirement, and the account is assumed to run to zero at the end of the planning age. It uses end-of-year amounts and a fixed return, so test lower returns and a longer life. State or workplace pensions and other income would reduce the need, so subtract them from the goal.

The formula

W=INr−π[1−(1+π1+r)L],C=[W−S(1+g)N]g(1+g)N−1W=\frac{I_{N}}{r-\pi}\left[1-\left(\frac{1+\pi}{1+r}\right)^{L}\right],\quad C=\frac{\left[W-S(1+g)^{N}\right]g}{(1+g)^{N}-1}

Nest egg needed at retirement = first-year income / (r - inflation) x [1 - ((1 + inflation) / (1 + r))^L]; Yearly saving needed = (nest egg - current savings x (1 + g)^N) x g / ((1 + g)^N - 1)

Worked example

You are 35, plan to retire at 65 and plan to 90. You want 40,000 a year in today's money, inflation is 2.5%, the account earns 5% in retirement and 6.5% before. You have 30,000 saved.

  1. Years until retirement65-35= 30
  2. Years in retirement90-65= 25
  3. First-year income in future money = d x (1 + inflation)^n40000 x (1+0.025)^30= 83,902.70
  4. Nest egg needed83902.7 / (0.05-0.025) x (1-((1+0.025) / (1+0.05))^25)= 1,518,723.40
  5. Current savings grown to retirement30000 x (1+0.065)^30= 198,430.98
  6. Yearly saving neededmax((1,518,723-198431) x 0.065 / ((1+0.065)^30-1), 0)= 15,285.61
  7. Per month (approximate)15285.6 / 12= 1,273.80

Your first-year retirement income in future money is 83,902.70. The nest egg needed at 65 is 1,518,723.40. Your current savings will grow to 198,430.98, so you need to save 15,285.61 a year (1,273.80 a month) until then.
