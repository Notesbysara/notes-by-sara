Retirement Nest Egg and Required Saving

IntermediatePersonal FinanceAlso known as: retirement accounts, pension target, retirement savings goal, how much to save for retirement
← Retained Earnings Roll-ForwardReturn on a Margin Purchase →

Finds: the nest egg you need and what to save each year.

When to use it: Use it to turn a retirement income goal into a savings target. Income rises with inflation in retirement, and the account is assumed to run to zero at the end of the planning age. It uses end-of-year amounts and a fixed return, so test lower returns and a longer life. State or workplace pensions and other income would reduce the need, so subtract them from the goal.

The formula

W\displaystyle W=INr−π[1−(1+π1+r)L]\displaystyle =\frac{I_{N}}{r-\pi}\left[1-\left(\frac{1+\pi}{1+r}\right)^{L}\right]C\displaystyle C=[W−S(1+g)N]g(1+g)N−1\displaystyle =\frac{\left[W-S(1+g)^{N}\right]g}{(1+g)^{N}-1}

In words: Nest egg needed at retirement = first-year income / (r − inflation) x [1 − ((1 + inflation) / (1 + r))^L]; Yearly saving needed = (nest egg − current savings x (1 + g)^N) x g / ((1 + g)^N − 1)

Worked example

You are 35, plan to retire at 65 and plan to 90. You want 40,000 a year in today's money, inflation is 2.5%, the account earns 5% in retirement and 6.5% before. You have 30,000 saved.

Years until retirement65 − 3530
Years in retirement90 − 6525
First-year income in future money40,000 × (1 + 0.025)^3083,902.70
Nest egg needed83,902.7 ÷ (0.05 − 0.025) × (1 − ((1 + 0.025) ÷ (1 + 0.05))^25)1,518,723.40
Current savings grown to retirement30,000 × (1 + 0.065)^30198,430.98
Yearly saving neededmax((1,518,723 − 198,431) × 0.065 ÷ ((1 + 0.065)^30 − 1), 0)15,285.61
Per month (approximate)15,285.6 ÷ 121,273.80
The answer

Your first-year retirement income in future money is 83,902.70. The nest egg needed at 65 is 1,518,723.40. Your current savings will grow to 198,430.98, so you need to save 15,285.61 a year (1,273.80 a month) until then.
