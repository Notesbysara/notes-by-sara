Geometric Mean Return

IntermediateFinance FoundationsAlso known as: compound average return, time-weighted return, average compound return
← Geometric Brownian Motion: Terminal Price DistributionGood Debt vs Bad Debt Test →

Finds: average compound growth when returns vary.

When to use it: Use it to find the average growth rate of an investment whose returns vary from period to period. It is always at or below the arithmetic mean.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

RG\displaystyle R_{G}=[∏i=1n(1+Ri)]1/n−1\displaystyle = \left[\prod_{i=1}^{n}(1+R_{i})\right]^{1/n}-1

In words: Geometric mean = ((1 + R1) x (1 + R2) x ... x (1 + Rn))^(1/n) − 1

What each symbol means

R_GGeometric mean return, the steady return per period that gives the same final resultpercent
R_iReturn in period ipercent
nNumber of periodsperiods

Worked example

An investment returned 10%, −5% and 15% in three years. What was the geometric mean return?

The answer

The geometric mean return is 6.32%, below the arithmetic mean of 6.67%.
