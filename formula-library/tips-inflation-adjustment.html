Inflation-Linked Bond (TIPS) Cash Flows and Breakeven Inflation

Intermediate

Also known as: TIPS, index linked gilt, real return bond, inflation protected, breakeven inflation

Cash flows of an inflation-linked bond and priced-in inflation. Use it to see what an inflation-linked bond pays. The principal is scaled up by inflation, and the coupon is a fixed rate applied to that higher principal, so both rise with prices. Breakeven inflation shows the inflation rate the market is pricing in.

The formula

Adjusted principal=F×CPItCPI0,Coupon=Adjusted principal×cm,BE=1+ynom1+yreal−1\text{Adjusted principal}=F\times\frac{CPI_{t}}{CPI_{0}},\quad \text{Coupon}=\text{Adjusted principal}\times\frac{c}{m},\quad BE=\frac{1+y_{nom}}{1+y_{real}}-1

Adjusted principal = Face x CPI now / CPI at issue; Coupon = Adjusted principal x real coupon rate / payments per year; Breakeven inflation = (1 + nominal yield) / (1 + real yield) - 1

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
FOriginal face valuemoney
CPI_t, CPI_0Consumer price index now and at issuenumber
cReal coupon rate, fixed at issueper year
y_nom, y_realYields on a nominal bond and an inflation-linked bond of the same maturityper year

Worked example

A 1,000 inflation-linked bond with a 1.5% real coupon paid twice a year was issued when the CPI was 250. The CPI is now 262.5. A nominal bond of the same maturity yields 4.2% while the inflation-linked bond yields 1.8% in real terms. What does it pay and what inflation is priced in?

  1. Inflation since issue = CPI now / CPI at issue - 1262.5 / 250-1= 5.00%
  2. Adjusted principal = face x CPI now / CPI at issue1000 x 262.5 / 250= 1,050.00
  3. Coupon = adjusted principal x c / m1050 x 0.015 / 2= 7.88
  4. Breakeven inflation = (1 + nominal) / (1 + real) - 1(1+0.042) / (1+0.018)-1= 2.36%

Prices are up 5.00% since issue, so the principal is now 1,050.00 and each coupon is 7.88. Breakeven inflation is 2.36%. If inflation averages more than that, the inflation-linked bond does better.
