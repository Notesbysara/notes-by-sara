Interest Rate Swap: Value After Inception

Intermediate

Also known as: mark to market swap, swap valuation, fixed leg and floating leg

What a swap is worth part way through. Use it to find what an existing swap is worth after rates have moved. Just after a floating reset the floating leg is worth its notional, like a par bond. The fixed leg is a fixed-coupon bond. The payer gains if market swap rates have risen above the contract rate.

The formula

Vpayer=N(1−DFn−c∑iDFi)V_{payer}=N\left(1-DF_{n}-c\sum_{i}DF_{i}\right)

Value to the fixed payer (just after a floating reset) = Notional x (1 - Final discount factor - Fixed rate x Sum of discount factors)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
NNotionalmoney
cFixed rate in the swap contractper year
DF_iDiscount factors for the remaining payment dates, from current market ratesnumber
V_payerValue to the party paying fixed and receiving floatingmoney

Worked example

You pay a fixed 3.5% on a 10,000,000 swap with 3 annual payments left. Today's discount factors for those dates are 0.9615, 0.9246 and 0.8890. What is the swap worth to you, and to the other side?

  1. Fixed leg value = notional x (c x sum of DF + last DF)= 9,861,285.00
  2. Floating leg value just after reset = notional10,000,000= 10,000,000.00
  3. Value to the fixed payer = floating - fixed10,000,000-9,861,285= 138,715.00
  4. Value to the fixed receiver-138715= -138,715.00
  5. Current market fixed rate = (1 - last DF) / sum of DF= 4.000%

The fixed leg is worth 9,861,285.00 and the floating leg 10,000,000.00. The swap is worth 138,715.00 to the fixed payer, and -138,715.00 to the receiver. The market rate for the remaining term is 4.000%, above the contract rate, which is why the payer benefits.
