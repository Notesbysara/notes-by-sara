Defined Benefit vs Defined Contribution Pension

Intermediate

Also known as: pension funds, final salary pension, pension pot, replacement ratio

What each pension type delivers and who carries the risk. Use it to compare who bears the risk. In a defined benefit plan the employer promises the income and bears the investment and longevity risk. In a defined contribution plan the worker gets whatever the pot buys, so the worker bears the risk. The comparison shows what contribution would be needed to match a promised pension. Real plans have indexation, tax rules and survivor benefits.

The formula

B=a N Sf,PVDB=B 1−(1+r)−nr,Pot=C (1+g)N−1gB=a\,N\,S_{f},\quad PV_{DB}=B\,\frac{1-(1+r)^{-n}}{r},\quad \text{Pot}=C\,\frac{(1+g)^{N}-1}{g}

Defined benefit pension = accrual rate x years of service x final salary; Its value at retirement = pension x annuity factor, where annuity factor = (1 - (1 + r)^(-n)) / r; Defined contribution pot = yearly contribution x ((1 + g)^N - 1) / g; Income the pot can buy = pot / annuity factor

What each symbol means

NYears of service or savingyears

n, rYears in retirement and the discount rate for the annuityyears, percent

Worked example

A DB plan pays 1.5% of final salary per year of service, with 35 years and a final salary of 70,000. A DC plan receives 7,000 a year for 35 years at a 5% return. Retirement lasts 20 years and annuities are priced at 4%.

  1. DB pension = a x N x final salary0.015 x 35 x 70000= 36,750.00
  2. Annuity factor(1-(1+0.04)^(-20)) / 0.04= 13.590
  3. Value of the DB pension at retirement36750 x 13.5903= 499,444.49
  4. DB replacement ratio36750 / 70000= 52.5%
  5. DC pot at retirement7000 x ((1+0.05)^35-1) / 0.05= 632,242.15
  6. Yearly income the pot buys632242 / 13.5903= 46,521.48
  7. DC replacement ratio46521.5 / 70000= 66.5%
  8. DC contribution needed to match the DB pension499444 x 0.05 / ((1+0.05)^35-1)= 5,529.70

The DB pension is 36,750.00 a year, worth 499,444.49 at retirement, a replacement of 52.5% of final salary. The DC pot grows to 632,242.15 and buys 46,521.48 a year, a replacement of 66.5%. A DC contribution of 5,529.70 a year would be needed to match the DB pension.
