Defined Benefit vs Defined Contribution Pension

IntermediateBanking & Financial InstitutionsAlso known as: pension funds, final salary pension, pension pot, replacement ratio
← DeFi Automated Market Maker: Constant-Product SwapDegree of Combined Leverage (DCL) →

Finds: what each pension type delivers and who carries the risk.

When to use it: Use it to compare who bears the risk. In a defined benefit plan the employer promises the income and bears the investment and longevity risk. In a defined contribution plan the worker gets whatever the pot buys, so the worker bears the risk. The comparison shows what contribution would be needed to match a promised pension. Real plans have indexation, tax rules and survivor benefits.

The formula

B\displaystyle B=a N Sf\displaystyle =a\,N\,S_{f}PVDB\displaystyle PV_{DB}=B 1−(1+r)−nr\displaystyle =B\,\frac{1-(1+r)^{-n}}{r}Pot\displaystyle \text{Pot}=C (1+g)N−1g\displaystyle =C\,\frac{(1+g)^{N}-1}{g}

In words: Defined benefit pension = accrual rate x years of service x final salary; Its value at retirement = pension x annuity factor, where annuity factor = (1 − (1 + r)^(-n)) / r; Defined contribution pot = yearly contribution x ((1 + g)^N − 1) / g; Income the pot can buy = pot / annuity factor

What each symbol means

aAccrual rate: share of final salary earned per year of servicepercent
NYears of service or savingyears
S_fFinal salarymoney
C, gYearly contribution and its investment returnmoney, percent
n, rYears in retirement and the discount rate for the annuityyears, percent

Worked example

A DB plan pays 1.5% of final salary per year of service, with 35 years and a final salary of 70,000. A DC plan receives 7,000 a year for 35 years at a 5% return. Retirement lasts 20 years and annuities are priced at 4%.

DB pension0.015 × 35 × 70,00036,750
Annuity factor(1 − (1 + 0.04)^(−20)) ÷ 0.0413.590
Value of the DB pension at retirement36,750 × 13.5903499,444.49
DB replacement ratio36,750 ÷ 70,00052.5%
DC pot at retirement7,000 × ((1 + 0.05)^35 − 1) ÷ 0.05632,242.15
Yearly income the pot buys632,242 ÷ 13.590346,521.48
DC replacement ratio46,521.5 ÷ 70,00066.5%
DC contribution needed to match the DB pension499,444 × 0.05 ÷ ((1 + 0.05)^35 − 1)5,529.70
The answer

The DB pension is 36,750 a year, worth 499,444.49 at retirement, a replacement of 52.5% of final salary. The DC pot grows to 632,242.15 and buys 46,521.48 a year, a replacement of 66.5%. A DC contribution of 5,529.70 a year would be needed to match the DB pension.
