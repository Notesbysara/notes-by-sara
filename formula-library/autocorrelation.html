Lag-1 Autocorrelation

Intermediate

Also known as: serial correlation, time series correlation, persistence, momentum check

Whether a series carries on or reverses. Use it to see whether a series tends to continue in the same direction (positive), or reverse (negative), from one period to the next. Values near zero mean the past does little to predict the next move.

The formula

ρ1=∑t=2n(xt−xˉ)(xt−1−xˉ)∑t=1n(xt−xˉ)2\rho_{1}=\frac{\sum_{t=2}^{n}(x_{t}-\bar{x})(x_{t-1}-\bar{x})}{\sum_{t=1}^{n}(x_{t}-\bar{x})^{2}}

Lag-1 autocorrelation = sum of (xt - mean)(x(t-1) - mean) / sum of (xt - mean)^2

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
rho_1Correlation between a value and the one just before itnumber
x_tValue in period tpercent
x_(t-1)Value in the previous periodpercent
x-barMean of all the valuespercent
nNumber of valuesnumber

Worked example

A series of monthly returns was 2%, -1%, 3%, 1%, -2%, 4%, 1% and -3%. What is the lag-1 autocorrelation?

  1. Lag-1 autocorrelation= -0.362

The lag-1 autocorrelation is -0.362. It is negative, so a strong month tends to be followed by a weaker one. With only 8 values this is a rough guide.
