Economic Value Added (EVA)

Intermediate

Also known as: EVA, economic profit, NOPAT less capital charge

Profit after the full cost of capital. Use it to see whether a business earns more than the cost of the capital it uses. A positive EVA creates value, and a negative EVA destroys it even when accounting profit is positive.

The formula

EVA=NOPAT−WACC×ICEVA=NOPAT-WACC\times IC

EVA = NOPAT - WACC x Invested capital

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
EVAEconomic value added, profit after a charge for all the capital usedmoney
NOPATNet operating profit after tax, EBIT x (1 - T)money
WACCWeighted average cost of capitalper year
ICInvested capital, the money tied up in the businessmoney

Worked example

A company has NOPAT of 600, invested capital of 5,000 and a WACC of 8%. What is its EVA?

  1. Capital charge = WACC x invested capital0.08 x 5000= 400.00
  2. EVA = NOPAT - capital charge600-400= 200.00
  3. Return on invested capital = NOPAT / IC600 / 5000= 12.00%

The capital charge is 400.00, so the EVA is 200.00. The company earns a return on capital of 12.00%, above its WACC of 8%.
