Stablecoin Reserve Backing and Issuer Income

Intermediate

Also known as: stablecoins, reserve ratio, collateralization, T-bill income, de-peg buffer

A stablecoin's backing, income and run risk. Use it to judge how safe a fiat-backed stablecoin is and how the issuer makes money. Full backing in cash and short bills should let every holder redeem at par, but a run on redemptions can exceed the cash on hand. The issuer earns interest on reserves, which is why the business is profitable when rates are high.

The formula

CR=RL,Buffer=R−L,Income=Rtb×yCR=\frac{R}{L},\quad \text{Buffer}=R-L,\quad \text{Income}=R_{tb}\times y

Backing ratio = reserve assets / coins in circulation; Buffer = reserves - coins in circulation; Issuer income per year = government bills held x yield (holders usually receive none); Run loss = coins redeemed in a run that cannot be paid from cash = max(0, redemptions - cash)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
RReserve assets (cash and short government bills)money
LCoins in circulation, each redeemable for 1money
yYield on the billsper year

Worked example

An issuer has 30bn in cash and 70bn in Treasury bills against 98bn of coins. Bills yield 4.3%. In a run, holders try to redeem 20% of coins at once.

  1. Backing ratio(30,000,000,000+70,000,000,000) / 98,000,000,000= 102.04%
  2. Buffer above coins issued30,000,000,000+70,000,000,000-98,000,000,000= 2,000,000,000.00
  3. Income from bills per year70,000,000,000 x 0.043= 3,010,000,000.00
  4. Redemptions in the run0.2 x 98,000,000,000= 19,600,000,000.00
  5. Cash enough for the run? (1 = yes)1 if 30,000,000,000>=19,600,000,000 else 0= 1
  6. Share of the run covered by cashmin(30,000,000,000 / 19,600,000,000, 1)= 100.0%

The backing ratio is 102.04% and the buffer is 2,000,000,000.00. Annual income from bills is 3,010,000,000.00. A run of 20% means 19,600,000,000.00 to be paid out, which is 1 by cash on hand (1 = cash is enough, 0 = bills would have to be sold quickly). Cash covers 100.0% of that run.
