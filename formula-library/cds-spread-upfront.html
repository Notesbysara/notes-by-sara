Credit Default Swap: Spread, Upfront and Payout

Intermediate

Also known as: CDS, credit triangle, CDS premium, protection buyer, risky annuity

A fair CDS spread, the upfront and the payout. Use it to price protection against default. The buyer pays a yearly premium and receives the notional less the recovery if the borrower defaults. Contracts have a standard coupon, so the difference from the fair spread is settled with an upfront payment.

The formula

s≈h (1−R),Upfront=(s−c)×RPV01×N,Payout=N(1−R)s\approx h\,(1-R),\quad \text{Upfront}=(s-c)\times RPV01\times N,\quad \text{Payout}=N(1-R)

CDS spread = Hazard rate (annual default probability) x (1 - Recovery); Upfront = (Spread - Standard coupon) x Risky annuity x Notional; Payout on default = Notional x (1 - Recovery)

What each symbol means

hHazard rate, the annual probability of defaultper year
RRecovery ratepercent
sFair CDS spread, the yearly premium that makes the swap worth zeroper year
cStandard coupon fixed by the contract, often 1% or 5%per year
RPV01Risky annuity: the present value of 1 paid each year while the borrower survivesnumber
NNotional protectedmoney

Worked example

A borrower has an annual default probability of 2.5% and a recovery rate of 40%. The risk-free rate is 3% and the CDS runs 5 years with a standard coupon of 1% on 10,000,000. What are the fair spread, the upfront payment and the payout if default occurs?

  1. Fair spread = h x (1 - R)0.025 x (1-0.4)= 1.500%
  2. Spread in basis points0.015 x 10000= 150
  3. Survival-and-discount factor per year = e^-(rf + h)exp(-(0.03+0.025))= 0.94649
  4. Risky annuity = q x (1 - q^T) / (1 - q)0.946485 x (1-0.946485^5) / (1-0.946485)= 4.2523
  5. Upfront paid by the buyer = (spread - coupon) x annuity x notional(0.015-0.01) x 4.2523 x 10,000,000= 212,615.20
  6. Payout on default = notional x (1 - R)10,000,000 x (1-0.4)= 6,000,000.00

The fair spread is 1.500%, or 150 basis points. The risky annuity is 4.2523, so the buyer pays an upfront of 212,615.20 on top of the 1% coupon. If default occurs, protection pays 6,000,000.00.
