Sum-of-the-Years'-Digits Depreciation

Intermediate

Also known as: SYD, accelerated depreciation, syd depreciation

Accelerated depreciation in a chosen year. Use it when you want more depreciation early in an asset's life, but less sharply than double-declining balance. The yearly amounts fall in equal steps.

The formula

Dept=(Cost−Salvage)×L−t+1L(L+1)/2Dep_{t}=(\text{Cost}-\text{Salvage})\times\frac{L-t+1}{L(L+1)/2}

Depreciation in year t = (Cost - Salvage) x (L - t + 1) / (L x (L + 1) / 2)

What each symbol means

SymbolMeaningUnit
Dep_tDepreciation in year tmoney
CostWhat the asset costmoney
SalvageExpected value at the end of its lifemoney
LUseful life in yearsyears
tThe year you are calculatingyear number

Worked example

A machine costs 60,000, has a salvage value of 6,000 and a useful life of 5 years. What is the depreciation in year 2?

  1. Depreciable amount = cost - salvage60000-6000= 54,000.00
  2. Sum of the years = L x (L + 1) / 25 x (5+1) / 2= 15
  3. Fraction for the year = (L - t + 1) / sum of the years(5-2+1) / 15= 0.2667
  4. Depreciation = depreciable amount x fraction54000 x 0.266667= 14,400.00

Depreciation in year 2 is 14,400.00. The "sum of the years" is 15.
