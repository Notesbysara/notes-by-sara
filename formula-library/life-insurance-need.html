Life Insurance Needs Analysis

IntermediatePersonal FinanceAlso known as: insurance, income replacement, DIME method, human life value
← Letter of Credit and Discounting: All-In CostLimits to Arbitrage: Holding Cost and Adverse Move →

Finds: how much life cover a family needs.

When to use it: Use it to size life insurance for people who depend on your income. It is a needs method: it asks what the family would need, not a multiple of salary. A negative result means existing assets already cover the need. The discount rate matters a lot, so test a lower one for caution. It ignores taxes and any survivor benefits, so treat it as an estimate.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Need\displaystyle \text{Need}=C 1−(1+g1+r)nr−g+Debts+Goals−Assets\displaystyle =C\,\frac{1-\left(\frac{1+g}{1+r}\right)^{n}}{r-g}+\text{Debts}+\text{Goals}-\text{Assets}

In words: Cover needed = present value of the income to replace (first-year amount, growing at g, discounted at r for n years) + debts to clear + future goals (such as education) − existing assets and cover

What each symbol means

CFirst-year income to replace = income x replacement sharemoney
gYearly growth in that income (inflation)per year
rRate you could earn investing the payoutper year
nYears the family needs supportyears

Worked example

Income is 60,000. The family needs 70% of it for 20 years, growing 3% a year, and the payout could earn 5%. Debts to clear are 180,000, education goals 100,000, and existing assets and cover total 150,000.

First-year income to replace60,000 × 0.742,000
Present value of the support42,000 ÷ (0.05 − 0.03) × (1 − ((1 + 0.03) ÷ (1 + 0.05))^20)670,520.91
Existing assets and cover150,000150,000
Cover neededmax(670,521 + 180,000 + 100,000 − 150,000, 0)800,520.91
Rule of thumb: 10 x income10 × 60,000600,000
The answer

The first-year income to replace is 42,000, with a present value of 670,520.91 over 20 years. Adding debts and goals and subtracting 150,000 of existing assets gives a cover need of 800,520.91. A single-multiple rule of 10 times income would give 600,000.
