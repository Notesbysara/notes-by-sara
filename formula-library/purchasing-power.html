Purchasing Power of Money Over Time

BeginnerFinance FoundationsAlso known as: value of money after inflation, inflation erosion, real value of future money
← PSA Prepayment Speed, CPR and SMMPurchasing Power Parity: Valuation and Expected Rate →

Finds: what a future amount buys in today's prices.

When to use it: Use it to see what a future sum of money will really buy in today's prices once inflation has pushed prices up.

FormulaExampleCalculator

The formula

Real value today\displaystyle \text{Real value today}=Amount(1+i)n\displaystyle = \frac{\text{Amount}}{(1+i)^{n}}

In words: Real value today = Amount / (1 + i)^n

What each symbol means

AmountA sum of money received in the futuremoney
iInflation rate per year, as a decimalper year
nNumber of yearsyears

Worked example

You will receive 1,000 in 10 years. If inflation averages 3% a year, what is it worth in today's money?

Price increase factor(1 + 0.03)^101.3439
Real value today1,000 ÷ 1.34392744.09
Buying power lost1,000 − 744.094255.91
The answer

The 1,000 will buy only what 744.09 buys today, a loss of 255.91 in buying power.
