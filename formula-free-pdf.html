Simple Interest

Interest paid only on the original amount, never on interest already earned.

Future Value
Beginner
What one amount grows to when interest is added each period.
FormulaFV = PV × (1 + r)ⁿ

Rule of 72
Beginner
A quick way to find how many years it takes money to double.
FormulaYears to double = 72 ÷ r
4
Current Ratio
Beginner
Whether current assets cover the bills due within a year.
FormulaCurrent assets ÷ Current liabilities
5
Earnings per Share
Beginner
The profit that belongs to each ordinary share.
FormulaEPS = (Net income − Preferred dividends) ÷ Shares
6
Return on Equity
Beginner
The return earned on shareholders' money.
FormulaROE = (Net income − Preferred dividends) ÷ Average equity

Sharpe Ratio
Beginner
Return for each unit of total risk taken.
Formula(Return − Risk-free rate) ÷ Standard deviation
8
Net Present Value
Intermediate
Whether a project adds value, in today's money. Above zero means it does.
FormulaNPV = Σ CFₜ ÷ (1 + r)ᵗ
9
Loan Payment
Intermediate
The regular payment that pays off a loan over its term.
FormulaPMT = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)⁻ⁿ)

Cost of Equity (CAPM)
Intermediate
The return shareholders require for the share's risk.
FormulaRisk-free rate + Beta × Equity risk premium
