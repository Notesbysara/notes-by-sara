Financial Data-to-Insight

Learn to turn a company’s numbers into a clear answer someone can act on. Twelve short lessons follow one six-step method, then you use it on Walmart’s and Target’s real results.

Who it’s for

  • University students
  • People preparing for finance exams
  • Anyone who wants to understand how finance works

No finance background needed. If Excel or percentages feel shaky, the two toolkits cover what you need first.

By the end you’ll be able to

  • Read the three financial statements and check data before you trust it
  • Ask a sharp question and pick the metrics that matter
  • Work out ratios, variances, break-even and simple forecasts
  • Choose honest charts and write a one-page summary that leads with the answer

The six-step method

  1. 1Assess
  2. 2Inquire
  3. 3Analyze
  4. 4Visualize
  5. 5Interpret
  6. 6Prompt

Every lesson belongs to one step, so you always know where you are in an analysis.
