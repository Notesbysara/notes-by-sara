Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026. Last updated: November 2026.

Notes by Sara ("we", "us" or "the site") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect on notesbysara.org, why we collect it, who handles it on our behalf, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect​

Account details. If you create an account, we collect your email address and a password. Your password is stored in a protected form, so we cannot read it.

Email sign-up. If you request the free Financial Dictionary Quick Reference, we collect the email address you enter. Our email service may also record technical details of the sign-up, such as the date, the time and your IP address, so that we can show you agreed.

Information kept in your browser. The site stores a few small items on your own device, such as the dictionary terms you save and whether you have closed a sign-up prompt. If you are signed in, your browser also holds the details needed to keep you signed in. These items stay on your device and can be cleared in your browser settings at any time.

Technical information. Our website host, GitHub Pages, may keep ordinary technical records of visits, such as your IP address and the pages requested. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on the site.

2. How we use your information​

We use your information to:

  • create and manage your account, and keep you signed in;
  • send you the Quick Reference link and, if you agreed, occasional study updates and news about new notes;
  • keep the site secure and working properly.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. Email updates​

We email you only if you have signed up. When you subscribe, you tick a box to say you agree to receive our emails, and we then send you a welcome email that includes a link to the PDF.

Every email we send contains an unsubscribe link. You can also write to us and we will remove you. When you unsubscribe, we stop sending emails, and our email service keeps a record of this so that you are not emailed again.

4. Who handles your information​

A small number of service providers handle information on our behalf. They may use it only to provide their services to us.

  • MailerLite is our email service. It stores the email addresses of people who sign up and sends our emails. Its own privacy policy describes how it handles data.
  • Supabase provides sign-in and account storage.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Zoho Mail handles email sent to support@notesbysara.org.

5. Cookies and browser storage​

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use only the browser storage needed to keep you signed in and to remember your choices, such as saved terms and closed sign-up prompts. A sign-up prompt you close stays hidden for 30 days.

6. Payments​

If you purchase access to a subject in the future, your payment will be processed directly by Stripe, our payment processor. We do not see or store your card details at any time.

7. How long we keep information​

We keep your account details until you ask us to delete your account. We keep your email address on our mailing list until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it.

8. Your choices and rights​

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any email. You can also ask us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you, including your account, by contacting us using the details below.

9. Security​

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website or email system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy​

We may update this policy as the site grows. Any change will be shown by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

11. Contact us​

If you have questions about this policy or want to use any of your choices above, contact us at support@notesbysara.org.
