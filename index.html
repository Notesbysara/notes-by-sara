Frequently asked questions

What is Notes by Sara?

Notes by Sara is a free, structured set of finance notes. It covers finance subject by subject, from the time value of money and accounting basics to valuation, fixed income and derivatives.

Who are the notes for?

University students, people preparing for finance exams, and anyone who wants to understand how finance works. No finance background is needed to start with Foundations.

How is this different from asking an AI chatbot?

A chatbot answers the question you think to ask. Notes by Sara gives you the full path: what to learn, in what order, explained the same way every time, with worked examples you can check.

Are the notes free?

Yes. Every note on the site is free to read, with no account needed.

What else is on the site?

A Financial Dictionary with plain-English explanations of finance terms, and a Financial Formula & Calculator Library where every formula has a worked example and a calculator that shows its steps.

Is this financial advice?

No. All content is for education only and is not financial, investment or professional advice.

How often are new notes published?

Notes are published subject by subject. Foundations is open now, and the other subjects follow in order.

Can I suggest a topic?

Yes. Email support@notesbysara.org with the concept you would like explained.
