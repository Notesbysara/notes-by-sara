Frequently asked questions

What is Notes by Sara?

A personal, continuously-updated notebook of finance notes, written by Sara as she studies corporate finance, valuation, and markets. It's free to read and open to anyone studying the same subjects.

Is this financial advice?

No. Everything here is for learning purposes only and should not be treated as financial, investment, or professional advice.

How often are new notes added?

There's no fixed schedule. New notes and subjects go up as Sara writes them, alongside her own studying.

Can I suggest a topic?

Yes. Reach out at support@notesbysara.org with anything you'd like to see covered.
