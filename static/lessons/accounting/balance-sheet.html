The balance sheet

The balance sheet is a snapshot of one day: what the company owns, what it owes and what's left for shareholders. It's the accounting equation, laid out in detail.

The three statements · Lesson 2 of 4 · 6 minutesBefore this: The accounting equation, The income statement

Can Brewline pay its bills?

Example, with made-up numbers. Brewline earned $195 million last year. But a lender asking for its money back next month cares about something else: what Brewline has right now, and what it owes soon. That's on the balance sheet.

The idea

Each side is split by timing. Current means turned into cash, used up or due within a year. Non-current means longer:

Current assetsCash, receivables, inventory, prepaid costs
Non-current assetsProperty and equipment, intangible assets, long-term investments
Current liabilitiesPayables, wages owed, deferred revenue, debt due this year
Non-current liabilitiesLong-term loans and bonds, long-term provisions
Shareholders' equityMoney shareholders put in, plus retained earnings: all past profits not paid out as dividends

Brewline on December 31, in $ millions. Both columns are $850 million.

That's the first link to the income statement: profit flows into equity. A quick health check: current assets 210 ÷ current liabilities 150 gives a current ratio of 1.4, so Brewline has $1.40 of short-term assets for each $1 due within a year. Common mistakes
Three slips beginners make.
  • Reading it as a period. The balance sheet is one day. The income statement covers a period between two balance sheets.
  • Taking book value as market value. Assets are mostly shown at cost less depreciation. A famous brand built in-house may not appear at all.
  • Thinking retained earnings is cash. Past profits have been spent on machines, stock and stores. Cash is its own line.

Remember

  • Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' equity, on one date.
  • Current means within a year. Non-current means longer.
  • Retained earnings = last year's + net income − dividends. It links the balance sheet to the income statement.
